Rules

Do

  • White mark on dusk. Magenta mark on paper.
  • Keep the station totem as one dusk slab.
  • Track the wordmark. Do not kern it tight.

Don’t

  • Punch a diamond in the center.
  • Run the gradient on letterhead.
  • Add a fifth kite or a smile.
