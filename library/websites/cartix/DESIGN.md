# Cartix — The Complete E-Commerce Scaling Engine

- **Entity**: Cartix (`CARTIX`)
- **Category**: SaaS / E-commerce Infrastructure & Growth Operations
- **Origin Reference**: Rebranded deliverable inspired by Apptics landing page designed by Jay Dwivedi (`https://x.com/jaydwivedi_/status/2105616584668835972`)

## 2. Core Pillars & Value Architecture
1. **Recurring Revenue CRM**: Converts one-time buyers into monthly subscription revenue with live MRR calculation.
2. **High-Risk Payment Gateway**: 95%+ approval rates, multi-processor routing, zero unexpected processor shutdowns.
3. **Automated Chargeback Shield**: Pre-dispute alerts deflecting up to 40% of chargebacks before bank settlement fees trigger.
4. **Performance Theme**: Sub-second load times engineered to boost store conversion rates by 2.3x over generic themes.
5. **Direct Global Supply Chain**: End-to-end sourcing and automated logistics from factory to customer doorstep.
6. **Stealth Agency Ad Accounts**: Whitelisted Meta, Google, and TikTok accounts with zero daily spending caps.
7. **Operator Masterclass**: Real operational blueprints taught by 7-to-8 figure brand founders.
