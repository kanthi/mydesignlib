# Cartix Design System (Apptics Rebrand)

## 1. Brand Identity & Visual Aesthetic
- **Fictional Brand**: **Cartix** (`cartix.io`)
- **Origin Reference**: Rebranded deliverable inspired by Apptics landing page designed and developed by Jay Dwivedi (`https://x.com/jaydwivedi_/status/2105616584668835972`).
- **Core Aesthetic**: High-end minimalist white SaaS canvas (`#FFFFFF` with `#F8FAFC`), crisp subtle hairline borders (`#E5E7EB` / `#F1F5F9`), soft ambient depth shadows, and rich iridescent 3D glassomorphic modules.
- **Brand Monogram**: Dual-loop infinity ribbon (`cx`) symbolizing recurring revenue, continuous checkout flow, and subscription scaling.

## 2. Color Palette
- **Canvas Base**: Pure White `#FFFFFF`, Subtle Grey `#F9FAFB`, Card Backing `#F8FAFC`.
- **Typography & Hairlines**: Dark Navy Charcoal `#0F172A`, Muted Slate `#64748B`, Subtle Border `#E2E8F0`.
- **Primary Accent**: Royal Blue to Electric Indigo Gradient (`linear-gradient(135deg, #4F46E5 0%, #3B82F6 100%)`).
- **Module Accent Themes**:
  - CRM: Cyan to Ocean Blue (`#0284C7` / `#38BDF8`)
  - Payment: Emerald Green (`#10B981` / `#059669`)
  - Max Creatives: Radiant Violet (`#A855F7` / `#6366F1`)
  - Shield: Warm Coral Amber (`#F97316` / `#EA580C`)
  - Ship: Deep Indigo (`#6366F1` / `#4F46E5`)
  - Stealth: Tactical Bronze Gold (`#F59E0B` / `#D97706`)
  - Learn: Royal Amethyst (`#9333EA` / `#7E22CE`)

## 3. Typography
- **Primary Typeface**: `Plus Jakarta Sans`, -apple-system, sans-serif.
- **Hierarchy**:
  - Main Hero Title: 3.8rem / font-weight 800 / letter-spacing -0.04em
  - Section Headings: 2.2rem / font-weight 800 / letter-spacing -0.035em
  - Eyebrow Badges: 0.75rem / font-weight 600 / uppercase or pill border
  - Body Text: 0.95rem / font-weight 400-500 / line-height 1.6

## 4. Key Sections & Modules
1. **Header**: Clean sticky glass navigation with infinity monogram, link menu, and gradient sign-up pill.
2. **Hero Stage**: "Built For Shopify" pill, "The Complete Ecommerce Engine" headline, and 3D isometric perspective grid with floating white slates and vibrant iridescent squircle glass block.
3. **Cartix CRM**: "Transform One-Time Buyers Into Monthly Recurring Revenue" with 2x2 metric cards and live 3D CRM glass dashboard with Shopify notification popups.
4. **Cartix Payment**: High-risk payment processing with multi-acquirer fallback routing, emerald holographic card, and floating payment network logos.
5. **Cartix Max**: High-converting creative studio showcase with D2C packaging bottle mockup and ROAS scaling metrics.
6. **Cartix Shield**: Chargeback dispute mitigation with frosted glass shield, floating dispute alerts, and churn reduction cards.
7. **Cartix Theme**: Interactive drag comparison slider comparing standard Shopify latency (4.8s) against Cartix headless engine (0.8s).
8. **Cartix Ship**: China fulfillment tracking route with active waypoints and global delivery SLAs.
9. **Cartix Stealth**: Whitelisted agency accounts for unrestricted ad spend scaling.
10. **Cartix Learn**: 7-8 figure operator curriculum visual with 3D isometric stepped knowledge pillars.
11. **Why Choose Cartix**: 3-card benefit pillars for predictable revenue, scaling, and support.
12. **Ecosystem Integrations**: 8-logo dashed grid (Shopify, Woo, WordPress, BigCommerce, Meta, Google, NMI, Stripe).
13. **Frequently Asked Questions**: Accordion with expandable inquiry cards.
14. **Bottom Constellation Hero**: "Build. Sell. Scale. Repeat." with 8-tile floating iridescent squircle app constellation.
15. **Footer**: Brand lockup, Telegram contact badge, links matrix, and oversized ghost watermark monogram.
