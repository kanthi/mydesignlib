Can I use just one Cartix service? +
Yes. You can start with Cartix Payment to unlock high-risk merchant accounts, add Cartix Shield to stop chargebacks, or adopt the complete engine as your scaling volume grows.
How fast can I get started? +
Most Shopify stores integrate within 48 hours. Gateway underwriting and merchant accounts are approved in an average of 3 to 5 business days.
