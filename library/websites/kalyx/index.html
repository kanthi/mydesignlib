Technical Inquiries

How does Kalyx differ from optimistic rollups? +
Unlike optimistic rollups that enforce a 7-day fraud-proof dispute window for withdrawals, Kalyx relies on zero-knowledge cryptographic validity proofs. Every state transition is proven mathematically, allowing instant L1 exits in minutes.
Are existing Solidity contracts fully compatible? +
Yes. Kalyx provides 100% EVM opcode equivalence at the bytecode level. You can use standard tooling including Hardhat, Foundry, ethers.js, viem, and MetaMask without altering contract code.
What are the typical transaction costs on Kalyx? +
Thanks to recursive proof compression and modular data availability, standard token transfers typically cost less than $0.0004, and complex AMM swaps settle for approximately $0.0018.
