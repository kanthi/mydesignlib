# Auraform — Design System & Spec

> Creative Direction & Generative Brand Agency.

---

## 1. Brand Identity & Creative Concept

- **Brand Name**: Auraform
- **Descriptor**: Avant-Garde Creative Direction & Generative Brand Lab
- **Core Visual Idea**: "Illumination out of shadow" — an interactive flashlight / spotlight cursor reveals the vibrant color human portrait hidden beneath an editorial shadow silhouette.
- **Tone**: Bold, editorial, cinematic, kinetic, and confident.

---

## 2. Color Palette & Lighting

- **Halftone Amber / Saffron**: `#f59e0b` / `#fbbf24` (energetic creative canvas)
- **Deep Obsidian Silhouette**: `#0a0a0c` (shadow portrait)
- **Ivory Cream**: `#fffbeb` (contrast highlights)
- **Spotlight Beam**: `radial-gradient(circle 140px at cursor, #ffffff, transparent)`
- **Card Surface**: `#ffffff` and `#111116` with glassmorphic backdrop blur

---

## 3. Typography

- **Hero Wordmark**: 'Syne', 'Plus Jakarta Sans', sans-serif (heavy condensed 800)
- **Editorial Subheads**: 'Plus Jakarta Sans', sans-serif (clean 500/600)
- **Telemetry & Labels**: 'JetBrains Mono', monospace

---

## 4. Key Interactive Components

1. **Dual-Layer Interactive Spotlight Reveal**:
   - Base layer: moody editorial silhouette in shadow.
   - Overlay layer: full color, radiant portrait masked by cursor-following radial gradient with spring physics.
   - Floating flashlight beam with soft ambient halo.
2. **Interactive Beam Controls**:
   - Customizer bar to toggle flashlight colors (Warm Gold, Electric Cyan, Acid Lime, Pure White) and toggle an automated wandering spotlight scan.
3. **Floating Metric Bento Cards**:
   - `95% Growth beyond expectations`, `Refresh your brand`, client satisfaction social proof.
4. **Curated Case Studies Grid**:
   - High-contrast editorial project showcases with hover zoom and tag pills.
5. **Interactive Inquiry Drawer**:
   - "Let's Connect" project onboarding modal.
