# Kairon — Design System & Architectural Blueprint

> **Rebrand Source**: Derived from Octolane (`octolane.com` / Revenue Superintelligence)
> **Identity**: **Kairon** (`kairon`) — *Superintelligence for Human Judgment*
> **Category**: `SaaS` / `Enterprise AI`

---

## 1. Brand Philosophy & Identity

- **The Problem**: Organizations preserve records (deals moved, tickets resolved, emails stored), but the underlying *human judgment*—why one deal was prioritized, why an exception was granted, how a seasoned leader negotiated—is discussed, then lost when employees depart.
- **The Core Thesis**: Superintelligence will not arise purely from larger foundational LLMs, but from systems that systematically accumulate, refine, and deploy human judgment at scale.
- **Product Name**: `Kairon Revenue™` (with core agentic substrate `Kairon OS™`).
- **Tone**: Quiet authority, cerebral precision, high-trust Swiss typography, mathematical restraint. Completely free of generic tech hype, neon gradients, or artificial cheerfulness.

## 3. Typography Hierarchy

- **Display & Titles**: `Newsreader` (Editorial Serif, optical size 16pt/72pt, weights 400 & 400i) — provides the intellectual, timeless gravitas of a classic publication or manifesto.
- **Interface & Body**: `Inter` (weights 400, 500, 600) — ultra-clean, neutral, and readable across all viewport scales.
- **Code & Telemetry**: `JetBrains Mono` / `Geist Mono` (weights 400, 500) — telemetry readouts, status feeds, parameter counters, and cryptographic hash logs.

---

## 4. Key Interactive Components

1. **Generative Gravitational Canvas**:
   - An ambient WebGL / 2D Canvas background rendering subtle gravitational particles that slowly drift, breathe, and subtly respond to viewport scroll and cursor coordinates.
2. **Corner Telemetry Monitors**:
   - Monospace telemetry streams running live in the hero corners (`// kairon agent · live run`, `researched 412 accounts`, `updated 1,068 fields`, `proposed 240 actions`, `approved 218 · rejected 22`).
3. **The Kairon Manifesto Drawer / Scroller**:
   - Expandable philosophical essay exploring how judgment differs from raw information, and why revenue teams provide the ideal continuous-feedback proving ground.
4. **Interactive Workload Shift Timeline**:
   - Interactive monthly slider & distribution visualization (Jun 2025 $\rightarrow$ Jun 2026) demonstrating how human vs. agent workload shifts safely from 99.2% human to 51% agent as judgment signals compound.
5. **Security & Cryptographic Isolation Matrix**:
   - Zero shared-model training, isolated tenant adapters, audit trails, and an interactive compliance badge grid (ISO 27001, SOC 2 Type II, HIPAA, GDPR, CCPA).
6. **Live Account Decision Simulator**:
   - Interactive step-by-step inspector showing an account agent observing account telemetry, formulating an intervention, and requesting human 1-click authorization.
7. **Comprehensive Enterprise FAQ Accordion**:
   - 7 in-depth answers addressing model ownership, point SDR comparison (vs. 11x), horizontal assistant comparison (vs. Claude Cowork/Codex), and team retention.
