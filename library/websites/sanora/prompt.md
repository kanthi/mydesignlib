# Sanora — Website Design & Prompt Reference

## Prompt Concept
A modern, clinical, trustworthy private healthcare clinic and specialized medical center website named "Sanora". Designed with light medical cerulean blues (`#0088FF`), deep navy text (`#0B2545`), soft coral accents (`#FF6B6B`), organic clinical blob graphics, multi-specialty department cards, asymmetrical surgery and patient photo collages, sequential process roadmap, 4-tier telemetry counters, doctor directory grid, and an interactive real-time appointment booking console.

## Visual Characteristics
- **Color Palette**: Clinical Cerulean (`#0088FF`), Deep Navy Slate (`#0B2545`), Soft Ice Blue (`#F0F7FD`), Crisp White (`#FFFFFF`), Coral Pink Accent (`#FF6B6B`), Subtle Gray Border (`rgba(11, 37, 69, 0.08)`).
- **Hero Staging**: Asymmetrical hero layout with bold clinical headline ("Take Best Quality Treatment & Care"), dual CTA pills ("Appointment →" and "Learn more"), and a medical specialist team graphic framed by an organic ice-blue backdrop.
- **Typography**: Plus Jakarta Sans (Headings & Display), Inter (Interface & Clinical Copy), JetBrains Mono (Counters & Telemetry).
- **Distinctive Elements**: Department grid with custom pastel two-tone medical line icons, 3-image surgical and patient consultation collage, 4-step research and care roadmap, prominent 4-pillar patient stat counters, doctor cards with specialization tags and social links, interactive appointment scheduling widget, and recent medical journal articles.
