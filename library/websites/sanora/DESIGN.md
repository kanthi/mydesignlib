# DESIGN — Sanora

## Overview
- **Product**: Sanora — Specialized Medical Center, Private Clinic & Hospital Network.
- **Audience**: Patients, families, and individuals seeking world-class specialized medical care, outpatient surgery, diagnostics, and experienced physicians.
- **Single CTA**: "Book Appointment →"
- **Emotion**: Trustworthy clinical precision, calm reassurance, human warmth, modern medical excellence.
- **Core Narrative**: "Take Best Quality Treatment & Care. Imperial private healthcare combining world-renowned specialists with modern, state-of-the-art facilities."

## Aesthetic Commitment
- **Theme**: Light Modern Clinical Healthcare (Cerulean Cyan, Navy Slate, Coral Pink).
- **Signature Staging**: Clean medical white canvas balanced with organic ice-blue wash backgrounds, coral accent rules, crisp two-tone medical iconography, photography mosaics, and interactive scheduling consoles.

## Color Tokens
- **Canvas Base**: `#FFFFFF` (Pure Clinical White)
- **Canvas Substrate**: `#F4F8FC` / `#EDF5FB` (Soft Ice Blue)
- **Primary Accent / Cerulean**: `#0088FF` (Medical Action & Interactive Links)
- **Accent Dark / Navy Slate**: `#0B2545` (High-Contrast Headings & Form Card)
- **Accent Coral / Coral Red**: `#FF6B6B` (Kicker lines & subtle focal dots)
- **Ink Secondary**: `#4A5E73` (Clinical Body Copy)
- **Ink Muted**: `#8295A8` (Captions & Meta)
- **Border Subtle**: `rgba(11, 37, 69, 0.08)`
- **Border Highlight**: `rgba(0, 136, 255, 0.35)`

## Typography
- **Headings & Display**: `Plus Jakarta Sans`, sans-serif (Weights: 600, 700; Tight tracking: -0.035em)
- **Interface & Prose**: `Inter`, sans-serif (Weights: 400, 500, 600)
- **Counters & Metrics**: `JetBrains Mono`, monospace (KPI numbers: 8900+, 26+, 750+, 150+)

## Section Architecture
1. **Header Navigation**:
   - Sanora medical shield mark + wordmark
   - Nav items: Demos, Departments, Specialization, Doctors, Articles
   - Action: `Get Started Now` pill button
2. **Hero Section**:
   - Coral rule kicker: `— Total Health Care Solution`
   - H1: `Take Best Quality Treatment & Care.`
   - Subtitle: "The art of medicine consists in amusing the patient while nature cures the disease. Comprehensive care, modern diagnostics, and world-renowned specialists."
   - Dual CTAs: `Appointment →` (cerulean pill) + `Learn more` (ghost bordered)
   - Hero doctor photography staging with organic ice-blue backdrop
3. **Departments Section**:
   - Kicker: `— Our Departments` + H2: `Professional care for your health` + `All Departments →`
   - 6 Specialty Cards: Orthopedics, Neurology, Dental Care, Cardiology, Urology, General Surgery
4. **Specialized Care & Hospital Experience**:
   - Asymmetrical 3-photo surgical/consultation collage (`about-senior.jpg`, `about-pediatric.jpg`, `about-team.jpg`)
   - Narrative: `— 15+ Years of Experiences` + H2: `Our hospital providing specialized treatment.`
   - Checkpoint list + `Find Doctor →` button
5. **Care Roadmap (Our Process Step)**:
   - Kicker: `— Our Process Step` + H2: `We apply our latest research`
   - 4-step progressive timeline with doctor circular thumbnails (01 Make Application, 02 Get Check-Ups, 03 Great Consultation, 04 Personalized Treatment)
6. **Key Metrics Banner**:
   - 4 Stats: `8900+` Happy patients, `26+` Departments, `750+` Expert Doctors, `150+` Award Winning
7. **Doctors Directory & Appointment Booking**:
   - 6 Doctor Cards with portraits, names, specialties, and appointment triggers
   - Interactive "Book An Appointment" console with doctor selector, name, phone, date picker, and instant confirmation
8. **Latest News & Medical Articles**:
   - 3 Editorial cards with clinical photography and medical journal digests
9. **Patient Testimonials**:
   - Quotes from verified patients with 5-star ratings and navigation carousel
10. **Comprehensive Clinical Footer**:
    - Emergency hotline, department links, clinical hours (24/7), and legal compliance
