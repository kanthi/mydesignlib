Institutional Architecture Answers

Key details regarding custody compatibility, legal structuring, and network integration.

Stratum implements the open-source ERC-3643 permissioned token standard. Every investor identity is bound to an off-chain verified credential (ONCHAINID). Before any token transfer or secondary trade executes, the smart contract verifies that both sender and receiver satisfy regulatory criteria, sanctions checks, and investor accreditation limits.
Yes. Stratum offers native institutional custody adapters. Asset managers and allocators hold private keys within their existing MPC or HSM hardware vaults. Stratum never holds unilateral custody of client private keys or underlying reserve funds.
Stratum supports settlement in regulated stablecoins (USDC, EURC, PYUSD) as well as direct institutional fiat wires (USD via Fedwire, EUR via SEPA Instant) with automated atomic escrow. We support Ethereum Mainnet, Arbitrum, Base, Avalanche subnet architectures, and permissioned enterprise ledgers.
Fund managers deposit cash yield or Treasury coupon payments into the Stratum Smart Vault. The contract calculates pro-rata allocations based on token holder snapshot blocks and streams dividends directly into authorized investor wallets without manual transfer agent reconciliation.
