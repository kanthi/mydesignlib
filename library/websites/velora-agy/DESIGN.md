# Velora HR — Design System & Layout Architecture

Synthesized using the **L.I.F.T. Layout Framework** and **Anti-Slop SaaS Standard** (2026 Edition).

---

## 1. Brand Strategy & Positioning
* **Brand Name**: Velora HR (Agent provenance: `velora-agy`)
* **Category**: Next-Gen Workforce Operating System & Autonomous People Operations
* **Target Tribe**: High-growth tech scaleups, distributed enterprise workforces, and forward-thinking People & Culture leaders.
* **Core Promise**: Transforming manual administrative drag into high-velocity operational momentum. Full visibility, frictionless shift allocation, and real-time labor telemetry.

---

## 2. Visual Identity & Shape Psychology
* **Master Emblem (Kinetic Wing 'V')**: Dual angular wings converging at an acute base point, symbolizing alignment across diverse departments converging into focused execution.
* **Central Pulse Node**: Represents the living employee at the center of the organization.
* **Color Psychology**:
  - **Deep Wine / Sangria Night (`#260611`)**: Conveys executive authority, high-end production value, and cinematic contrast.
  - **Vivid Coral Red (`#eb4438`)**: Active kinetic energy, urgent clarity, and decisive conversion.
  - **Clean Paper White (`#ffffff`)**: Modern clarity, readability, and spacious calm.

## 5. L.I.F.T. Layout Framework Architecture

* **L (Leverage Point)**: Deep wine hero header with glowing crimson horizon arc, bold $4.2	ext{rem}$ headline (*"Manpower your workforce with an HR system"*), dual pill action triggers, and a floating interactive video player dashboard displaying an executive presenter stream.
* **I (Internal Rhythm)**: 7-Zone spatial progression:
  1. Sticky navigation header with pill conversion CTA & mobile drawer
  2. Deep wine hero anchor with live video stage
  3. Clean monochrome trust wall with fictional enterprise brands
  4. Alternating 2-column feature suite:
     - *Zone 4A: Leave & Absence Simulator* with interactive request dropdown, typology chips, and animated approval
     - *Zone 4B: Shift Scheduling Matrix* with interactive department filter and shift calendar
     - *Zone 4C: Records & Reports Ledger* with real-time hours breakdown and status chips
  5. Deep wine ROI & Results Chamber with three 3D isometric glowing monoliths (`80% HR Department`, `65% Managers' Hours`, `12X Repetitive Work`)
  6. 3-Card HR Control Center architectural bento (`Data`, `Processes`, `People`)
  7. Enterprise security compliance banner (SOC2, GDPR, ISO 27001) & final conversion CTA chamber
* **F (Friction & Flow)**: Generous whitespace, high-contrast light/dark thematic transitions, optical alignment, and reduced motion accessibility.
* **T (Transferability)**: Fluid responsive CSS grid, concentric radii, mobile navigation drawer, and `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` support.
