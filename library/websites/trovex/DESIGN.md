# Trovex — Design System & Spec

> Mobile Quest & Rewards Platform (Derived from Chatto reference, fully rebranded).

---

## 1. Brand Identity & Vision

- **Brand Name**: Trovex
- **Descriptor**: Verified Quest & Rewards Platform
- **Value Proposition**: Turn spare hours into verified digital assets, gift cards, and direct instant payouts.
- **Tone**: Kinetic, tactile, trusted, gamified yet premium fintech-grade polish.

---

## 2. Color Palette & Lighting

- **Background Void**: `#0a0b10` (deep space obsidian)
- **Card Surface**: `#13141f` with 1px border `#23253a` and inner glow
- **Brand Violet**: `#8b5cf6` (Electric Purple primary)
- **Brand Fuchsia**: `#ec4899` (Accent gradient partner)
- **Neon Mint**: `#10b981` (Verification / live payout badge)
- **Text Primary**: `#f8fafc`
- **Text Muted**: `#94a3b8`

---

## 3. Typography

- **Display**: Plus Jakarta Sans, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, sans-serif
- **Telemetry & Numbers**: JetBrains Mono, SF Mono, Menlo, monospace

---

## 4. Key Interactive Components

1. **3D Floating Multi-Phone Hero Stage**:
   - Central hero iPhone 16 Pro mockup featuring interactive Trovex OS interface.
   - Flanking side devices (Quests browser on left, Daily Streaks on right) with subtle 3D perspective depth and blur.
   - Dynamic cursor perspective tilt (`perspective(1200px) rotateX(...) rotateY(...)`).
   - Interactive balance card with "Claim Reward" particle coin explosion.
   - Switchable phone tabs (Trending, Rewards, Streaks, Games).
2. **Live Global Payout Feed**:
   - Ticker streaming simulated real-time verified redemptions with user country badges, amounts, and withdrawal rail (Instant USDC, Steam, Apple, PayPal).
3. **Interactive Rewards Showcase**:
   - Filterable catalog (Gift Cards, Crypto, Gaming, Hardware).
   - Dynamic point-to-dollar redemption calculator.
4. **How It Works 3-Step Bento**:
   - Step 1: Discover verified developer quests.
   - Step 2: Automated zero-delay completion checks.
   - Step 3: Sub-15-minute global payouts.
5. **Security & Trust Proof**:
   - Zero invasive tracking, verified sponsor checks, anti-fraud telemetry.
6. **FAQ Accordion & Conversion CTA Bar**:
   - Smooth expand/collapse, store download badges with QR scan hover.
