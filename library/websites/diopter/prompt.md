# Rebuild Prompt — Diopter

Create a production-grade, highly tactile dark-mode website template for **Diopter**, an independent cybernetic optics, spatial computing interface, and conversion architecture atelier.

## Requirements
1. **Brand Identity**:
   - Brand name: "Diopter".
   - Theme: High-tech obsidian black (`#070709`), warm photonic amber (`#ff601c`), laser cyan (`#00e5ff`), frosted glass card panels, and crisp technical hairline grids.
   - Distinctive typography using `Syne` for display headlines, `Plus Jakarta Sans` for body copy, and `JetBrains Mono` for telemetry HUD readouts.

2. **Hero & 3D Optical Lens Assembly**:
   - Integrated Three.js WebGL canvas displaying a multi-element physical glass lens with bi-convex curvature, titanium knurled aperture bezel, counter-rotating reticle rings, floating dust motes, and caustic light pulses.
   - Smooth mouse/touch drag tilt physics and subtle inertia damping.
   - Floating Conversion Design Card ("Crafted to convert.") with interactive 3-tab switcher (Optics, Haptics, Latency) and animated telemetry progress bar.
   - Live multi-city world clock capsules in the header updating in real time (Tokyo, San Francisco, Zürich).

3. **Sections**:
   - Infinite smooth marquee partner rail (Kaido, North, Vellum, Orbit, Bright, Cobalt, Mesa).
   - 4-card Selected Works Grid with custom SVG technical HUD wireframes, tags, year, client, and metric outcomes.
   - Interactive Optical Lab: live WebGL slider controls for focal curvature, chromatic dispersion (IOR), and spectral polarization themes (Amber, Cyan, Emerald, Obsidian).
   - Telemetry metrics strip with hairline borders and bold numbers.
   - Terminal-style calibration contact form with tier buttons and simulated CLI response.
   - Clean dark-mode footer with coordinates and copyright.

4. **Compliance & Constraints**:
   - Pure vanilla HTML/CSS/JS without npm/build dependencies.
   - No search indexing meta tags (`robots` and `googlebot` noindex).
   - Google Analytics gtag `G-Z97ZD3EVSF` in `<head>`.
