Automating deal prioritization without losing judgment

Leah Ibarra September 4, 2026

Queues are useful until they hide the deal a human would have taken. We score pipeline for coverage, then we force a manager to accept or override — with a reason that sticks to the record.

What the model is allowed to do

It can rank. It can surface stalled stages. It cannot close a commit. Reps still own conversations. Managers still own the call. The machine owns the reminder that a $400k deal has been silent for eleven days.

Write-back

If the action does not land in the CRM, it did not happen. Prioritization that lives only in Kedge is a second system. We refuse to ship that.
