What a CRO actually needs on Monday morning

Helena Cho August 12, 2026

I do not need another export. I need a short list: what moved, who owns it, and the one decision that cannot wait until Thursday’s forecast call.

Three lines

Pipeline that slipped a stage with no activity. A renewal whose health fell through a threshold. A commit someone changed after Friday. If those three are empty, I can drink my coffee. If they are not, I know who to call before standup.

Stop restating last week

Most Monday decks are last week with new colors. Kedge’s Monday view is a delta. That is the only view I open first. Everything else is for the people who have to explain the delta.
