Why most enterprise forecasts fail and how to fix them

Owen Brant September 10, 2026

Forecasts fail in three boring ways: the sources disagree, the humans round in private, and nobody can say who changed the commit. Fancy models do not fix that. Structure does.

Disconnected data

If CRM, billing, and capacity live in three files, the call is a negotiation about whose file is true. Unify the objects first. Then argue about judgment.

Manual updates

A manager who types the number by hand will protect the number. A change log that records every edit — who, when, from which assumption — makes that protection visible. Shame is a better control than another slide.

Siloed teams

Sales, CS, and finance cannot share a forecast they cannot all open. Give them one model with role-specific views, not three “aligned” decks. That is the entire product.
