Introducing Kedge 2.0: real-time revenue visibility across teams

Nia Calder September 18, 2026

Most revenue stacks still assemble the week in a spreadsheet. CRM is a day late. Billing is a week late. Product usage never makes the call. Kedge 2.0 is the release that treats those delays as the product problem they are.

One clock

Every object in the model now shares a clock. A closed-won in CRM, an invoice in billing, and a seat change in product land on the same timeline within minutes — not in Friday’s export. When the number moves, you can point at the event, not a feeling.

What teams actually see

Sales sees pipeline risk with a reason. Finance sees the same commit they heard on the call. CS sees expansion and churn on that same strip. We stopped shipping three “aligned” dashboards. Alignment is a shared clock, or it is theater.

If you already run Kedge, 2.0 is on your instance. If you do not, request a demo and bring last quarter’s miss. We will replay it on the new clock.
