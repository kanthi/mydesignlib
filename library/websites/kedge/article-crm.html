From CRM data to real customer intelligence

Theo Maren August 29, 2026

A CRM knows the commercial story. It does not know that usage fell 30% or that support load doubled. Those signals sit in tools CS already has — and never reach the forecast until the logo is already gone.

Join, don’t duplicate

We join usage, tickets, and commercial stages on the account. Health is a derived field with a recipe you can read, not a mystery score. Expansion alerts fire when usage and stage agree, not when a CSM has a hunch.

Before it hits the quarter

The point is lead time. A health drop six weeks out is a save motion. The same drop in week twelve of the quarter is a footnote. Put the join in the model early, or keep being surprised.
