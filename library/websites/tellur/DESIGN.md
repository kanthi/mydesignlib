# Tellur — Layers Hold Tales of Time

A geological deep-time exploration and cartography platform template inspired by computational geoscience, planetary visualization, and high-craft editorial education.

## Visual Identity & Atmosphere

- **Theme**: Void space black (`#050508`), cosmic purple glow (`#522dff`), starlight lavender (`#9e88ff`), and crisp parchment white (`#ffffff`).
- **Typography**:
  - Hero Emphasis: `Instrument Serif` italic 400 (`Layers hold`) paired with modern sans-serif (`tales of time`).
  - Body & UI: `Inter` variable sans-serif with monospace accents for geological ages (e.g. `66 MA — TODAY`).
- **3D Interactive Centerpiece**:
  - Three.js WebGL rotating Earth globe with:
    - Custom atmospheric Fresnel glow shader in vibrant purple (`#522dff`).
    - Fibonacci-distributed dot cloud defining continental landmass clusters.
    - Subtle latitude/longitude wireframe strata.
    - Two counter-rotating luminous orbital rings with 3D directional arrow cones.
    - Mouse parallax depth tilting.

## Structure & Sections

1. **Navigation**:
   - Left brand mark: Geological stratum diamond chevron + `Tellur`.
   - Center frosted glass capsule menu (`Course`, `Field Guides`, `Geology`, `Plans`, `Live Tour`).
   - Right action pill button (`Sign Up`).
2. **Hero Stage**:
   - Centered editorial headline lockup.
   - Left narrative column detailing deep sedimentary history.
   - Right callout with glowing purple action pill (`Start Digging`).
   - Bottom center seismic audio waveform capsule with pulsing orange LED indicator.
3. **Six Eras Chronostratigraphy Section**:
   - Interactive timeline table spanning Cenozoic, Mesozoic, Paleozoic, Proterozoic, Archean, and Hadean eras with hover glow states and geological timestamps.
4. **Field Guides for Every Ground**:
   - Tri-column cards showcasing Igneous Extrusive, Sedimentary Chemical, and Metamorphic Foliated formations.
