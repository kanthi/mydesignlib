# Telora Systems — Design System Specification

## Brand Identity & Vision
**Telora Systems** (`telora`) is a business-centric observability platform that bridges the gap between deep technical telemetry and real-time business outcomes. Engineered for high-scale digital transaction ecosystems, financial institutions, and mission-critical cloud enterprises, Telora unifies metrics, events, logs, and distributed traces into coherent business transaction journeys.

> *“Business Observability That Connects Every Dot. Eliminate operational blind spots with AI-driven transaction journey intelligence.”*

---

## 2. Typography

- **Primary Interface & Display**: `Plus Jakarta Sans`, `sans-serif` (weight 700 / 800 for authoritative headlines, 500 / 600 for navigation and buttons)
- **Monospace Telemetry & Metrics**: `JetBrains Mono`, `monospace` (scale counters, latency measurements, throughput rates)

---

## 3. Structural Blueprint & Sections

1. **Top Island Navigation**:
   - Telora wordmark with geometric connected-node glyph.
   - Core links: Platform, Product Suite, Solutions, Resources, Company, Pricing.
   - Primary action: "Get A Demo" with kinetic double-arrow icon translation.
2. **Hero Stage**:
   - Technical gridline boundary framing (`lines-container`).
   - Headline: `Business Observability That Connects Every Dot`.
   - Subhead & Dual CTAs.
   - Interactive Live Transaction Flow Stage: Animated SVG flow visualizer linking User Journey $\rightarrow$ Edge API $\rightarrow$ Core Services $\rightarrow$ Database $\rightarrow$ Revenue Outcomes.
3. **Transaction Volume Proof Marquee**:
   - `Delivers Visibility Across 28B+ Monthly Transactions`.
   - Monochrome banking and cloud partner logos.
4. **Connecting IT to Business Impact**:
   - Eyebrow: `Unifying Your Digital Ecosystem`.
   - Interactive flow simulator correlating technical spikes directly to business metrics.
5. **3-Pointer Tabbed Experience (Eliminate Observability Chaos)**:
   - Tab 1: `Complete Business Journey Visibility`
   - Tab 2: `AI-Powered Early Warning System`
   - Tab 3: `Smart Alerts & Integrations`
   - Interactive SVG dashboard visualizer that updates when tabs are clicked.
6. **Scale with Telora (High-Impact Numerals Bar)**:
   - Dark navy container with glowing hover effects:
     - `40TB+` Log data/day
     - `90K+` Nodes observed daily
     - `300+` Pre-built data adaptors
     - `28B+` Transactions observed monthly
     - `$1.5Tr` Monthly transaction volume
7. **Modular Product Suite Showcase**:
   - Interactive switcher for the 4 core pillars:
     1. `teloraInfra` (Multi-vendor infrastructure health)
     2. `teloraApp` (Distributed service tracing & MTTR reduction)
     3. `teloraTxn` (Micro-journey transaction observability)
     4. `teloraLog` (Sub-second log intelligence at petabyte scale)
8. **Executive Testimonial Reel**:
   - Real-world enterprise quotes highlighting incident MTTR reductions and uptime gains.
9. **Deployment Flexibility & Global Enterprise Footer**:
   - On-premise, Private Cloud, and Multi-Cloud deployment architectures.
   - Global footer with navigation links and legal indicators.
