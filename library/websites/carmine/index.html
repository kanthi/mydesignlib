How the desk opens

Carmine sits with the closer and holds the list, the note, and the next sentence until a person is ready. The floor can stay noisy. This room does not.

Learn more

How a thread moves

  1. 01

    Listen

    It stays on the call and keeps only the lines the closer will need again.

  2. 02

    Sort

    Names fall into a shorter list, by who is ready, not by who shouted.

  3. 03

    Draft

    The reply uses the closer’s voice and does not send itself.

  4. 04

    Hand off

    A person receives the thread with the order and the next step already set.

Notes from the floor

Keep the list shorter than the noise around it.

Leave the draft in the closer’s words.

Send nothing until someone on the desk decides.
