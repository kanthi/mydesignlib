Intelligence at every layer

Solveiq isn't just a chatbot. It's a complete routing and resolution engine that lives between your customers and your support agents.

01

Ingest & Analyze

Every incoming request is parsed by our NLP engine to determine intent, sentiment, and required expertise before it ever hits a queue.

02
