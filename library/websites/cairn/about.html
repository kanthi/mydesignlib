Why we exist

Useful work starts
with a better question.

The hard part of a decision is often not the analysis. It is getting the right people to share the same question, surface what is still uncertain, and agree on who will move it forward. Cairn makes that work visible and deliberate.

How we work

A thoughtful process creates shared understanding and keeps the next step small enough to move.

01

02

Compare trade-offs, evidence, and open questions on one shared board.
