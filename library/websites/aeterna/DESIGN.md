# Aeterna — Design System & Spec

> Timeless Celestial Memorial & Heritage Preservation Sanctuary.

---

## 1. Brand Identity & Emotional Concept

- **Brand Name**: Aeterna
- **Descriptor**: Digital Memorials & Heritage Preservation Sanctuary
- **Core Ethos**: "Where cherished lives remain timeless."
- **Emotional Tone**: Serene, sacred, cinematic, architectural, tender, and enduring.
- **Visual Motif**: Baroque neoclassical ivory archway ascending through golden celestial cumulus clouds, warm candle glow, and starlit night sky.

---

## 2. Color Palette & Lighting

- **Celestial Twilight**: `#070b14` (deep evening vault)
- **Sanctuary Ivory / Gold**: `#fdf8ea` (primary text / architectural marble)
- **Sunbeam Warm Gold**: `#d4af37` / `#f59e0b` (accents, candle flames, celestial halos)
- **Mist Azure**: `#38bdf8` (daylight break through cloud arch)
- **Card Obsidian**: `#0e1526` with subtle 1px border `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.2)`

---

## 3. Typography

- **Headings & Epitaphs**: 'Cormorant Garamond', Georgia, serif (high-contrast, regal, tender)
- **UI & Telemetry**: 'Plus Jakarta Sans', -apple-system, sans-serif (legible, calm, respectful)

---

## 4. Key Interactive Experiences

1. **Celestial Archway Hero**:
   - Atmospheric slow-drifting cloud parallax and golden celestial light rays.
   - Dynamic cursor perspective tilt over the celestial portal.
   - Elegant, serene call-to-action to begin an heirloom tribute.
2. **Interactive Candle Lighting Sanctuary**:
   - Visitors can click "Light an Everlasting Candle" to add their warmth to the global memorial wall, accompanied by an authentic soft chime tone and flickering flame animation with live counter.
3. **Curated Life Tributes Gallery**:
   - Profiles of celebrated lives (stories, dates, audio archives, lifetime milestones).
4. **The 4 Pillars of Perpetual Remembrance**:
   - 100-Year Decentralized Preservation, Interactive Life Thread, Audio Voice Archives, Hand-Cast Brass QR Plaques.
5. **Interactive Memorial Creator Studio Drawer**:
   - Live modal letting families configure name, memorial dates, sanctuary realm theme, and preview an instant golden tribute stone card.
