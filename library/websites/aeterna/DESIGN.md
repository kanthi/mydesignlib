# DESIGN — Aeterna (Celestial Memorial Sanctuary)

## Overview
- **Audience**: Families, memorial curators, genealogists, and loved ones seeking a sacred, timeless digital sanctuary to honor departed family members and celebrate cherished heritage.
- **Single CTA**: `Create Memorial →` (secondary: `Explore Tributes` / `How It Works`).
- **Emotion**: Reverent transcendence, celestial peace, timeless golden warmth, eternal serenity.
- **Create this feeling through**:
  - Full-bleed luminous celestial azure sky (`#1a4b8c` to `#629ae0`) filled with golden divine sunlight and billowing sunlit cumulus clouds.
  - Monumental Golden Baroque Triumphal Archway portal floating in the clouds as the central architectural icon.
  - Interactive 3D mouse parallax: dynamic perspective pitch/yaw tilt (`rotateX`, `rotateY`, `translateZ`) and drifting volumetric cloud layers.
  - Scroll-driven camera push-through: as user scrolls, the camera pushes forward into the golden portal with smooth scale expansion (`scale(1.0 -> 1.45)`) and atmospheric fog parting.
  - Interactive eternal candle tribute cards with Web Audio chime synthesis and interactive flame lighting.
  - Clean typography: Pure white geometric grotesque (`Plus Jakarta Sans`) paired with classical serif titles (`Cormorant Garamond`).
