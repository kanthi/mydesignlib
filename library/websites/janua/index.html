Operating Creed

Small teams, high agency, zero theater.

We are building verifiable intelligence. We do not hire for empire building, headcount metrics, or performative busywork. We hire builders who want their names on foundational breakthroughs.

01 / CERTAINTY

Verifiable by Construction

We believe AI systems without mathematical guarantees are ticking liabilities. Every model architecture we design is bound to formal verification proofs and reproducible guarantees.

02 / AGENCY

Uncapped Ownership

Every member is expected to own features from first principles mathematics through compiler runtime to end-user UI. There are no middle-management handoffs or approval committees.

03 / FOCUS

Protected Deep Work

We hold no recurring status meetings. Afternoons are quiet maker blocks. Decisions are written down in long-form memos, debated in public PRs, and executed with intense craft.
