Terms of Service & Master Agreement

Last updated: August 2026. Standard terms governing software development, technical consulting, and managed SRE engagements.

1. Scope of Engagement & SOWs

All software engineering, infrastructure architecture, AI runtime development, and managed SRE operations provided by Trestle AGY are governed by project-specific Statements of Work (SOWs) executed under our Master Services Agreement (MSA).

2. Intellectual Property & Sovereign Code Transfer

Trestle AGY operates on a full IP transfer model. Upon settlement of sprint invoices, 100% of bespoke source code, infrastructure as code (IaC) manifests, automated test suites, and system documentation become the exclusive intellectual property of the client.

3. Warranty & SDLC Verification Gates

We warrant that all software delivered conforms to the documented architecture specifications and passes all 5 automated CI verification gates (>94% test coverage, 0 compiler warnings, mutation testing passed). Engagements include 90 days of embedded hypercare to rectify any non-conforming defects at zero additional cost.

4. Service Level Agreements (SLAs) for Managed SRE

Managed SRE retainers are backed by a guaranteed 99.999% cluster availability target and a sub-15-minute emergency response SLA on Severity-1 operational incidents, monitored 24/7 by our dedicated engineering command center.

5. Mutual Non-Disclosure & Security

Both parties agree to maintain strict confidentiality regarding system architectures, trade secrets, and non-public business data under perpetual mutual non-disclosure obligations.
