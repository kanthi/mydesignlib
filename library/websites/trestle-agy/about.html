An engineering collective built for permanence.

Trestle AGY was founded on a straightforward belief: enterprise software should be built by senior practitioners who understand the physical and mathematical realities of systems under load.

Founding Thesis

The antidote to consulting theatre.

In 2014, our founders departed senior engineering and infrastructure roles at major financial institutions and high-scale distributed systems platforms. They were frustrated by the prevailing model of technology consulting: slick presentations sold by partners, handed off to offshore junior teams, resulting in brittle software that collapses when real traffic hits.

Trestle AGY was engineered as the direct opposite: a senior-only engineering firm with zero junior bait-and-switch. We deploy compact, high-velocity pods of seasoned architects and engineers who write production code, verify it against automated mathematical quality gates, and take operational accountability for the systems they deliver.

Today, our team of over 240 senior engineers across four dedicated physical laboratories designs, builds, and runs digital infrastructure for organizations that cannot tolerate failure.

Regional Infrastructure

Four dedicated engineering laboratories.

Our work is anchored across four specialized physical facilities, each equipped for specific engineering domains.

LAB 01 // EASTWICK

Core Software & Distributed Architecture

Our primary software engineering laboratory. Houses our compiler verification, distributed state machine, and high-concurrency backend teams.

Facilities Continuous integration staging cluster, multi-monitor pairing stations, hardware benchmarking rigs.

LAB 02 // PORT SABLE

Cloud Mesh & Infrastructure Testing

Our dedicated infrastructure facility. Focuses on multi-region Kubernetes orchestration, SDN networking, and automated chaos engineering.

Facilities Direct redundant dark fiber connections, isolated staging server racks, physical hardware load generators.

LAB 03 // MARROW BAY

AI Systems & Agent Runtime Sandbox

Our applied artificial intelligence laboratory. Focuses on domain-specific LLM fine-tuning, deterministic tool execution, and hybrid vector search.

Facilities Dedicated GPU evaluation clusters, automated hallucination benchmarking testbeds, air-gapped data sandboxes.

LAB 04 // OAKHAVEN

24/7 Managed Operations & SRE Incident Desk

Our 24/7/365 production reliability command center. Staffed continuously by senior site reliability engineers monitoring live telemetry.

Facilities Triple-redundant power systems, live kernel eBPF tracing consoles, sub-15-minute emergency dispatch desks.
