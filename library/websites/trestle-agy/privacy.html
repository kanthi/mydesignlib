Privacy Policy & Data Governance

Last updated: August 2026. Trestle AGY enforces zero-trust data protection standards across all client engagements and web surfaces.

1. Principles of Data Minimization

Trestle AGY operates on the strict principle of data minimization. We collect only the information necessary to evaluate technical briefs, execute client contracts, and maintain secure system telemetry.

2. Technical Briefs & Confidentiality

All architectural blueprints, system schemas, RFP disclosures, and proprietary source code transmitted to Trestle AGY are treated as strictly confidential under our mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA). Data submitted via our encrypted contact forms is stored in air-gapped repositories and accessible only to assigned Principal Architects.

3. Client Codebase Sovereign Ownership

Trestle AGY claims no intellectual property rights or telemetry logging rights over client repositories, databases, or proprietary models. All IP engineered under contract is transferred 100% to the client upon milestone settlement.

4. Telemetry & Cookie Policy

This showcase specimen utilizes Google Analytics (gtag.js) strictly for aggregated traffic verification and performance monitoring. We do not sell user data, utilize behavioral tracking advertising networks, or deploy invasive fingerprinting scripts.

5. Contact & Compliance Inquiries

For data privacy or compliance inquiries, contact our Data Governance Officer at governance@trestle-agy.example.
