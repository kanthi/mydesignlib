# Oravita — Dental Clinic & Orthodontics Design System

## 1. Brand Identity & Overview
- **Brand Name**: Oravita Dental Care (`oravita.`)
- **Category**: Dental Clinic / Healthcare / Orthodontics
- **Tagline**: *Your smile deserves the best quality dental care.*
- **Mission**: Providing trusted, pain-free dental care with advanced 3D scanning, gentle micro-treatments, clear aligners, and comprehensive orthodontic restorations.

---

## 2. Typographic Hierarchy
- **Headings & Display**: `Plus Jakarta Sans`, 700 / 800 weight, tight geometric tracking (`-0.03em`).
- **Body & Editorial**: `Inter`, 400 / 500 weight, balanced clinical legibility (`line-height: 1.6`).
- **Technical & Telemetry**: `JetBrains Mono`, 500 weight, uppercase tracking (`+0.06em`).

---

## 3. Color Tokens
- **Primary Clinical Blue**: `#0284C7` (Sky 600)
- **Deep Slate / Ink**: `#0F172A` (Slate 900)
- **Soft Ice Tint**: `#F0F9FF` (Sky 50)
- **Clinical Border**: `#E2E8F0` (Slate 200)
- **Accent Mint / Health**: `#10B981` (Emerald 500)
- **Canvas White**: `#FFFFFF`
- **Text Secondary**: `#64748B` (Slate 500)

---

## 4. Key Sections & Components
1. **Clinical Header**:
   - Navigation (`Home`, `Services`, `Problems`, `Doctors`, `Pricing`, `Reviews`).
   - Emergency hotline indicator (`24/7 On-Call Dentists`).
   - `Book Appointment ↗` quick action pill.
2. **Hero Section (Faithful to Reference)**:
   - Eyebrow tag: `Your Trusted Partner For Dental Health`.
   - Monumental headline: `Your Smile Deserves The Best Quality Dental Care`.
   - Subhead with gentle treatments & advanced technology highlights.
   - Dual action buttons: `Book Appointment ↗` & `Explore Services`.
   - Interactive 3D intraoral scanner display with real-time occlusion metrics.
   - Trust statistics bar (`15k+ Smiles Perfected`, `99.4% Success Rate`, `12+ Board Certified Specialists`).
3. **Common Dental Problems & Solutions Grid**:
   - Cavities & Decay, Crooked / Crowded Teeth, Enamel Staining, Gum Disease, Missing Teeth, TMJ Jaw Pain.
4. **Interactive Treatment Cost & Timeline Estimator**:
   - Select concern (Whitening, Clear Aligners, Implants, Routine Cleaning).
   - Live cost and duration preview with zero-interest financing calculator.
5. **Specialist Doctor Profiles**:
   - Lead Prosthodontist, Orthodontic Director, Pediatric Specialist.
6. **Patient Testimonials & Verified Case Studies**:
   - Before & after aesthetic restorations.
7. **Appointment Booking Modal & Intake Drawer**:
   - Preferred time slot, doctor selection, insurance provider verification.
8. **Clinical Footer**:
   - Operating hours, accepted insurance networks, ADA certification badges.
