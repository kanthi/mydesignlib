# Quire — Curated Editions & Rare Literature

An archival book discovery and rare literature platform inspired by classic letterpress printing, contemporary editorial typography, and interactive 3D spatial book displays.

## Visual Identity & Typography

- **Theme**: Deep bookbinder black (`#07080a`), archival paper ivory (`#f5f4f0`), warm terracotta accent (`#ff4d2e`), and amber gold (`#f4a261`).
- **Typography**:
  - Primary Display & Headings: `Cormorant Garamond` serif with high contrast and graceful italic curves.
  - UI & Metadata: `Inter` variable sans-serif with uppercase tracking for authors and publication dates.

## Key Features & Structure

1. **Massive Centered Hero Logotype**:
   - Ultra-large editorial serif headline `Quire` positioned centrally behind the 3D book rack.
2. **3D Interactive Book Rack Carousel**:
   - 7 unique typographic book jacket designs standing in 3D perspective space with spine creases, realistic drop shadows, and individual fan-out tilt angles.
   - Mouse-tracking 3D room tilt (`rotateY` / `rotateX`).
   - Hover zoom elevation (`translateZ(90px) translateY(-25px) scale(1.08)`).
3. **Category Filter Pill Bar**:
   - Translucent frosted glass capsule bar filtering by `All`, `Romance`, `Short Story`, `Memoir`, `Classic`, `Fantasy`, and `Architecture`.
   - Dynamic dimming and focusing of matching volumes.
4. **Archival Compendium Section**:
   - Series spotlight for Letterpress Folios, Smyth-Sewn Hardcovers, and Monograph Boxsets.
