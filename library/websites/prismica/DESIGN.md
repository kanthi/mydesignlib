# Prismica AI — Design System

## Vision & Brand Identity
Prismica AI is a spectral intelligence platform. The visual language combines deep pitch-black space (#08090d) with vibrant, rainbow-refracted chromatic aberration flares, delicate hairline grids, and frosted glass elements.

## Color Palette
- Background: `#08090d` (Deep Pitch Black)
- Surface Card: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03)` with `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` border
- Prism Spectra Accent: Linear gradient `linear-gradient(135deg, #ff4500, #ff8c00, #ffd700, #00ff7f, #00bfff, #8a2be2)`
- Text Primary: `#f0f2f8`
- Text Muted: `#8a91a8`

## Typography
- Display / Headline: `Syne`, sans-serif / `Playfair Display`, serif
- Body / Code: `Plus Jakarta Sans`, `Inter`, sans-serif

## Core Layout & Components
1. **Header & Navigation**: Floating frosted glass bar with brand logo & `Menu ::` toggle pill.
2. **Hero Section**: Left-aligned headline with spectral gradient highlight, subhead, "Book a Call ↗" glass button. Right side features dramatic chromatic light architecture artwork and live canvas prism simulator.
3. **Trust Bar**: SVG logo grid of innovative partner teams.
4. **Spectral Intelligence Bento**: 4-quadrant feature cards with glass hover refraction.
5. **Interactive Prism Simulator**: Live canvas element allowing users to bend light beams into rainbow frequencies.
6. **Telemetry & Benchmarks**: Real-time accuracy, latency, and throughput counters.
7. **Pricing & Call Booking Modal**: Interactive CTA system.
