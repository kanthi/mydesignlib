# Resona — Customer Voice & Review Sentiment Intelligence

**Type:** Website Template  
**Category:** SaaS / Product Intelligence  
**Status:** Complete deliverable  
**Reference Origin:** Rebranded AI customer feedback & review synthesis SaaS platform with deep obsidian/indigo dark mode, vibrant purple-blue ambient illumination, rich interactive dashboard UI preview, and executive summary cards.

---

## 1. Brand Essence & Narrative
Resona is an enterprise-grade customer sentiment intelligence platform that aggregates reviews, support tickets, app store feedback, and social discussions into real-time actionable product directives.

## 4. Key Interactive Components

1. **Hero with Ambient Radiance**:
   - Glowing gradient radial backdrop.
   - Sparkle pill badge: `✦ Analyze thousands of reviews with AI`.
   - Dual action buttons: "Get started free" (gradient purple) + "Explore features" (bordered glass).
2. **Interactive High-Fidelity Dashboard Replica**:
   - Real-time review feed with platform origin tags (Google Reviews, App Store, Trustpilot, G2).
   - "Top Issues" frequency bar counters with click drilldowns.
   - "AI Summary" executive digest with automated recommendations.
3. **Monochrome Enterprise Logo Marquee**:
   - Algolia, Coinbase, Metricly, OrbitHQ, Clay, PostHog.
4. **Live Sentiment Classification Tester**:
   - Interactive widget allowing visitors to type or test sample reviews and watch Resona extract sentiment, urgent keywords, and recommended engineering tasks in real-time.
5. **Multi-Source Integrations & Metrics**:
   - 40+ review connectors and automatic Slack/Jira task routing.
