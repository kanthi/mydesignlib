## 2. Logo Design & Visual Identity System

### A. The Master Emblem & Shape Psychology
* **Outer Architectural Frame (Rounded Geometry)**: Conveys containment, structural stability, and structural engineering.
* **Continuous Spatial 'S' Traversal (Internal Geometric Path)**: Symbolizes spatial flow, kinetic light movement, and seamless transition between rooms.
* **Focal Center Node**: Anchor point representing human harmony and bespoke curation.
* **"Color Enhances, Never Rescues" Compliance**: The mark is constructed as pure single-color geometry that performs with 100% clarity in solid Ochre Brass (`#f5b82e`), Inverted White (`#ffffff`), and Flat Obsidian Black (`#101216`).

### B. The 4-Tier Responsive Logo Stack
1. **Tier 1: Master Lockup** — Geometric Portal Mark + `SOLVANTE` Wordmark (`letter-spacing: 0.18em`) + `ARCHITECTURAL INTERIORS` descriptor (for desktop headers, billboards, signage).
2. **Tier 2: Compact Nav Lockup** — Horizontal emblem + primary wordmark.
3. **Tier 3: Standalone Glyph / Monogram** — Pure vector portal icon without typography (social avatars, app icon, wax seal).
4. **Tier 4: Micro Favicon ($16\text{px} \times 16\text{px}$)** — Optimized vector stroke geometry for browser tabs and smartwatch badges.

### C. 3-Step Logo Stress Test Suite
* **Test 1: Gaussian Blur ($20\text{px}$)** — The bold outer frame and iconic 'S' traversal remain instantly identifiable under heavy blur.
* **Test 2: 5-Second Recall** — A single clear visual hook (the geometric spatial portal 'S') ensures immediate mental retention.
* **Test 3: 16px Scalability** — Engineered with a 7px base stroke on a 100-unit grid, preserving razor-sharp legibility at $16\text{px} \times 16\text{px}$.

* **Obsidian Charcoal (Canvas)**: `#101216` — Deep architectural darkness representing timeless quiet luxury.
* **Surface Slate**: `#191c24` — Elevated reading and card surface.
* **Warm Ochre Brass (Accent Primary)**: `#f5b82e` — Represents polished brass joinery, warm ambient lighting, and refined craft.
* **Forest Moss Slate (Accent Secondary)**: `#1b3d2f` — Evokes biophilic courtyards, aged patina, and natural stone.
* **Paper Light (Inversion)**: `#ffffff` — Crisp white readability for inverted applications.
* **Technical Gridwatermark**: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.025)` — Subconscious architectural drafting grid.

* **L (Leverage Point)**: Asymmetric 7/5 hero grid with bold $4.4\text{rem}$ display headline, glowing studio beacon badge, and live project specimen showcase.
* **I (Internal Rhythm)**: 8-Zone spatial progression:
  1. Header & Responsive Nav
  2. Asymmetric Hero Anchor
  3. Curated Architectural Trust Strip
  4. Materiality & Studio Philosophy
  5. 12-Column Compound Bento Capabilities
  6. **Interactive Brand Identity & Logo Stress-Test Lab**
  7. Filterable Portfolio Showcase
  8. Endorsements & Forest Moss CTA Chamber
* **F (Friction & Flow)**: Architectural telemetry annotations (`[PROJECT SPECIMEN // 084]`, `[16PX_FAVICON_VERIFIED]`) balanced with generous line heights ($1.65$) and spacious reading bands.
* **T (Transferability)**: Fluid responsive typography using CSS `clamp()`, flexible CSS grid columns, and full mobile drawer navigation.
