Meet The Baker Behind The Counter

Founder Note

"I wanted Miel to feel like the bakery you grew up with — only a little more playful."

That means knowing the dough by touch, learning every regular's morning order, and refusing to rush the parts that make a crust shatter into golden memory. We mill stoneground grain every Tuesday and let sourdough rest for 48 hours in cold cellar stone.

Arthur Vance Head Baker & Founder
