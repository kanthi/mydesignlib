# Fieldnote - Design System

## Aesthetic Commitment
Swiss editorial magazine — dense ink on cream, Cormorant Garamond display, DM Sans body. Asymmetric grid. Hairline rules and bold section numerals. Reminiscent of a curated print travel journal turned digital. NOT warm terracotta cliché — use deep forest green and dusty slate as accents.

## Palette
- `--surface-page: #f7f4ef` (aged cream, not pure white)
- `--surface-section: #f0ece4` (slightly deeper cream)
- `--text-primary: #1a1a18` (rich black ink)
- `--text-secondary: #6b6860` (warm muted)
- `--border-default: rgba(26,26,24,0.12)` (hairline)
- `--action-primary: #2d4a3e` (deep forest green — used sparingly)
- `--accent-slate: #4a5568` (dusty slate for tags/labels)
- `--accent-warm: #c4a882` (warm sand — one decorative use)

## Typography
- Display: Cormorant Garamond (Google Fonts) — H1/H2/pull quotes, weight 600
- Body: DM Sans (Google Fonts) — body, nav, labels
- Signature: large editorial section numbers (01, 02...) in Cormorant, muted

## Signature Device
Full-bleed hero with an editorial landscape photo (mountain pass or coastal path, muted film grain look) with a bold Cormorant headline overlaid at the bottom-left — like a magazine cover.
