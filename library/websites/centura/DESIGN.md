# Design System & Specification: Centura

## 1. Brand Identity & Rebrand Blueprint
- **Reference URL:** `https://x.com/Website_tmplts/status/2104202007787479260`
- **Original Brand:** Catalis by Temlis ("Build and Growth with Scalable Tools")
- **Fictional Brand:** **Centura**
- **Tagline:** Next-Generation Capital Architecture & Cloud Treasury
- **Category:** Fintech
- **Core Aesthetic:** Sky-inspired airy minimalism with azure cerulean gradients, soft billowy cumulus clouds, high-contrast modern editorial serif typography (`Playfair Display` / `Instrument Serif`), crisp floating glass telemetry cards, and cobalt radial meters.

## 2. Color Palette
- **Azure Sky Primary (`--sky-top`):** `#4B93E6`
- **Cerulean Midtone (`--sky-mid`):** `#7FB3F0`
- **Cloud Horizon (`--sky-light`):** `#D8E8FA`
- **Card Surface (`--card-bg`):** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.92)` with frosted backdrop blur
- **Primary Text (`--text-primary`):** `#111827`
- **Secondary Text (`--text-secondary`):** `#4B5563`
- **Electric Cobalt Accent (`--accent-blue`):** `#0052FF` (for gauge arcs, active tags, and telemetry)
- **Positive Green (`--accent-green`):** `#10B981`

## 3. Typography
- **Hero Display:** `'Playfair Display', Georgia, serif` with italic emphasis on *Growth* / *Velocity*.
- **Body & UI:** `'Plus Jakarta Sans', -apple-system, sans-serif` for navigation, controls, and body text.
- **Numbers & Metrics:** `'JetBrains Mono', monospace` for percentages and financial tickers.

## 4. Layout & Components
- **Floating Island Header:** Curved pill navbar docked against the top with brandmark, drop navigation, and pill CTA.
- **Cloud Sanctuary Hero:** Grand sky backdrop with floating frosted glass financial widgets:
  - Radial arc gauge (`Card User: 80%`) with animated blue LED ticks
  - Transaction growth card (`Transactions: 85% ↑ 12%`)
  - Monthly expenses bar telemetry
- **Empowerment Statement ("About Us"):** Centered large serif manifest on financial sovereignty.
- **Feature Matrix:** 4 frosted glass cards with interactive financial widgets (Intuitive UI, Automated Pipelines, Cryptographic Security, Real-Time Ledgers).
- **Interactive Expense & Yield Simulator:** Sliders allowing users to model annualized treasury yield and automated spend limits.
- **Pricing & Tiering:** Transparent starter, growth, and institutional tiers.
- **Footer:** Minimalist white cloud footer with compliance notice and directory.
