# Pulso — Website Design & Prompt Reference

## Prompt Concept
A warm, contemporary, high-impact digital marketing & brand growth agency website template named "Pulso". Fused with sunlit lifestyle photography, bold editorial typography with vibrant lime accent highlights (`#D9F958`), floating frosted glass cards, 3-stage roadmap progression, dark-mode portfolio contrast section, and high-conversion client metric indicators.

## Visual Characteristics
- **Color Palette**: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`), Soft Editorial Paper (`#FBFBF9`), Warm Beige Surface (`#F4EFEA`), Obsidian Ink (`#121413`), Kinetic Lime Accent (`#D9F958`), Lime Aura (`rgba(217, 249, 88, 0.35)`).
- **Hero Staging**: Warm lifestyle photography background featuring an entrepreneur at a sunlit wooden desk with an open laptop and notebook. Left-aligned bold headline with highlighted lime gradient text, dual CTA pills, and a floating dark glass "Our Trajectory" card.
- **Typography**: Plus Jakarta Sans (Headings & Display), Inter (Body & Editorial Copy), JetBrains Mono (Metrics & Telemetry).
- **Distinctive Elements**: High-contrast dark portfolio showcase section, 3-card metric manifesto block with colored status indicators (`120m+`, `40+`, `90%`), roadmap step milestone cards, split capabilities guarantees panel in vivid lime, verified client portrait testimonials, and interactive contact modal.
