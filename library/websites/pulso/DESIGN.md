# DESIGN — Pulso

## Overview
- **Product**: Pulso — High-Impact Digital Marketing & Brand Growth Agency.
- **Audience**: Scale-ups, DTC brands, venture-backed tech startups, and visionary founders looking for creative performance marketing and measurable ROI.
- **Single CTA**: "Book A Call ↗"
- **Emotion**: Creative confidence, strategic rigor, warm editorial humanity, vibrant kinetic growth.
- **Core Narrative**: "Power your growth with digital marketing services & brands. We combine strategy, creativity, and analytics to deliver campaigns that convert and scale."

## Aesthetic Commitment
- **Theme**: Warm Contemporary Editorial Agency (Luminous White, Rich Charcoal, Lime Accent `#D9F958`).
- **Signature Staging**: Warm lifestyle photography paired with bold geometric typography, high-contrast monochrome layout structure, tactile frosted glass cards, and electric lime badge accents.

## Color Tokens
- **Canvas Base**: `#FFFFFF` (Pure Studio White)
- **Canvas Warm Substrate**: `#FBFBF9` / `#F7F7F5` (Editorial Soft Paper)
- **Ink Primary**: `#121413` (Obsidian Charcoal Black)
- **Ink Secondary**: `#454A47` (Readable Editorial Prose)
- **Ink Muted**: `#767C78` (Subtitles, Meta, Captions)
- **Lime Accent**: `#D9F958` (Signature Kinetic Energy)
- **Lime Glow**: `rgba(217, 249, 88, 0.35)`
- **Warm Beige Card**: `#F4EFEA` (Tactile Stat Cards & Flatlays)
- **Dark Studio Section**: `#0F1110` (High-contrast Portfolio Block)
- **Border Subtle**: `rgba(18, 20, 19, 0.08)`
- **Border Glass**: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.22)`

## Typography
- **Headings & Display**: `Plus Jakarta Sans`, sans-serif (Weights: 500, 600, 700; Tight tracking: -0.035em)
- **Interface & Prose**: `Inter`, sans-serif (Weights: 400, 500, 600)
- **Metrics & Accents**: `JetBrains Mono`, monospace (KPI counters, timestamps, step numbers)
