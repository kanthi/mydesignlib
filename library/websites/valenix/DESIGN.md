# DESIGN — Valenix (Decentralized Infrastructure Protocol)

## 1. Overview & Positioning
- **Brand**: Valenix (`valenix.network`, fictional Web3 infrastructure protocol)
- **Reference**: Full 4-board Web3 startup design specification featuring ethereal alabaster ivory, frosted glass hardware microchips, concentric liquid ripple rings, gold caustic lighting, and tubular 3D glass typography.
- **Value Proposition**: Scalable, zero-knowledge decentralized infrastructure engineered for institutional throughput, cross-chain finality, and developer velocity.
- **Target Audience**: Web3 founders, protocol architects, enterprise fintech engineering leads, smart contract developers.

## 2. Color System & Semantic Tokens
- **Background Base**: `#ece9e3` to `#f4f2ee` (Warm Alabaster Ivory)
- **Background Surface / Card**: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.78)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(24px) saturate(180%)`
- **Card Border**: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9)` with hairline highlight `inset 0 1px 1px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.95)`
- **Accent Primary (Gold Caustic)**: `#d79738` (Amber Champagne)
- **Accent Glow**: `rgba(215, 151, 56, 0.24)`
- **Accent Muted**: `#f7d28b`
- **Text Primary**: `#141518` (Deep Titanium Obsidian)
- **Text Secondary / Muted Display**: `#848690` (Neutral Frosted Titanium)
- **Text Body**: `#555761` (Muted Graphite)
- **Button Dark**: `#18191c` (Solid Charcoal)
- **Button Light**: `#ffffff` (Solid White with soft drop shadow)
