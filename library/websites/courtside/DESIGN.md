# DESIGN — Courtside (Own The Court)

## Overview
- **Brand**: Courtside (`club@courtside-tennis.internal`, premier tennis club & championship academy)
- **Reference**: GetLayers "Baseline" template featuring a members' tennis club and academy carried with championship poise — deep court-blue, confident type ("OWN THE COURT"), and a calm scroll that reveals the coaching, the courts, and the pedigree one beat at a time.
- **Value Proposition**: From first-timers to nationally ranked juniors, players train here because the progress shows up on the court.
- **Target Audience**: Competitive juniors, touring athletes, and tennis club members.

## Color System
- `court-blue`: `#071224` (Deep Tournament Blue)
- `court-blue-card`: `#0c1c38`
- `chalk-white`: `#ffffff`
- `tennis-ball`: `#ccff00` (Optic Yellow / Neon Tennis Ball)
- `text-muted`: `#94a3b8`

## Typographic Hierarchy
- Primary Display: Ultra-heavy bold sans-serif `Plus Jakarta Sans` 900 for the massive poster typography (`OWN THE COURT`).
- Monospace Data: `Space Grotesk` for index labels (`01`, `02`, `03`, `04`) and proof telemetry.

## Layout & Architecture (Exact Match to Baseline)
1. **Header**: Left links (`Programs & Coaches`, `Club & Events`), center brand (`O COURTSIDE`), right button (`BOOK A VISIT`).
2. **Hero Fold**:
   - Giant uppercase statement: `OWN THE / COURT`
   - Bold rotated yellow sticker: `SHOW UP, / LEVEL UP`
   - Right description block: `Reserve a court for focused practice...` with visit button.
3. **Court Series / Social Proof**:
   - Tag: `COURT SERIES`
   - Headline: `Trusted by serious players`
   - Metrics: `100% ATP/WTA Coaching`, `#01 Regional Training Club`
   - Active members on court counter (`48 Players Live`).
4. **Training Programs**:
   - `TRAINING PROGRAMS / Built for every level`
   - 4 horizontal cards:
     - `01 Junior Development`: Fundamentals, footwork, and match play for ages 6–14.
     - `02 High Performance Squad`: Tournament prep, match strategy, and mental resilience.
     - `03 Adult Masterclass`: Tactical conditioning, serve velocity, and doubles positioning.
     - `04 Private Tour Coaching`: 1-on-1 personalized technical overhaul.
5. **By The Numbers**:
   - `18 Championship Courts`, `94% Tournament Win Rate`, `40+ Certified Coaches`.
6. **Footer**:
   - Copyright, invitation disclaimer, social links.
