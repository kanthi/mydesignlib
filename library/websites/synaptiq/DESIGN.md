# Synaptiq — Design System Specification

## Brand Identity & Vision
**Synaptiq** (`synaptiq`) is an autonomous AI customer support orchestration platform. It replaces sluggish tiered support queues with multimodal AI agents that learn institutional knowledge, triage complex inquiries, and autonomously resolve up to 80% of customer tickets with human-grade empathy.

The visual system pairs high-calm alpine biophilia (misty forest greens `#214336` and sage washes `#D8E2DC`) with surgical enterprise typography and crisp telemetry dashboards.

---

## 2. Typography

- **Headings & Primary Interface**: `Plus Jakarta Sans`, `sans-serif` (weight 700 / 800 for high-impact bold titles, weight 500 / 600 for buttons)
- **Display Serif Accents**: `Newsreader`, `serif` (used for editorial quotes)
- **Monospace Telemetry**: `JetBrains Mono`, `monospace` (benchmarks, SLAs, system coordinates)

---

## 3. Structural Sections

1. **Top Navigation**: Synaptiq mark, navigation anchors (Home, Features, Pricing, Blog, About, Contact), and dark `Get Started` button.
2. **Hero Stage**:
   - Eyebrow: `● New - Synaptiq 2.0 with multimodal AI agents`
   - Headline: `Automate customer support with intelligent AI agents`
   - Dual CTAs: `Get started free →` (with lime arrow badge) and `Talk to our team`.
   - Visual: Panoramic misty alpine forest stage with central AI agent junction connecting to live omni-channel traffic nodes (LinkedIn, Page visitors, Engagers, Commenters).
3. **Logo Ribbon**: Monochrome enterprise partner logos.
4. **The Problem Section**:
   - `Support teams are overwhelmed`: 48h average first response vs. 73% repetitive tickets.
5. **Multi-Channel Hub Stage**:
   - `Meet Synaptiq AI`: Central AI engine routing to Chat, Voice, Analytics, and Email.
6. **Scalable Support Capabilities**:
   - 3 interactive feature tabs (Automation, Smart Ticket Routing, Live Analytics) paired with live telemetry widgets:
     - Concentric radial architecture ring
     - AI resolution benchmark bar chart (92.4% vs 81.7% vs 58.9%)
     - Response time area curve chart.
7. **Conversion Banner & Footer**: Symmetrical enterprise footer.
