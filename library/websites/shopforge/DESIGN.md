# ShopForge — Design System & Engineering Architecture

> AI-powered commerce engine and autonomous storefront generator engineered for high-converting direct-to-consumer (D2C) brands and retail operators.

---

## 1. Brand Positioning & Identity

- **Brand Name**: ShopForge
- **Tagline**: Launch Your E-Commerce Store with our AI Commerce Engine
- **Core Value Proposition**: Generate, merchandise, and launch a complete production-grade online storefront populated with 30 pre-vetted winning products in under 10 minutes.
- **Visual Personality**: High-energy commerce tech. Deep obsidian forest dark ground (`#080c09`) accented with high-luminance neon lime (`#00ff66` / `#22c55e`), crisp white bento surfaces, and tactile e-commerce preview cards.

---

## 2. Color Tokens & Spatial Foundations

- **Forest Charcoal Dark**: `#080c09` (Hero ground and dark value stack)
- **Vibrant Lime Green**: `#00ff66` / `#22c55e` (Primary CTA buttons, active state indicators, checkmarks)
- **Pristine White Surface**: `#ffffff` (Bento feature containers, preview viewport)
- **Muted Gray Surface**: `#f8fafc` / `#f1f5f9` (Sub-cards, niche tiles, input boxes)
- **Text Dark**: `#0f172a` (Headlines on light surfaces)
- **Text Light**: `#f8fafc` (Headlines on dark surfaces)
- **Text Muted**: `#64748b` (Light surface subtitles) / `#94a3b8` (Dark surface subtitles)
- **Border Subtle**: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` (Dark) / `#e2e8f0` (Light)

---

## 3. Key Interactive Components

### A. Dynamic Niche Store Previewer (Hero)
- Category tabs: `Health`, `Beauty`, `Fashion`, `Kids`, `Tech`, `Pets`, `Home`, `Outdoor`.
- Live updating storefront mockup showing responsive header, hero banner with organic wellness imagery, promotional banner, and product catalog.

### B. 5-Step AI Generation Bento Flow
1. **Choose Store Niche**: Interactive 8-niche selector grid with live active badge.
2. **Choose Branding**: Live palette swatches, typography preview (`Aa Bb Cc`), and instant store name generator.
3. **30 Winning Products Carousel**: Interactive catalog of trending viral goods with margin calculations and supplier sync.
4. **Claim Free AI Store**: Store structure wireframe with floating green shopping bag icon.
5. **Start Making Money**: Real-time simulated push notifications of incoming orders ($450, $350).

### C. $1,050+ Free Value Stack Grid
- 6 high-value capability cards: AI Store Engine, 30+ Winning Products, High-Converting Product Pages, Branded Pages, StoreCopilot AI, Speed-Optimized Theme.

### D. Metric Counter Band & FAQ Accordion
- Triple metric stat cards: `800+ AI Stores Created`, `$1M+ Revenue Generated`, `100+ Hours Saved`.
- Multi-item interactive FAQ accordion addressing store ownership, supplier fulfillment, custom domains, and transparent pricing.

---

## 4. Typography & Layout Specifications

- **Headings Font**: Plus Jakarta Sans (Weights 700, 800)
- **Body Font**: Plus Jakarta Sans (Weights 400, 500, 600)
- **Monospace Font**: JetBrains Mono (Order IDs, pricing tags, margins)
- **Grid**: 1360px maximum container width, 12-column responsive layout.
