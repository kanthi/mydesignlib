## 3. Typographic Hierarchy

- **Technical Monospace Display:** `Space Mono` / `JetBrains Mono`, weights 400, 700.
  - Used for the primary manifesto statements, studio coordinate navigation, technical telemetry, and metadata tags.
  - Uppercase styling with generous letter-spacing (`0.04em` to `0.08em`) delivers authoritative, engineering-grade clarity.
- **High-Contrast Editorial Serif:** `Newsreader` / `Fraunces` / `Instrument Serif`, Roman 400 & 600.
  - Used for client wordmarks (`Covary`, `Lumex`, `Strand`) and campaign headlines, providing editorial warmth against cold technical geometry.
- **Precision Interface Sans:** `Plus Jakarta Sans`, weights 400, 500, 600.
  - Used for body copy, capability breakdowns, and interactive tool controls.
