Three jobs on the desk

Fathom keeps three jobs on the desk. It does not open a second set of books.

  1. 01

    The week letter

    A Monday note that names what moved in the shop books and what can wait.

  2. 02

    The forecast line

    One autumn line, held still until a single cost — flour or rent — is enough to move it.

  3. 03

    The spend question

    Where the money went, before anyone rewrites the whole book.
