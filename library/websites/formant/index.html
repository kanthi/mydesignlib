FAQs

Before we partner

Who will actually work on our project?

A founder leads every engagement for strategy and creative direction, supported by a small team of design and engineering collaborators. Care stays consistent regardless of scope.

How long do projects usually take?

Most projects run 10–14 weeks end-to-end. We set milestones from day one so timelines stay clear.

How do you communicate?

A shared project portal for timelines and deliverables, async updates, and weekly check-ins. Live calls for key decisions only.

What happens after launch?

Ninety days of hands-on support, documentation, and CMS training so your team can run the site with confidence. Care plans available after.

What is the investment range?

Projects typically start in the mid five figures depending on scope. We’ll scope a tailored proposal after a short intro call.
