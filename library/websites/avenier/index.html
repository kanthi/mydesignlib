Materiality & Spatial Craft

We reject synthetic facsimiles. Our atelier sources authentic geological and botanical elements directly from heritage quarries and artisan workshops across Europe and Japan.

MAT-01 • STONE

Travertine Navona

Cross-cut porous Roman limestone with organic natural veining, honed to a velvety matte tactile finish.

Origin: Tivoli, Italy • Thermal Mass Rating: High
MAT-02 • TIMBER

Fluted Smoked Oak

Slow-grown European oak fumed with natural ammonia vapors and hand-wirebrushed to accentuate grain depth.

Origin: Black Forest, Germany • FSC Certified
MAT-03 • METAL

Hand-Poured Bronze

Living metallurgical bronze hardware and bespoke plinths with chemical sulfur patinas that age with grace.

Origin: Foundry Basel, Switzerland • Custom Alloy
MAT-04 • TEXTILE

Belgian Bouclé & Linen

Unbleached natural flax fibers and dense wool loops woven on traditional heritage looms for effortless comfort.

Origin: Flanders, Belgium • Oeko-Tex Standard 100
MAT-05 • MINERAL

Honed Nero Marquina

Deep carbon black calcite marble streaked with crisp white fossilized veins, hand-buffed for subtle light refraction.

Origin: Markina, Basque Country • Non-Reflective
MAT-06 • GLASS

Low-Iron OptiWhite

Ultra-clear architectural glazing eliminating green tint shifts, ensuring pristine natural outdoor landscape vistas.

Origin: Saint-Gobain, France • Acoustic Laminate

The Architectural Protocol

A rigorous, collaborative methodology refined over a decade of international residential commissions.

01

Spatial Cartography

Site orientation analysis, solar path simulation, acoustic envelope mapping, and client lifestyle discovery.

02

Monolithic Modeling

Volumetric massing studies, continuous line drawings, physical timber scale maquettes, and natural lighting tests.

03

Artisanal Fabrication

Direct commission of bespoke joinery, stone mason sculpting, bronze pourings, and integrated architectural fixtures.

04

Turnkey Handover

Curated art acquisition, fine linen styling, scene-based architectural lighting calibration, and private estate key delivery.

RESIDENTIAL • 2025

Villa Lumière

Location

Provence, France

Area

820 m² / 8,825 sq ft

Materials

Travertine, Bronze, Smoked Oak

Handover

Autumn 2025

Conceived as a dialogue between daylight and nocturnal serenity, Villa Lumière integrates sweeping curved ceiling coves with recessed warm lumen emitters. The polished concrete gallery floor reflects twilight skies while floor-to-ceiling glass expanses dissolve the boundary between the interior living salon and the hillside infinity terrace.
