# Cognita — Design System Specification

**Brand:** Cognita (`cognita`)  
**Type:** Website Template  
**Category:** Education  
**Reference Origin:** Fictional rebrand of academic & workforce AI credentialing reference (`Answerr` by @oiharshit). Re-engineered as an institutional-grade AI fluency, readiness measurement, and cognitive certification platform for top universities and global enterprises.  
**Aesthetic Core:** Neoclassical Ivy League institutional prestige meets surgical AI verification telemetry. Warm parchment ivory substrate, rich Prussian blue architectural engravings (`#1E3B5C`), stark black typography, multi-axial radar/spider evaluation charts, and verified candidate dossier badges.

---

## 1. Overview & Brand Proposition

- **Core Problem:** Everyone uses AI, but organizations have no objective way to measure whether their students, faculty, or workforce are using it critically or merely outsourcing cognition.
- **Strategic Solution:** Cognita issues the **AIQ™ (Artificial Intelligence Quotient)** credential — evaluating Prompt Architecture, Judgment Quality, Epistemic Verification, Depth of Reasoning, and Learning Velocity.
- **Audience:** Provosts, Chief Information Officers, Deans, Corporate L&D leaders, and individual students/professionals.
- **Emotional Tone:** Scholarly, unassailable, rigorous, institutional yet forward-leaning.

## 3. Typographic Hierarchy

- **Institutional Display Serif:** `Newsreader` / `Cormorant Garamond` (Google Fonts), Optical sizes 16-72pt, weights 400, 500, 600, Italic.
  - Used for master questions: *"Anyone can use AI. Can you prove you use it well?"*, section headlines, and certification quotes.
- **Interface & Analytical Sans:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` / `Inter`, weights 400, 500, 600, 700.
  - Used for navigation, radar labels, dashboard telemetry, trust badges, and body copy.
- **Monospace Certification Telemetry:** `JetBrains Mono`, weights 400, 500.
  - Used for verification keys (`cognita.ai/certified/sarah-johnson`), score readouts (`87/100`), and evaluation rubrics.

## 4. Key Component Anatomy

1. **Top Institutional Announcement & Navigation:**
   - Pill announcement: *"Free for individuals. Deployed by universities and enterprises to measure, improve, and certify AI readiness at scale."*
   - Clean classical navigation: Higher Ed, Enterprise, AIQ™, Security, Research & Blog.
   - Dual actions: "Book a demo" (ghost), "See your AIQ" (solid black pill).

2. **Hero AIQ™ Credential Card & Neoclassical Dome Background:**
   - Background: Classical architectural etching of neoclassical university rotunda & library.
   - Floating Card with verified green dot badge, recipient identity (`Sarah Johnson, Babson Class of 2026`), composite score `87/100`, interactive SVG radar spider chart (5 dimensions), percentile benchmark (`Top 8% · AI Strategist`), and 5-tier evaluation metrics.

3. **Core AI Capabilities 4-Pillar Grid:**
   - AI Governance, Learning Intelligence, AI Readiness, AI Security.

4. **Institutional Scaled Pipeline (1 to 10,000):**
   - Individual Earns AIQ™ &rarr; Readiness Measured at Scale &rarr; Institution Deploys Cognita.

5. **Empirical Field Proof (Babson College Full-Term Case Study):**
   - Neoclassical campus frame with verifiable metrics: 40% Rise in curiosity-driven prompting, 4.5/5 Learner confidence, 211 Students across live coursework.

6. **The Old Contract vs The New Contract:**
   - Side-by-side comparison of traditional output grading (A+ paper) vs cognitive process evaluation (AIQ verified reasoning rubric).

7. **Multi-Role Operating Views (Tabs & Dashboards):**
   - CIO / Admin (Institutional policy, model usage, risk heatmaps).
   - Faculty (Learning trajectory, critical thinking depth, authentic inquiry).
   - Students (Personal AIQ profile, growth radar, portfolio verification).

8. **Enterprise Trust & Standards Seals:**
   - SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, FERPA, GDPR, HIPAA, SAML SSO, No Model Training, WCAG 2.1 AA.
