"Altamira wasn't merely constructed on the hillside; it was sculpted into the volcanic cliff like an extension of the earth itself."
Hand-hewn Soufrière quarry basalt anchors the foundation, while sustainably harvested Brazilian Ipe and Western Red Cedar soffits soften the clean cantilevered lines. Custom floor-to-ceiling glass pockets slide flush into hidden structural wall cavities, welcoming the Atlantic trade winds throughout the day and the soft sound of ocean surf at night.
