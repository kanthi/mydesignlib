# CANOPIA — Design System & Craft Specification

Modern editorial conservation and living biome web experience featuring atmospheric forestry cinematography, tactile sand-linen editorial layouts, floating frosted glass initiative cards, and verifiable grassroots climate telemetry.

## 1. Overview & Aesthetic Commitment

- **Brand:** Canopia (Global Ecological Collective & Reforestation Network)
- **Tagline:** Restoring Earth's Living Canopies Today
- **Core Narrative:** "Make earth green again today. Turning citizen awareness into verified physical restoration across 40+ global watersheds."
- **Aesthetic Direction:** Modern Botanical Editorial meets High-Impact Climate Telemetry.
  - Full-bleed cinematic forest immersion with organic light rays.
  - Tactile editorial transition into warm botanical sand/cream (#F7F5EC) for intellectual credibility and deep readability.
  - High-visibility solar lime accents (#D4E76C) guiding the eye toward high-conversion community actions.
  - Frosted dark emerald glass cards with floating elevation and circular directional arrow tokens.
  - Zero decorative AI-slop: real verified metrics, authentic documentary photography, explicit biome coordinates, and tactile interactive controls.

- **Display Headline:** `Plus Jakarta Sans`, 800 weight, line-height 0.98, tracking -0.04em.
- **Editorial Sub-Headings:** `Plus Jakarta Sans`, 700 weight, line-height 1.15, tracking -0.03em.

- **Micro-Labels & Telemetry:** `JetBrains Mono` / monospace, 500 & 600 weight, uppercase, tracking +0.08em.

## 4. Key Interactive Mechanics

1. **Hero Floating Card Tilt & Focus:**
   Hovering any of the 3 featured initiative cards highlights its solar arrow token, subtly tilts the glass plane, and dims sibling cards to isolate attention.
2. **Global Protection Pillars Switcher:**
   4-state pillar selector switching active mission focus (Afforestation, Circular Waste, Renewable Microgrids, Community Education) with synchronized documentary visual updates and metric readouts.
3. **Field Drive Chapter Drawer / RSVP Modal:**
   Interactive modal enabling users to register for upcoming tree-planting drives with immediate seat confirmation and calendar export.
4. **Ecological Transparency Ledger:**
   Filterable table documenting watershed coordinates, sapling species mix, canopy survival rate, and satellite verification status.
5. **Impact Counter Animation:**
   Scroll-triggered smooth count-up on key impact statistics.
6. **Objection Handling FAQ Accordion:**
   Clean spring-animated disclosure panels addressing operational transparency, funding allocation, and seedling monitoring.
