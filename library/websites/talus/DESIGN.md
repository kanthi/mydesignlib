# Talus — Product Studio Turning Ideas into Meaningful Products

A high-end cinematic product design and venture engineering studio template featuring an interactive cybernetic canvas hero.

## Aesthetic & Design Identity

- **Theme**: Deep space carbon (`#07080a`), tactical obsidian (`#0e1014`), and neon laser red/amber accents (`#ff441f` / `#ff8833`).
- **Atmosphere**: Technical, speculative, cinematic, high-authority.
- **Hero Centerpiece**: Full-bleed interactive canvas with mouse-tracking head rotation, procedural scanning laser visor HUD, autonomous breathing oscillation, and ambient studio vignettes.
- **Typography**:
  - Primary UI & Body: `Inter` variable sans-serif with tight letter-spacing.
  - Headings & Logotype: `Outfit` geometric display grotesk for strong editorial character.

## Core Sections

1. **Header & Navigation**:
   - Fixed navbar with triple-slash neon brandmark (`/// Talus`).
   - Frosted glass scroll transition (`rgba(7, 8, 10, 0.88)` + backdrop-filter blur).
   - Desktop links + mobile slide-down drawer.
2. **Hero Stage**:
   - Full-bleed interactive 60fps canvas stage with mouse-reactive parallax head rotation, real-time procedural HUD visor matrix, and ambient vignettes.
   - Split lockup: Left headline (`Product studio turning ideas into meaningful products`), Right editorial thesis and dual action buttons (`Work with Us`, `Explore Our Work`).
3. **Selected Work (Showcase)**:
   - 4 full case study cards with interactive hover translations, category pills, and generative vector illustrations (Kinetix Spatial OS, Aura Acoustic Platform, Vesper Agent Mesh, Chronos Cockpit HMI).
4. **Disciplines (Services)**:
   - Numbered horizontal rows: Product Discovery, Brand Architecture, Spatial & WebGL, Design Engineering.
5. **Impact Metrics**:
   - Quantitative proof strip ($2.4B+ Enterprise Value, 38 Awards, 99.4% Retention, 12ms latency).
6. **Footer**:
   - Global studio locations (San Francisco, London, Tokyo) and direct project inquiry callout.
