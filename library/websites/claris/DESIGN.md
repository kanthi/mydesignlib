# Claris — Design System Specification

## Brand Identity & Vision
- **Brand Name**: Claris
- **Product Proposition**: Minimalist, warm editorial cognitive space and thought architecture canvas. Designed for deep thinking, uncluttered research, and tranquil executive clarity.
- **Tagline**: *A clearer mind for a brighter you.*
- **Sub-tagline**: *Claris helps you capture ideas, organize your thoughts, and stay focused — all in one calm, distraction-free space.*

---

## Visual Architecture & Aesthetic DNA
- **Theme**: Warm Alabaster & Cream Editorial Minimalism.
- **Color Tokens**:
  - Canvas Base: `#FBFBF9` (warm off-white pearl)
  - Surface Card: `#FFFFFF` with whisper-soft border `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.06)`
  - Obsidian Ink: `#111216` (deep charcoal black text)
  - Muted Slate / Stone: `#6B7280` / `#9CA3AF`
  - Subtle Frosted Highlights: Soft mint `#ECFDF5`, Lavender `#F5F3FF`, Apricot `#FFF7ED`
- **Atmospheric Lighting**:
  - Soft peripheral indigo/sky vignette halo at the very top edge (`rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.08)` to transparent)
  - High-key center stage highlighting the sculptural portrait.

---

## Typography
- **Headings & Hero Serif**: `Instrument Serif` / `Playfair Display`, serif (italicized second line: *"for a brighter you."*)
- **Body & Controls**: `Inter`, sans-serif (400, 500, 600 weight)
- **Badges & Stamps**: `JetBrains Mono` / circular stamped badge (*"MORE FOCUSED YOU • EVERYDAY"*)

---

## Key Interactive Sections
1. **Pill Navigation Bar**: Floating frosted white pill with centered tab group (`Features`, `How it works`, `Integrations`, `Pricing`) and high-contrast pill CTA (`Get the app ↗`).
2. **Hero Stage**:
   - High-contrast editorial headline with italicized serif emphasis.
   - Dual floating glass cards with organic bezier connector curves:
     - Left: Audio waveform capture pill ("Turn thoughts into progress")
     - Right: Multi-device sync card with avatar stack ("Ideas, anytime, anywhere")
   - Sculptural thinker centerpiece with orbiting frosted glass cards (`Focus`, `Ideas`, `Plan`, and monochrome photography tiles).
   - Stamped rotating sunburst badge & social proof counter ("Trusted by 50,000+ creators worldwide").
3. **01 — The Studio of the Mind**: Multimodal split workspace showing real-time Markdown outlining, live web clipping, and acoustic focus mode.
4. **02 — The Cognitive Canvas**: Infinite drag-free spatial thought canvas with automatic bidirectional wikilinks.
5. **03 — Frictionless Integrations**: Native hooks into Notion, Obsidian, Linear, Slack, and Apple Reminders.
6. **04 — Thoughtful Metrics & Testimonials**: Editorial pull-quotes from prominent essayists, architects, and founders.
7. **05 — Simple Pricing**: Solitary ($0), Claris Member ($12/mo), Lifetime Patron ($280).
8. **06 — Minimalist Footer**: Clean typography, legal links, and quiet sign-off.
