# Clarion — Website Design & Prompt Reference

## Prompt Concept
A high-performance dark alpine minimalist SaaS landing page for an autonomous AI email intelligence platform named "Clarion". Designed with atmospheric verdant mountain ridge photography, high-density telemetry dashboards, interactive SVG analytics charts, live AI insight notifications, bento feature grid, multi-tier pricing calculator, and crisp typography.

## Visual Characteristics
- **Color Palette**: Deep Alpine Obsidian (`#080B09`), Surface Graphite (`#121714`), Forest Emerald (`#10B981`), Alpine Sky (`#38BDF8`), Muted Border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`), Crisp White (`#FFFFFF`).
- **Hero Staging**: Layered visual depth staging where an atmospheric panoramic mountain ridge sits behind a floating dark telemetry console with live KPIs ($4.28M Pipeline Value, 1,847 AI Leads), interactive revenue forecast area chart, and live pulsing AI insight alerts.
- **Typography**: Plus Jakarta Sans (Display & Headings), Inter (UI & Body), JetBrains Mono (Telemetry & Metrics).
- **Distinctive Elements**: Interactive 5-cell bento intelligence grid with embedded widgets (lead scoring bar charts, timeline forecast SVG, event-aware calendar), dual-cadence pricing switch with annual discount calculations, interactive command palette (`⌘K`), verified user testimonials, and a framed panoramic mountain landscape banner CTA.
