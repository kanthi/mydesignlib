# DESIGN — Clarion

## Overview
- **Product**: Clarion — Autonomous AI Email Intelligence & Telemetry Platform.
- **Audience**: High-output executives, founders, product leaders, engineering managers, and distributed teams buried in email threads.
- **Single CTA**: "Get Started Free"
- **Emotion**: Alpine tranquility, effortless executive clarity, high-velocity intelligence, quiet competence.
- **Core Narrative**: "Say Goodbye to Inbox Overwhelm. Meet Clarion — your intelligent inbox that summarizes, prioritizes, and suggests actions."

## Aesthetic Commitment
- **Theme**: Dark Alpine Minimalist SaaS (Obsidian & Emerald Atmospheric Telemetry).
- **Signature Staging**: Atmospheric rolling green mountain ridges and misty valleys sitting behind high-precision dark UI chrome, anchoring the contrast between calm natural serenity and powerful real-time AI automation.

## Color Tokens
- **Canvas Base**: `#080B09` (Deep Alpine Obsidian)
- **Canvas Secondary**: `#0D110F` (Surface Matte)
- **Surface Card**: `#121714` (Elevated Panel)
- **Surface Hover**: `#171D1A` (Interactive State)
- **Border Subtle**: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.07)`
- **Border Highlight**: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.16)`
- **Primary Accent / Emerald**: `#10B981` (High Impact / Live Pulse)
- **Accent Soft Glow**: `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.25)`
- **Secondary Accent / Alpine Sky**: `#38BDF8` (Forecast & Trajectory)
- **Attention Amber**: `#F59E0B` (Needs Attention Tag)
- **Text Crisp White**: `#FFFFFF` (Headings & Primary Labels)
- **Text Body**: `#D1D5DB` (Readable Prose)
- **Text Muted**: `#9CA3AF` (Secondary Copy & Subtitles)
- **Text Faint**: `#6B7280` (Grid lines, Monospace Telemetry)

## Typography
- **Headings & Display**: `Plus Jakarta Sans`, sans-serif (Tight letter-spacing: -0.04em, clean geometric terminals)
- **Interface & Prose**: `Inter`, sans-serif (Weights: 400, 500, 600)
- **Telemetry & Monospace**: `JetBrains Mono`, monospace (Timestamps, live models, currency counters)

   - Announcement Pill: `✨ AI 3.0 has been launched ->`
   - H1: `Say Goodbye to Inbox Overwhelm`
   - Subhead: `Meet Clarion — your intelligent inbox that summarizes, prioritizes, and suggests actions.`
   - Dual Action CTAs: `Get Started Free` (dark solid) + `See How It Works` (crisp white)

7. **Section: Real Results from Real Users (Testimonials)**:
