# DESIGN — Stratis (Intelligent Revenue Command Center & AI Deal Intelligence)

## Overview
- **Audience**: Chief Revenue Officers, VPs of Sales, RevOps leaders, and high-velocity AE teams requiring real-time pipeline forecasting, deal sentiment analysis, and autonomous CRM copilots.
- **Single CTA**: `Start Free Trial` (secondary: `Book Executive Demo` / `Calculate ROI`).
- **Emotion**: Frictionless clarity, calm enterprise precision, absolute revenue confidence.
- **Create this feeling through**:
  - Luminous light canvas (`#f9fafb`) layered with hairline borders (`rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.07)`) and soft ambient elevation shadows.
  - Signal color accents: Revenue Emerald (`#10b981`), Cobalt Indigo (`#4338ca`), and Violet (`#6366f1`).
  - Interactive Sales Command HUD: Floating multi-tier pipeline velocity card with live deal probability bars, quarterly pacing dial, and AI meeting assistant feed.
  - Interactive annual/monthly billing toggle with real-time price re-calculation and savings badge.
  - Smooth interactive FAQ accordion with micro-transitions.
  - Clean typography: `Plus Jakarta Sans` for geometric grotesque balance and `JetBrains Mono` for revenue metrics and currency telemetry.
