# DESIGN — Crafton (Precision Architectural Renovation & Construction System)

## Overview
- **Brand**: Crafton (Crafton Renovation & Construction Partners)
- **Reference**: High-converting architectural renovation landing page featuring orange circuit/conduit trace lines, modular grid numbered service cards with architectural schematic watermarks, real craftsman work photography, proof metrics, and a tabular project archive.
- **Fictional Rebrand**: **Crafton** (`contact@crafton.example`, licensed architectural contractor collective).
- **Core Value Proposition**: Full-scope home transformations, structural rebuilds, bespoke kitchen remodeling, and commercial fit-outs executed with millwork precision and guaranteed timelines.
- **Audience**: Homeowners, property developers, architects, and estate investors seeking dependable, licensed general contracting.
- **Primary Action (One CTA)**: `Get a Quote` / `Request Free Estimate`.

## Typography & Hierarchy
- **Display / Primary Headings**: `Syne` or `Plus Jakarta Sans`, 700/800 bold uppercase and tight geometric lockups (`font-weight: 800; letter-spacing: -0.03em; text-transform: uppercase`).
- **Body & Captions**: `Plus Jakarta Sans`, 400/500/600 for clear readability and high conversion ergonomics.
- **Numbers & Trace Accents**: `JetBrains Mono` or `Plus Jakarta Sans` for step indicators `01`, `02`, `03`, `04` and project metadata.

## Layout & Architecture (L.I.F.T. System)
1. **Header & Navigation**:
   - Brand lockup: Bold geometric angled gable icon + `CRAFTON`.
   - Links: Home, About Us, Services, Projects, Cost Calculator, Reviews.
   - Primary CTA: Solid orange pill button `Get a Quote ->` with animated hover arrow.
2. **Hero Section**:
   - Large bold typography: `RENOVATE` in heavy black, `YOUR SPACES` in radiant safety orange (`#ff5e14`).
   - Top right intro note: `"Welcome to Crafton, your licensed partner in precision renovation. We bring your vision to life with millwork accuracy."` + inline text link `Get a Quote ->`.
   - Left social proof badge: 4 circular team/craftsman avatars + `4.5k+ Homes Renovated across the U.S.`.
   - Continuous SVG Conduit Vector: An orange circuit line entering from the left margin, threading through a node anchor, bending over the title, and routing through the photography.
   - Hero Photography: Full-width framed craftsman image showing real sawdust, safety goggles, and power tools.
3. **Services Bento Grid (01 - 04)**:
   - Header: Pill tag `[•] Our Services`, H2 `Comprehensive Home Renovation Services`, and `View All Services ->` link.
   - Card 01 (Home Renovation): Top-featured card with routed orange circuit node, step `01`, blueprint architectural floorplan watermark, and `View Details ->`.
   - Card 02 (Kitchen Remodeling): Step `02`, custom SVG island/sink icon, blueprint watermark.
   - Card 03 (Bathroom Renovation): Step `03`, custom SVG freestanding tub icon, blueprint watermark.
   - Card 04 (Roofing & Structural): Step `04`, custom SVG truss structure icon, blueprint watermark.
   - Interactive Detail Drawer: Clicking any card opens a modal drawer with scope breakdown, materials, and timeline estimates.
4. **Experience & Craftsmanship Section**:
   - Asymmetric split layout:
     - Left: Real craftsmanship collage (timber framer with power tools + interior craftsman fitting cabinetry) with overlapping shadows.
     - Right: Pill badge `[•] About Us`, H2 `Expertise and Precision in Crafton Renovation`, descriptive copy on eco-friendly materials and guaranteed delivery.
     - 4 Ticked Proof Bullets:
       - `[✓] Certified master builders & dedicated craftsmen`
       - `[✓] Trusted by 450+ residential & commercial property owners`
       - `[✓] Every millimeter drafted and leveled with laser accuracy`
       - `[✓] Over 18 years of uninterrupted general contracting excellence`
     - Action button: `Know About Us ->` opening interactive company credentials modal.
5. **Interactive Project Portfolio / Excellence Archive**:
   - Header: Pill tag `[•] Recent Work`, H2 `Explore Our Excellence in Renovation Projects`, supporting paragraph.
   - Interactive Filter Tabs: `All`, `Residential`, `Kitchen`, `Bathroom`, `Commercial`.
   - Tabular Project Ledger with columns: Project Name, Location, Duration, Budget Tier, Year, Status.
   - Expandable rows or instant photo previews on hover.
6. **Interactive Renovation Cost Estimator**:
   - Dynamic real-time estimation widget: Select space (Kitchen, Bath, Whole Home, Attic/Roof), square footage slider, finish tier (Standard, Premium, Luxury).
   - Real-time estimated quote calculator with instant breakdown.
7. **Social Proof & Client Testimonials**:
   - Verified homeowner reviews with 5-star ratings, before/after metric callouts, and location tags.
8. **High-Converting Footer**:
   - Prominent conversion banner: `"Ready to transform your home with millimeter precision?"`
   - Estimate contact form modal trigger, license disclosure (`General Contractor Lic #NY-489201`), zero-commercial-copyright notice.
