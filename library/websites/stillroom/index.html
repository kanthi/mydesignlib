How a room opens

The order is the work. Skip a step and the week has nothing to sit on.

  1. Name the room

    North glass, Harbor type, a reprint. One name, one board. The room does not invent a project you did not write.

  2. Say who can touch it

    Full for the lead. Limited for build. View for the client who should see Friday and change nothing.

  3. The week draws itself

    Dates you already keep become the bars. The trail starts the same afternoon, with the branch you opened.
