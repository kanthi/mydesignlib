Before we start

Questions we get on the first call

Do you take retainers?

No. Four projects at a time, then we open the calendar. If you need a bench, we will say so on the call.

How long is a start?

Six to ten weeks for a system plus one surface. We send the sentence in week one. If that sentence is wrong, we stop.

Is Aetherstudio a real studio?

No. This is a sample in a private library. Names and work are invented. Mail goes to hello@aetherstudio.example.
