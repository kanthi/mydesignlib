# Vaultra — Design Specification

## Overview
Vaultra is a modern financial infrastructure and capital velocity platform that eliminates transaction drag, hidden interchange fees, and settlement latency in real time.

The design synthesizes:
1. **The Website Architecture**: A high-velocity SaaS framework featuring a sky-lit hero header, real-time connected rails console, social proof marquee, manifesto copy with colorful strikethroughs and badges, 6-card metric results bento, stacked feature suites, floating alert notifications, cloud-lit intelligence banner, floating integrations dock, and split FAQ contact layout.
2. **The Brand Identity**: A fintech visual system featuring the geometric 7-element construction logo mark (3 central horizontal pill capsules flanked by 4 circular circuit nodes), a high-energy cobalt & electric indigo palette (`#523eff`, `#7565ff`, `#978bff`, `#bab2ff`), tactile debit card textures, and crisp modern financial card containers.

## Logo Mark Specification
- **Symbol**: A precision grid mark representing financial consensus and multi-rail packet routing.
- **Components**:
  - Three centered horizontal rounded capsules (`rx=8`, `height=14`, `width=42`) stacked vertically.
  - Four outer circular nodes (`r=7`) positioned in the two flanking columns, aligned between the capsule gaps.
- **Brand Colors**: Cobalt gradient `#523EFF` to `#7565FF`.
