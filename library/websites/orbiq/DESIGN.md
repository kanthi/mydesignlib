# Orbiq — Design System & Technical Specification

> **The Modular Operations & Workflow Suite**  
> Fictional Rebrand derived from [@abid_led_design](https://x.com/abid_led_design/status/2096111340964466794) (Design by Zahra [@zahragr8r])  
> Clean, connected, high-clarity SaaS interface system

---

## 1. Brand Concept & Philosophy

**Orbiq** is a modular operational platform designed for engineering, inventory, and growth teams who are tired of fragmented tooling. It eliminates busywork by combining code insights, automated workflow triggers, inventory optimization, and customer management into a unified, high-speed workspace.

- **Primary Metaphor**: Modularity, precision, clarity, connection.
- **Brand Mark**: An eight-point geometric starburst / asterisk glyph representing convergence and multifaceted operations.
- **Voice & Tone**: Direct, energetic, utilitarian, calm confidence.

---

## 2. Design Tokens & Color Palette

### Color System
- **Background Pure**: `#ffffff` (Main content canvas)
- **Background Subtle**: `#f8fafc` (Card backings & neutral surfaces)
- **Background Alt**: `#f1f5f9` (Grid borders & division lines)
- **Primary Accent / Core Coral**: `#f95738` (High-energy orange/coral for CTAs, highlighted words, and featured plan)
- **Primary Accent Hover**: `#e04627`
- **Accent Soft**: `rgba(249, 87, 56, 0.08)` (Pills, badges, and active state fills)
- **Text Headings / Dark Slate**: `#0f172a` (High legibility, sharp contrast)
- **Text Body**: `#334155` (Balanced reading gray)
- **Text Muted / Dim**: `#64748b` (Secondary labels, metadata, captions)
- **Border Subtle**: `#e2e8f0` (1px clean outlines)
- **Status Colors**:
  - Success / Working: `#10b981` (Emerald)
  - Warning / Bug: `#f59e0b` (Amber)
  - Neutral / Done: `#64748b` (Slate)

### Typography
- **Headings & Display**: `Plus Jakarta Sans`, 700 / 800 weight, `-0.03em` tight tracking
- **Body & UI**: `Inter`, 400 / 500 / 600 weight, regular tracking
- **Monospace / Tags / Meta**: `Space Grotesk`, 500 weight, uppercase with `0.1em` letter-spacing

---

## 3. Signature Visual Devices

1. **Hero Asterisk Grid**: Subtle 80px × 80px architectural grid lines accented with miniature starburst glyphs at intersections.
2. **Product-as-Proof Interactive Dashboard**: Real HTML/CSS/SVG dashboard component showing real-time metrics, interactive tab toggling, filterable workflow table, and stacked telemetry bar charts.
3. **Featured Pricing Block**: A solid signature coral card (`#f95738`) with crisp inverted white typography that anchors the pricing tier hierarchy.
4. **Seamless Multi-View Switcher**: Quick client-side navigation allowing seamless transitions between *Product Tour*, *Integrations Directory*, *Pricing & Matrix*, and *Company / Culture*.
5. **App Icon Stamp Grid**: Curated 12-card integration ecosystem with custom SVG iconography, version numbers, and live search filtering.
