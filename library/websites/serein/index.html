Collection 01

Four things. Nothing else on the shelf.

Drawn before the room is warm. It does not foam, and it does not scent the towel. The morning starts when the water is gone.

40 ml · water

A light oil, not a coat. Pressed from the palms onto the face while the water is still there. It should disappear before you reach the door.

30 ml · oil

A short distillate of garden leaves. Pat it where the day will hit. The color in the bottle is the color of the shade, not a dye.

50 ml · distillate

The last step, and only at night. Enough to quiet the skin. Not enough to pillow the face in wax.

45 ml · cream

The list stays short.

Serein is a morning practice: water, a thin oil, a green distillate, and a cream for when the light goes. Nothing is added to cover the smell of the leaves.

Collection 01 is pressed in small lots. When a lot ends, this page says so. The next one does not arrive early.

The shadow of a leaf

The fern stays in the window. The mark it leaves is the only pattern the house allows on skin.

2 April 2026

Why the oil stays thin

Four drops between the hands. A heavier oil would sit on the skin and hide the water underneath.
