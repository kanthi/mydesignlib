# DESIGN — Brinekeep AGY (Charminar Study)

## Overview
- **Monument:** Charminar (Hyderabad Deccan, 1591 CE) 3D Architectural Construction Study.
- **Audience:** Design-curious visitors, architects, and studio teams exploring Indian architectural heritage through autonomous procedural WebGL geometry.
- **Single CTA:** Request a berth
- **Emotion:** Radiant Deccan heritage — four soaring minarets and cardinal arches rising course by course in granite, lime-mortar stucco, and amber gold.
- **Signature Technique:** Dynamic WebGL clipping plane elevation with procedural teak scaffolding raising itself one course ahead of the stone and stucco.

---

## 1. Aesthetic Commitment & Visual Tone

- **Indo-Islamic Architectural Geometry**: Four soaring 4-storey fluted minarets with double-corbelled balconies, four grand cardinal pointed arches, upper jharokha loggias, and gilded dome finials.
- **Deccan Palette**: Warm granite plinth (`#8A8476`), yellow sandstone masonry (`#DED2B8`), polished lime stucco (`#F4ECD8`), saffron amber accents (`#E07A2A`), and gold finials (`#D49B38`).
- **Dynamic 3D Study Frame**: Full-viewport WebGL stage with interactive camera orbit, solar time-of-day engine, atmospheric weather particle systems, and 2D canvas pointer trail.

---

## 2. 3D Architectural Stages

1. **`I. BUNYAAD`** (`GRANITE PLINTH & BASIN`): 0.0m – 0.80m dressed granite stepped plinth and fountain basin.
2. **`II. CHAUDISHA`** (`FOUR CARDINAL ARCHES`): 0.80m – 4.80m four soaring pointed arches with stucco spandrel medallions.
3. **`III. JHAROKHA`** (`DOUBLE BALCONY LOGGIAS`): 4.80m – 7.80m arcaded galleries, stone jali screens, and chhajja eaves.
4. **`IV. MEHRAAB`** (`FOUR FLUTED MINARETS`): 7.80m – 11.20m four cylindrical minaret shafts with corbelled balconies.
5. **`V. GUMBAD`** (`DOMES & GOLD FINIALS`): 11.20m – 13.50m four onion domes, petal calyxes, and gilded kalasa finials.

---

## 3. Lighting & Solar Engine

- **Dawn (`Bhor / Fajr`)**: Lilac-blue morning sky, soft low-angle solar rays on Deccan granite.
- **Noon (`Dopahar`)**: High radiant sun casting crisp shadows on yellow sandstone and white stucco.
- **Sunset (`Shaam`)**: Rich saffron and amber sunset glow across the four minarets.
- **Night (`Raat`)**: Midnight starry sky with golden architectural floodlights and minaret illumination.

---

## 4. Atmospheric Weather Systems

- **Clear (`Mausam`)**: Radiant sunlit clarity.
- **Haze (`Dhund`)**: Warm atmospheric dust haze across the plateau.
- **Monsoon (`Barsaat`)**: Heavy tropical rain with lightning strikes and wet stone reflections.
- **Noor / Embers (`Diya Sparks`)**: Floating golden celebratory sparks and embers rising around the minarets.
