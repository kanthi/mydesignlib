# Lumind — Design System Specification

## Brand Identity & Vision
- **Brand Name**: Lumind
- **Product Proposition**: Personal AI Second Brain & Cognitive Operating System. Capture chaotic thoughts, synthesize connected memory graphs, and effortlessly channel intent into focused execution.
- **Tagline**: *Your mind, upgraded.*
- **Sub-tagline**: *A calmer mind builds a brighter future.*

---

## Visual Architecture & Aesthetic DNA
- **Theme**: Luminous Glassmorphic Deep Charcoal / Obsidian Cosmos.
- **Canvas Base**: `#0B0C10` transitioning upward into an ethereal radiant silvery-white aurora core (`#FFFFFF` to `#E0E7FF` soft flare) in the hero section, framing the glass orbital thinker centerpiece.
- **Accent Glows**:
  - Indigo/Violet: `#6366F1` / `#818CF8`
  - Sky Cyan: `#38BDF8`
  - Electric Amber (Focus/Notes): `#FBBF24`
  - Emerald Mint (Signal/Online): `#10B981`
- **Glassmorphism Spec**:
  - Background: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)` to `rgba(18, 20, 29, 0.75)`
  - Backdrop Filter: `blur(20px) saturate(180%)`
  - Border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`
  - Outer Glow: `0 20px 40px -15px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.5), inset 0 1px 1px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`

---

## Typography
- **Headings & Brand**: `Plus Jakarta Sans`, sans-serif (800, 700, 600 weight, crisp kerning `-0.03em`)
- **Body & Micro-UI**: `Inter`, sans-serif (400, 500, 600 weight)
- **Monospace Accents**: `JetBrains Mono`, monospace (`01`, `02`, timecodes, audio Hertz indicators)

---

## Core Interactive Sections
1. **Pill Glass Header**: Minimal floating frosted glass navigation bar with blur reflection and magnetic CTA button.
2. **Kinetic Hero with Orbiting Thinker**:
   - Central high-res visual: Refractive glass halo thinker with subtle ambient floating physics.
   - Floating interactive 3D glass tiles:
     - *Ideas Card* ("Turn thoughts into progress", tag badges, holographic orb)
     - *Organize Card* ("Clarity in chaos", mini knowledge graph connector preview)
     - *Focus Card* ("Less noise. More you.", interactive sound wave toggle with Web Audio pink noise/binaural drone)
     - *Mon 17 Calendar Chip* & yellow memo note
   - Dual corner eyebrows & 3 numbered annotations (`01 Capture Anything`, `02 Stay Organized`, `03 Get More Done`).
   - Avatar social proof stack ("100,000+ creators, researchers, and founders").
3. **Feature Deep-Dive 01: Capture Frictionless**: Multimodal voice memo transcriber simulation, quick markdown capture, and smart clipboard ingest.
4. **Feature Deep-Dive 02: Neural Memory Graph**: Interactive canvas simulating interconnected thoughts, semantic tagging, and vector auto-clustering.
5. **Feature Deep-Dive 03: Flow State & Audio Sanctuary**: Built-in interactive ambient sound engine (Binaural Beta Waves 40Hz, Deep Brown Noise, Rainfall) with real-time SVG waveform visualizer.
6. **Feature Deep-Dive 04: Action Synthesis Engine**: Automated turning of scattered unstructured notes into structured Gantt/task checklists with 1-click calendar sync.
7. **Social Proof & Metrics**: Quantitative telemetry (4.2 hrs saved/week, 99.4% recall accuracy, 85% less cognitive load).
8. **Transparent Pricing**: Personal (Free), Lumind Pro ($14/mo), Studio ($29/mo).
9. **Curated FAQ Accordion & Closing Manifesto**: Expandable interactive Q&A and final call to elevate human cognition.
