Is my personal knowledge base kept private? ▼

Yes, uncompromisingly. All embeddings and notes are end-to-end encrypted with your private key. We never train frontier AI models on your notes, and you can export your entire database as raw Markdown anytime.

How does the binaural 40Hz focus generator work? ▼

Our audio engine utilizes the Web Audio API to play two slightly offset sine wave frequencies (e.g., 200Hz left ear, 240Hz right ear). The brain perceives an illusory 40Hz beat frequency which promotes gamma-band neuronal coherence, clinically linked to working memory enhancement.

Can I import my existing notes from Notion or Apple Notes? ▼

Yes! Lumind offers 1-click importers for Notion, Obsidian, Roam, and Apple Notes zip archives. It immediately builds the neural graph connections across your historical library.
