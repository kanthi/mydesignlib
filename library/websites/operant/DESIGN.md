# Operant — Design System Specification

## Brand Identity & Vision
- **Brand Name**: Operant Partners (`operant`)
- **Product Proposition**: Autonomous & Embedded Back-Office Operations Partner for high-growth ventures and technical founders. Invoicing, supplier reconciliation, compliance filings, and recurring vendor workflows.
- **Value Guarantee / Headline**: *We take care of the operational work behind your company. If it's not handled, you don't pay.*
- **Sub-tagline**: *From invoices and supplier requests to recurring deadlines, Operant keeps track of what your team needs, so founders can stop being the link between every moving part and get back to building.*

---

## Visual Architecture & Aesthetic DNA
- **Theme**: Deep Forest Obsidian & Halftone Stippled Monolith.
- **Color Tokens**:
  - Canvas Background: `#0C1210` (deepest forest obsidian)
  - Surface Card: `#121A17` / `#16211D`
  - Text Primary: `#EDEDE8` (warm bone white)
  - Accent Muted Sage: `#9CAE9F` / `#A3B899`
  - Stipple Flow: Warm silver stippled ribbon gradient (`#D0D5CD`)
  - Accent Glow / Guarantee: `#4ADE80` (emerald reassurance)
- **Atmosphere**:
  - Subtle noise grain texture overlay.
  - Minimalist, high-conviction Swiss editorial typography with classical serif statements.

---

## Typography
- **Headings & Manifesto**: `Playfair Display` / `Newsreader` (500, 600 weight serif, elegant proportional tracking).
- **Body & Controls**: `Inter` / `Plus Jakarta Sans` (400, 500 weight).
- **Metadata & Indicators**: `JetBrains Mono` (`01`, `STATUS: GUARANTEED`, `AUDITED`).

---

## Key Interactive Sections
1. **Header**: Minimalist wordmark with geometric eight-dot constellation icon and discrete nav links.
2. **Hero Stage**:
   - Serif conviction manifesto: "We take care of the operational work behind your company. If it's not handled, you don't pay."
   - Cream call-to-action button: `Book a discovery call`.
   - Halftone ribbon wave with interactive coordinate nodes (diamond control handles).
3. **01 — The Handled Guarantee**: Interactive SLA calculator (Hours saved, vendor cycles reconciled, refund protection).
4. **02 — Scope of Operations**:
   - Accounts Payable & Invoicing automation
   - Vendor & SaaS contract renewals
   - Payroll, tax clearance & state filings
   - Cross-border contractor compliance
5. **03 — Real-time Operational Ledger Simulation**: Interactive live feed of completed back-office operations.
6. **04 — Founder Trust & Transparent Retainers**: Growth ($2,500/mo), Scale ($4,800/mo), Custom Enterprise.
7. **05 — Booking Dialog & Quiet Sign-off**.
