# DESIGN — Glyphora (High-Craft Editorial Typography & Subtle Motion Studio)

## Overview
- **Audience**: Design directors, creative technologists, digital typographers, and frontend craft engineers seeking understated, whisper-quiet kinetic typography.
- **Single CTA**: `Explore Specimens` / `Copy Animation CSS` (secondary: `View Type Laboratory` / `Test Playground`).
- **Emotion**: Timeless editorial prestige, quiet confidence, typographic tactile poise.
- **Create this feeling through**:
  - Warm alabaster paper canvas (`#fbfaf8`) contrasted against ink obsidian (`#121316`) and muted terracotta copper (`#c86446`).
  - High-refinement editorial serif typography (`Instrument Serif` / `Cormorant Garamond`) paired with architectural grotesque (`Plus Jakarta Sans`) and mechanical monospace (`JetBrains Mono`).
  - 4 signature subtle text animation primitives:
    1. Character Stagger & Rise (`split-char`)
    2. Word Focus & Opacity Roll (`word-roll`)
    3. Hairline Underline Velocity Scrub (`line-trace`)
    4. Kinetic Masked Phrase Lift (`mask-lift`)
  - Interactive live typing playground with real-time animation switching, duration controls, and one-click copyable CSS keyframes.
