# Kalliope — Autonomous Multilingual AI Voice Receptionist

**Type:** Website Template  
**Category:** SaaS / Developer / Services  
**Status:** Complete deliverable  
**Reference Origin:** Rebranded voice AI startup design with pristine Scandinavian whitespace, vertical pinstripe header/footer accents, moss-green stepped pyramid geometry, luminous acoustic gradient orb, and clean bento metrics.

---

## 1. Brand Essence & Narrative
Kalliope is an autonomous, multilingual voice AI agent designed for medical clinics, dental practices, and legal firms. She answers inbound phone calls 24/7 without delays or busy signals, schedules appointments directly into CRM/EHR calendars, and resolves routine patient questions with empathetic human inflection.
