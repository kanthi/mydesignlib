# Klyro — Design System & Architectural Blueprint

> **Rebrand Source**: Derived from Heloxone personal finance landing page reference (`expeckly` on X / Twitter status `2104775674095645092`)
> **Identity**: **Klyro** (`klyro`) — *Take Control Of Your Money, Effortlessly*
> **Category**: `Fintech` / `Personal Finance & Mobile App`

---

## 1. Brand Philosophy & Aesthetic

- **Mission**: Make personal finance and expense intelligence effortless, transparent, and delight-driven. Replace intimidating spreadsheets and fragmented banking portals with a unified, predictive cashflow companion.
- **Visual Style**: Clean, modern, optimistic neo-fintech aesthetic. Off-white canvas, floating pill navigation with frosted glass blur, high-fidelity smartphone device showcase, ambient peach-lime gradient aura, and playful dual-tone headline accents (`Effort` in warm coral `#FF5E36` and `lessly` in sky blue `#3B82F6`).
- **Signature Devices**:
  - Floating `# Category` hashtag chips (`# Coffee`, `# Groceries`, `# Subscriptions`, `# Transport`, `# Electricity`) with organic bobbing motion.
  - Floating social review chip featuring overlapping real user avatars, `+1.8K Reviews`, and 5 gold stars.
  - Inline typographic pill badges seamlessly embedded within display text (`[ ✓ Income ]`, `[ 🪙 Manage Your Money ]`, `[ ⊖ Expense ]`, `[ ↗ Compound Yield ]`).
  - Interactive phone mockup with real-time portfolio balance, interactive weekday spline chart (`Mon` through `Sun`), and quick Send/Deposit triggers.

## 3. Typography Hierarchy

- **Primary Display & Sans**: `Inter` / `Plus Jakarta Sans` (weights 400, 500, 600, 700, 800) — geometric, legible, premium consumer technology feel.
- **Brand Wordmark**: Bold uppercase tracking `KLYRO` with tight letter-spacing (`-0.03em`).
- **Tabular Figures & Metrics**: `Inter` / `JetBrains Mono` for portfolio balances, percentages, and stock stats.

---

## 4. Key Interactive Components

1. **Floating Frosted Pill Header**:
   - `KLYRO` wordmark, anchor links, WhatsApp/Telegram launch bubbles, and `Start For Free` pill CTA.
2. **Interactive Phone Device Mockup**:
   - Live portfolio balance (`$24,580.45`), animated interactive spline chart with weekday switcher, interactive deposit/send counter, and stock market ticker.
3. **Surrounding Floating Physics Chips**:
   - 5 floating hashtag pills (`# Coffee`, `# Groceries`, `# Subscriptions`, `# Transport`, `# Electricity`) with subtle CSS floating float keyframes.
   - 5-avatar social review badge (`+1.8K Reviews` & 5 stars).
4. **Inline Typographic Badge Statement**:
   - High-impact visual sentence with embedded, interactive micro-badges (`[ ✓ Income ]`, `[ 🪙 Manage Your Money ]`, `[ ⊖ Expense ]`, `[ ↗ Growth ]`).
5. **The Problem Section**:
   - Dark glossy pill `The Problem`, headline with highlighted `Spending?` and 3 interactive cards diagnosing the friction of modern money management.
6. **Live Interactive Annual Savings Calculator**:
   - Drag slider to calculate projected annual wealth saved through automatic subscription audits and 4.85% APY cash sweeps.
7. **Pricing, FAQ, and App Store CTA Footer**.
