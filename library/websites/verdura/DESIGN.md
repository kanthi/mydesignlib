## 4. Complete Section Motion & Interaction Architecture

1. **Lenis Momentum Physics & Global Navigation:**
   - Butter-smooth wheel scrolling with cubic deceleration curve.
   - Pinned top navbar with dynamic blur and padding contraction upon scrolling past 50px.
   - Real-time botanical progress bar running along the top viewport edge.
   - Custom magnetic botanical cursor with follower halo expanding over interactive surfaces.

2. **Hero Monolith & Spatial Parallax Handoff:**
   - Central photorealistic botanical rock with real-time pointer-tracking 3D perspective rotation ($rx, ry$) and inertial spring damping.
   - Interactive 3D botanical blooming cluster: hovering the monolith triggers an organic expansion of flowering lichen and moss clusters in 3D space.
   - Seamless scroll-driven parallax handoff: as the user scrolls, the monumental "Verdura" typography expands and fades upward, while the monolith gracefully sinks and scales down into the page substrate.

3. **Section 02 — Living Monolith Collection & 3D Card Physics:**
   - Staggered scroll-triggered entrance for header labels, titles, and card elements.
   - Interactive 3D perspective card physics: each specimen card calculates mouse coordinates on hover to apply subtle $rx/ry$ tilts, inner image zoom, and glass glare reflection.
   - Smooth FLIP filter animation: switching specimen filters triggers smooth scale-down/fade transitions before presenting the active category.
   - Specimen Quick-Inspection Drawer / Modal: clicking any specimen opens an architectural specification sheet with scientific readouts and reservation controls.

4. **Section 03 — Greenhouse Grading Protocol (Sticky Interactive Scrubber):**
   - Media viewport on the left features animated holographic laser scanline beam and live telemetry chips (`CHAMBER: ALPHA`, `RELATIVE FLUX`, `CIRCADIAN SPECTRUM`).
   - Interactive 3-step timeline: clicking or scrubbing steps activates smooth cross-fades between high-resolution chamber environments and updates laboratory readouts.

5. **Section 04 — Habitat Vitality Simulator (Dynamic Spore Bio-Engine):**
   - Live HTML5 Canvas rendering organic chlorophyl spore dispersal and cellular drifting.
   - Interactive humidity and temperature sliders modulate spore velocity, cell radius, and luminescence.
   - Dynamic ambient lighting shift: changing Kelvin temperature transitions the simulator's background gradient from warm sunrise amber (2700K) to crisp alpine daylight (6500K).
   - Real-time organic vitality index dial with animated SVG progress bar.

6. **Section 05 — Architectural Spatial Installations:**
   - Multi-card architectural gallery displaying installations in luxury private residences, atriums, and botanical studies.
   - Card lift, image scale, and gradient overlay transitions with direct dossier requests.

7. **Section 06 — Commission Studio & Customizer:**
   - Interactive chip customizer for geological matrix, botanical strains, and spatial scale.
   - Live specification estimate generator with animated state updates.
