Questions before the walk

Do you take payment before the first visit?

No. The site walk is free. A deposit is only due when you accept the written quote and we hold a start week.

What does the five-year planting cover include?

Stock we supply and plant. If it fails in ordinary conditions, we replace it. Storm damage and neglect are out of cover — we write that on the quote.

Can you work a small terrace?

Yes, if we can get tools in. We will say no if access means tearing a hallway. That is cheaper than pretending otherwise.

Are you the same as a landscape architect?

No. We design planting and keep the plot. If you need drawings for planning, we will point you to someone who does that work.
