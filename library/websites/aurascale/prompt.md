# Rebuild Prompt — AuraScale

Create a production-grade, mint-lit dark revenue growth SaaS website template for **AuraScale**.

## Requirements
1. **Brand Identity**:
   - Brand name: "AuraScale".
   - Theme: Deep space background (`#020611`), mint emerald (`#10b981`), atmospheric cyan (`#00f2fe`), frosted glass cards, and crisp white typography.
   - Clean grotesque typography using `Plus Jakarta Sans` and `Space Grotesk`.

2. **Centered Hero & 3D Living Earth**:
   - Viewport underlaid with an interactive Three.js 3D Earth globe with realistic day/night lighting, night city lights, a drifting cloud shell, and a mint-cyan atmospheric Rayleigh scattering glow halo.
   - Exact centered hero typography:
     - Headline: "Scale Smarter, Not Harder"
     - Subhead: "Drive your funnel forward with clever workflows, analytics, and seamless lead management — all in one immersive command center."
     - CTAs: "Start Free Right Now →" and "Schedule a Consult"
   - Scroll-driven kinematics: As user scrolls, the Earth sinks and swings smoothly.

3. **Sections**:
   - Partner strip: "TRUSTED BY FAST-MOVING TEAMS" (Nimbus, Pluma, Citrus, Spider, Helod, Vertx).
   - "One platform to run the entire revenue engine" with clever workflow branching cards.
   - "BUILT FOR MOMENTUM / See the whole funnel move in real time" with live pipeline telemetry bars.
   - "READY WHEN YOU ARE / Put your growth on a new trajectory" final CTA section.
   - Standard dark footer.

4. **Compliance & Constraints**:
   - Pure vanilla HTML/CSS/JS without npm/build dependencies.
   - No search indexing meta tags (`robots` and `googlebot` noindex).
   - Google Analytics gtag `G-Z97ZD3EVSF` in `<head>`.
