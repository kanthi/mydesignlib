# DESIGN — AuraScale (Scale Smarter, Not Harder)

## Overview
- **Brand**: AuraScale (`growth@aurascale.internal`, revenue growth engine & funnel command center)
- **Reference**: GetLayers "Ascend" WebGL template featuring a living 3D Earth turning in deep space behind a mint-lit SaaS landing page, city lights, drifting clouds, centered hero copy ("Scale Smarter, Not Harder"), and dynamic scroll sink/swing.
- **Value Proposition**: Drive your funnel forward with clever workflows, analytics, and seamless lead management in one immersive command center.
- **Target Audience**: High-velocity B2B SaaS teams, revenue operations leaders, and growth marketers.

## Color System
- `bg-dark`: `#020611` (Deep Cosmic Black)
- `card-bg`: `rgba(9, 16, 33, 0.72)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`
- `card-border`: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`
- `accent-mint`: `#10b981` (Ascend Mint)
- `accent-cyan`: `#00f2fe` (Atmospheric Cyan)
- `text-white`: `#ffffff`
- `text-muted`: `#94a3b8`

## 3D Earth Mechanics
- Three.js WebGL Earth with procedural oceans, realistic continent masks, golden night lights, drifting cloud shell, and mint-cyan Rayleigh glow.
- Scroll Kinematics: As the user scrolls, the Earth sinks and swings laterally on its polar axis.

## Page Architecture
1. **Header**: Brand mark with mint arrow icon, navigation links (`Features`, `Solutions`, `Plans`, `Learning`), and `Sign Up` button.
2. **Centered Hero Fold**:
   - Headline: `Scale Smarter, / Not Harder`
   - Subhead: `Drive your funnel forward with clever workflows, analytics, and seamless lead management — all in one immersive command center.`
   - CTAs: `Start Free Right Now →` and `Schedule a Consult`.
3. **Partner Logo Strip**: `TRUSTED BY FAST-MOVING TEAMS` with 6 logos (Nimbus, Pluma, Citrus, Spider, Helod, Vertx).
4. **Platform Architecture**: `One platform to run the entire revenue engine` with branching workflow cards.
5. **Live Funnel Telemetry**: `BUILT FOR MOMENTUM / See the whole funnel move in real time` with live pipeline stage bars.
6. **Trajectory Launch CTA**: `READY WHEN YOU ARE / Put your growth on a new trajectory`.
7. **Footer**: Security & privacy disclaimers, copyright, noindex robots.
