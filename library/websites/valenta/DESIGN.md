## 3. Typography Architecture

- **Primary Grotesque**: **Plus Jakarta Sans** (Google Fonts)
  - Bold (700) and ExtraBold (800) for authoritative clean headlines.
  - Medium (500) and SemiBold (600) for interface navigation, form inputs, and policy text.
- **Display Editorial Serif**: **Instrument Serif** (Google Fonts)
  - Expressive cursive italics rendered with chromatic gradient text clipping (`-webkit-background-clip: text`) for high-contrast focal emphasis (*"Business Efficiency"*, *"your business"*, *"the paperwork"*, *"Save both"*).
- **Tabular Monospace**: **JetBrains Mono** (Google Fonts)
  - Currency figures (`$12,450.00`, `$1,299.00`), transaction IDs, policy thresholds, and live ticker counters.
