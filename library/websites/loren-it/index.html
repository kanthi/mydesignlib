How we work

Delivery sequence

Our engagements follow a deterministic progression designed to uncover architectural bottlenecks prior to committing production traffic.

1

Infrastructure Audit & Threat Model

Two-week technical deep dive reviewing existing repository state, IAM trust policies, network ingress paths, and single points of failure. We produce an actionable remediation roadmap with concrete risk ratings.

2

Target Architecture & Proof-of-Concept

Collaborative RFC phase specifying IaC modules, VPC topologies, and deployment gates. We validate the architecture on an isolated staging cluster under simulated production load.

3

Canary Migration & Traffic Switch

Zero-downtime cutover utilizing DNS weighted routing, database CDC replication streams, and automated rollback triggers. Production cutovers take place during scheduled maintenance windows.

4

Operational Handover or Ongoing SRE Retainer

Full runbook documentation, Chaos Engineering fire drills with your internal teams, and either complete sovereign handover or ongoing 24/7 SRE monitoring backed by financial SLA guarantees.
