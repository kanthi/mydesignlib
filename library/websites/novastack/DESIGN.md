# NOVASTACK — Design System & Craft Specification

Modern enterprise IT services and digital infrastructure platform featuring an electric cobalt blue hero canvas, high-density modular domain architecture, interactive service matrix across all 10 IT disciplines, and high-assurance security telemetry.

## 1. Overview & Aesthetic Commitment

- **Brand:** Novastack (Enterprise IT Services & Autonomous Infrastructure)
- **Tagline:** Autonomous IT & Enterprise Infrastructure for the Digital Era
- **Core Narrative:** "IT & innovative tech solutions for enterprise. End-to-end cloud infrastructure, cybersecurity, AI data systems, and 24/7 managed IT operations engineered for mission-critical scale."
- **Aesthetic Direction:** High-Energy Enterprise Cobalt meets Crisp Modular White & Deep Slate Security.
  - Electric royal blue canvas (#1E40FF) with subtle geometric circuit tracing and grid coordinates.
  - High-clarity white card architecture (#FFFFFF on #F8FAFC) with elevated hover shadows and interactive domain selectors.
  - Deep obsidian slate (#0A0F1D) for high-assurance cybersecurity and FAQ consultation blocks.
  - Interactive multi-domain filtering covering all modern IT sectors.
  - Zero decorative AI-slop: real enterprise SLAs (99.99%), authentic team photography, verified compliance certifications (SOC2, ISO27001, HIPAA), and transparent scoping calculators.
