Home / Work

Case files, not logos.

Names changed. Numbers kept. These are jobs we finished and, in three cases, still run.

Healthcare · software + support

Ward 7 clinic — the board matches the floor

A 90-bed clinic ran admissions on paper after 20:00. We replaced the night board, joined it to the day system, and sat the first six weeks.

Result Door-to-room time down 41%. Two severity-1 incidents in twelve months, both closed inside the hour-one note.

Retail · software + cloud

Loom & Last — one book for three stalls

Stock lived in a spreadsheet and a till that did not speak. We built one book, moved the night job off a single machine, and trained the Saturday crew.

Result First year, the desk stopped reconciling by hand. Peak Saturday held without a second till crash.

Financial services · software + security

Saltpan Credit — a desk the examiner can sit

Member onboarding was four queues. We cut one desk, wrote the night file to the questions they already get, and put a named watch on identity.

Result 61,000 members in seven months. Counter time down a third. Audit pack produced from the live book.

Software · build + run

Holdfast Labs — first rooms, then a desk

A four-person product needed a desk customers could pay. We built the first rooms, instrumented week one, and kept the night line after launch.

Result Paid desk in eleven months. They still buy the watch. That was the deal, written down.

Consulting

Eastwick works — the cut we did not take

Six systems, pressure to “go cloud.” We recommended one cut and two deferrals. They hired us for the map, not the move.

Result The deferred work is still deferred. The one cut landed on the date in the pack.

Support

Port Sable desk — one number, two rooms

A manufacturer had three vendors and no owner at 02:00. We took the line, wrote the book, and published monthly medians.

Result Median first response 11 minutes. Escalations dropped by half in a quarter.
