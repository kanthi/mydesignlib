# Design System & Specification: Syntrix

## 1. Brand Identity & Overview
- **Reference URL:** `https://x.com/AltamashAyazz/status/2104126397274902576`
- **Original Brand:** Nexora ("Build. Automate. Scale effortlessly.")
- **Fictional Brand:** **Syntrix**
- **Tagline:** Intelligent Workflow Architecture & Autonomous Systems
- **Category:** SaaS / Developer & Productivity
- **Core Aesthetic:** Warm minimalist tactile SaaS. Fuses high-end physical architectural materials (travertine, fluted glass, polished marquina marble, brushed brass) with sleek contemporary software typography, high-contrast buttons, and soft organic foliage dapple.

## 2. Color Palette & Materiality
- **Base Background (`--bg-base`):** `#FAF9F6` — Warm porcelain sand with soft natural daylight.
- **Card Background (`--bg-surface`):** `#FFFFFF` — Crisp optical white with frosted borders.
- **Primary Text (`--text-primary`):** `#111111` — Deep obsidian carbon.
- **Secondary Text (`--text-secondary`):** `#5E5B56` — Warm neutral slate.
- **Muted Elements (`--text-muted`):** `#8C877E` — Subtle labels and metadata.
- **Emerald Growth Accent (`--accent-green`):** `#10B981` — Metrics, sparklines, and positive telemetry.
- **Warm Gold Accent (`--accent-gold`):** `#D4A359` — Ingot reflection and 5-star rating badges.
- **Borders & Dividers:** `rgba(17, 17, 17, 0.08)`.

## 3. Typography Hierarchy
- **Display Sans:** `'Plus Jakarta Sans', sans-serif` (Weights: 600, 700, 800) with tight negative tracking (`-0.035em`).
- **Editorial Accent:** `'Playfair Display', serif` or `'Cormorant Garamond', serif` (Italic, weight 400) for emotive contrast word *effortlessly.*
- **System Body:** `'Plus Jakarta Sans', sans-serif` (Weights: 400, 500) for high legibility.
- **Telemetry & Monospace:** `'JetBrains Mono', monospace` for stats, pill chips, and code parameters.

## 4. Architectural Structure
- **Navigation Bar:** Minimal pill header with chevron brandmark, dropdown anchors, login, and high-contrast black action button.
- **Hero Grid:** Split 50/50 layout. Left side anchors the conversion spine (announcement pill, typographic headline with editorial serif accent, dual CTAs, verified team social proof). Right side houses an interactive Three.js 3D sculptural installation featuring physical travertine stone, fluted glass, marquina marble, gold ingot, and floating frosted telemetry glass cards with cursor-responsive parallax.
- **Partner Marquee:** Clean monochrome logomarks for modern engineering ecosystems (Linear, Notion, Raycast, Vercel, Supabase).
- **Interactive Workflow Architecture:** Live node simulation demonstrating trigger dispatch, AI reasoning, and multi-branch execution.
- **Enterprise Features Grid:** High-precision cards highlighting automated workflows, autonomous agents, and end-to-end security.
- **Proof & Telemetry Band:** High-scale enterprise volume metrics (`10M+`, `99.99%`, `3.4x`).
- **Pricing Matrix:** Transparent, conversion-focused plans with feature checklists.
- **Footer:** Deep contextual links, status telemetry indicator, and copyright.
