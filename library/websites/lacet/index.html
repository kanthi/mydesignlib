How it works

Create, customize, and track your links in a few clicks.

01 Generate your link

Paste a site, image, or vCard. Lacet mints a unique short URL in a second — ready to share, no queue, no code.

02 Customize & brand it

Lock a slug, wrap it in your domain, and lace QR, UTM, and preview cards so every click still looks like you.

03 Share anywhere

Drop the same lace into mail, social, print, or a slide. JPG, PNG, and SVG exports sit next to the live redirect.

04 Track & optimize

Watch clicks, devices, and geos as they land. Reroute a dead dest without reprinting the QR on the crate.
