# Vortigo — Conversion Architecture & Growth Funnel Agency

**Vortigo** (`vortigo`) is a full-funnel growth engineering agency and conversion rate optimization (CRO) studio. Vortigo designs, scripts, builds, and scales paid advertising campaigns, high-converting VSLs, direct-response landing pages, and deterministic multi-touch attribution pipelines for high-velocity software, consumer apps, and venture-backed startups.

> *“Build profits while you build your business. Autonomous growth infrastructure engineered to maximize enterprise value without draining capital.”*

## 2. Typographic Hierarchy

- **Headings & Numerical Callouts**: `Plus Jakarta Sans` (800 / 700, tracking `-0.035em`, optical line-height 1.15).
- **Body & Editorial**: `Inter` (400 / 500, line-height 1.6, tracking `-0.01em`).
- **Data, Ratios & Code Tokens**: `JetBrains Mono` (500, tabular figures) for UTM parameters, attribution formulas, and financial metrics.
