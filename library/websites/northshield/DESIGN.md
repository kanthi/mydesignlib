# Northshield Insurance — Design System & Technical Spec

## 1. Brand Identity & Overview
- **Brand Name**: Northshield Insurance (`northshield.`)
- **Category**: Insurance / Regional Financial Protection
- **Tagline**: *Insurance that protects what matters most.*
- **Mission**: Providing tailored coverage made simple and savings made easy for Alaska and Pacific Northwest families, remote cabins, maritime fleets, and commercial operators.

---

## 2. Typographic Tokens
- **Display & Headings**: `Plus Jakarta Sans`, 700 / 800 weight, strong trustworthy geometric sans (`-0.035em`).
- **Body & Editorial**: `Inter`, 400 / 500 weight, high-clarity legal and financial readability.
- **Monospace Telemetry**: `JetBrains Mono`, 500 weight, uppercase policy tracking (`+0.06em`).

## 4. Key Sections & Components
1. **Header**: Northshield shield mark, navigation (`Coverage`, `Personal`, `Commercial`, `Claims`, `About`), and `Get a Quote ↗` button.
2. **Hero Section (Faithful to Reference)**:
   - Eyebrow: `Trusted Insurance Solutions for Alaska Families`.
   - Main Headline: `Insurance That Protects What Matters Most`.
   - Subhead: `Coverage made simple, savings made easy. Backed by top-rated national underwriters, engineered specifically for rugged northern living.`
   - Interactive Quote Intake Card (Auto, Home, Marine, Business + Zip code).
   - Carrier Trust Bar (A+ AM Best Rating, $4.2B claims reserve).
3. **Coverage Made Simple, Savings Made Easy**:
   - Multi-policy bundle slider with dynamic annual savings preview ($480/yr).
   - Specialized endorsements: Sub-zero freeze & pipe burst, seismic earthquake, remote cabin off-grid power, marine floatplane & skiff rider.
4. **Customer Stories / Verified Alaska Claims**:
   - Kenai Peninsula winter storm recovery, Juneau commercial fishing vessel claim.
5. **Rapid Claims Dispatch Center**:
   - 24/7 emergency dispatch helpline and mobile claims upload.
6. **FAQ Accordion & Local Agency Branches**:
   - Anchorage, Juneau, Fairbanks, and Ketchikan.
7. **Quote Request Modal**:
   - Instant multi-line quote generation.
8. **Compliance Footer**:
   - AK Insurance Department license info, disclaimers, and legal disclosures.
