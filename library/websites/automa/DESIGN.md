# Automa — Design System & Engineering Architecture

> Enterprise workflow automation platform featuring a 3D isometric cybernetic node orchestration engine, real-time telemetry, and visual DAG sandbox.

---

## 1. Brand Identity & Positioning

- **Brand Name**: Automa
- **Tagline**: Automations Unleashed
- **Positioning**: Zero-latency distributed workflow automation plane for high-throughput engineering, operations, and enterprise infrastructure teams.
- **Design Persona**: Deep Tech / High-Performance Infrastructure. Stark monochrome obsidian backdrop with high-luminance phosphorescent white neon accents, isometric architectural precision, and micro-grid spatial choreography.

---

---

## 3. The 3D Isometric Orchestration Engine

- **Projection**: True isometric projection with $30^\circ$ angular tilt and depth layering.
- **Core Hub**: Layered cylindrical disk with segmented perimeter facets, radiating concentric ground waves, and center status display (`AUTOMA CORE`).
- **Distributed Nodes**: 6 satellite nodes positioned on an isometric hex grid:
  1. `Compute & Config` (Gear / Systems)
  2. `Cloud Ingress` (Cloud + Lightning)
  3. `Webhook & Messaging` (Chat stream)
  4. `State Persistence` (Distributed Database)
  5. `Custom Webhooks` (API Gateway)
  6. `Identity & Auth` (Security Shield)
- **Kinetic Pipeline**: Continuous cubic Bezier curves carrying particle pulses at variable velocity, simulating asynchronous streaming events.
- **Micro-Interactions**: Hovering any node intensifies packet frequency, activates pedestal neon rim glow, and displays real-time execution metrics.
