# Nocturna — Design System

## Vision & Brand Identity
Nocturna is an immersive nightlife, electronic festival, and music event platform. The visual identity evokes late-night high-energy concert production with deep twilight magenta-blue gradients, glowing neon laser accents, silhouette crowd photography, and frosted glass floating badges.

## Color Palette
- Background: Gradient `linear-gradient(180deg, #070410 0%, #170724 50%, #0a0314 100%)`
- Accent Pink/Magenta: `#ff2a8d`
- Accent Cyan/Blue: `#00f0ff`
- Glass Frosted Pill: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)` and `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.25)`
- Text Primary: `#ffffff`
- Text Subtitle: `#d0c8e0`

## Typography
- Display / Headline: `Space Grotesk`, `Outfit`, sans-serif
- Body / Microcopy: `Plus Jakarta Sans`, sans-serif

## Core Layout & Components
1. **Header & Navigation**: Centered logo badge with floating frosted glass `Menu ::` pill.
2. **Hero Section**: Centered bold headline "Where The Night Comes Alive.", subtext, vertical frosted glass pill CTA button ("Explore Events ↗"). Silhouette crowd background with neon stage lighting glow.
3. **Partners Bar**: Vanguard Journal, Chronicle Daily, Cyber Pulse, Altis Metrics fictional logo bar.
4. **Event Lineup Matrix**: Filterable schedule of headlining DJs and mainstage acts.
5. **Interactive Audio Waveform Visualizer**: Live canvas playing ambient festival pulse animation.
6. **Pass Reservation Modal**: VIP Ticket booking interface.
