# Kindred Website Template — Design Prompt & Reference

Derived from the "Editorial Serif Headlines on Warm Linen" design brief (measured from the In Tandem web specification) and reimagined as a distinct fictional brand: **Kindred** (a calm, family-focused technology umbrella collective).

## Core Design Directives
- **Palette**: Neutral linen canvas (`#f9f6f2`), deep plum text & rules (`#512731`), soft butter yellow callout card (`#f7eaa5`), dusty mauve accent (`#a48c94`).
- **Typography**: Display serif (`Playfair Display` / `Cormorant Garamond`) at 110px hero, 72px H2, 47px subhead; Geometric sans (`DM Sans`) for body (20px) and kickers (31px / 0.065em letterspaced uppercase).
- **Layout Rhythm**: Generous vertical whitespace (80px - 110px padding), corner photo tiles framing the hero headline, left-bordered lead callout block with adjacent portrait tile.
- **Interactions**: Vanilla JS smooth scroll behavior, subtle hover nudges on text-links with arrows, interactive tabbed brand details, and responsive down-scaling for mobile viewports.
