# Aevum Design System & Specification

## Brand Identity & Architecture
- **Fictional Brand:** Aevum (derived from Latin *aevum* — timeless duration, eternal uninterrupted operation).
- **Tagline:** Turn complex workflows into autonomous operations.
- **Mission:** Enterprise AI autonomous operations platform that replaces fragmented human integration layers with resilient multi-agent swarms, unified API fabrics, and self-healing business workflows.
- **Reference Inspiration:** High-fidelity autonomous executive AI platform and motion preloader interaction study.
- **Category:** SaaS / Developer / Product

## Color System & Visual Atmosphere
- **Canvas & Backgrounds:**
  - Void Black: `#060709` / `#08090d` (ultra-deep matte obsidian)
  - Dark Surface / Cards: `#0e1015` / `#12151b`
  - Inset Panels / Controls: `#161922`
  - Subtle Border / Glass Strokes: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` / `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.14)`
  - Active Cyan Glow Accent: `#38bdf8` / `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.25)`
  - Active Lime / Status Green: `#10b981` / `#34d399`
- **Typography Ink:**
  - Titanium Pure White (Headings & Display): `#ffffff`
  - Slate Muted (Subheadings & Descriptions): `#8e929b`
  - Dim Slate (Labels & Micro-Kickers): `#5c606b`
  - Dark Monospace Accent: `#6b7280`
- **Atmosphere & Materials:**
  - Hero Backdrop: Atmospheric overcast storm sky with misty rolling fog over minimal dark volcanic mountain ridges, diffusing seamlessly into void black.
  - Monumental Sculpture: Floating 3D obsidian/stone mechanical aperture shutter with 6 faceted blades and cylindrical pivot bearings, with ambient lighting and subtle cursor tracking tilt.

## Motion & Full Transition Architecture
1. **Preloader Entrance & Transition**:
   - Initial pitch-black loading canvas with 6-blade aperture mark spinning smoothly in center.
   - White particle beacon orbiting the aperture perimeter.
   - Kicker `AI AUTOMATION PLATFORM` with an animated horizontal loading progress line at bottom-left.
   - Monumental display percentage counter at bottom-right interpolating from `0%` to `100%`.
   - At `100%`, cinematic aperture reveal / scale transition unveiling the atmospheric hero section.
2. **Interactive Scroll & Section Transitions**:
   - Floating pill navigation slides down into place.
   - Statement metrics (`88%`, `42 hrs`, `71%`) count up dynamically with animated vertical glowing indicator bars.
   - Interactive rotary radar dial in "The Old Way" section switches active quadrant and highlights bracketed problem cards.
   - 3x3 Isometric integration hub with chamfered glass pucks responds to 3D cursor tilt with specular glow.
   - 4-Quadrant AI Agent bento with interactive animated audio visualizer, ticket status pill, A/B toggle switch, and dynamic finance bar chart.
   - Interactive workflow template cards with bracketed trigger buttons.
   - Spring-animated FAQ accordion with rotating `+` to `×` indicators.
