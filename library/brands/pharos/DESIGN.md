# DESIGN — Pharos One

## Overview
- Brand: **Pharos** (Ambient Spatial Computing & Retinal Waveguide Hardware)
- Proposition: *Light through the slit. Reality without glass.*
- Audience: Hardware founders, optical researchers, systems engineers, and spatial computing pioneers.
- Core Emotion: Monolithic engineering, obsidian darkness, hot cadmium laser signal, precision photonics.
- Core Assets:
  - Slit Square Logomark (100u bounding square with central diffractive aperture slit)

## Typography (Custom Hardware Stack)
- **Display & Headlines**: `Space Grotesk` (weights: 500, 600, 700) — chosen for its idiosyncratic geometric cuts, optical alignment, and mathematical authority.
- **Wordmark**: `PHAROS` in uppercase `Space Grotesk 700`, tracking `0.18em` with the slit-square glyph.
- **Body & Editorial**: `Plus Jakarta Sans` (weights: 400, 500, 600, 700) — clean geometric rhythm, circular counters, exceptional long-form legibility on OLED dark fields.
- **Telemetry & Hardware Specs**: `JetBrains Mono` (weights: 400, 500, 700) — high-precision tabular numerals, fixed-width engineering telemetry, and sensor readouts.

## Interactive Hardware Experience
1. **Interactive Spatial Hotspots**: Live inspection pins placed across the 3D-lit magnesium visor prototype (`visor.jpg`) detailing the diffractive waveguide, 142g magnesium unibody, and 848nm carrier sensor.
2. **HTML5 Canvas Waveguide Simulator**: Real-time multi-ray coherent light refraction demonstrating how photons enter the central slit aperture.
3. **Spatial OS Parameter Simulator**: Live adjustable telemetry controls for photon flux emission (nits), ocular convergence depth (meters), and Bragg diffraction angle.
4. **Dynamic Slit Geometry Workbench**: Adjustable aperture slider updating SVG vector paths in real time with vector code copying.
5. **Pre-Order Dev Deck**: Direct developer kit allocation form with instant validation.
