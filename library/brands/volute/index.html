Voice

Rules

Do

  • Set the mark in paper on stone photos, or ink on paper.
  • Keep the construction drawing on a 28px grid.
  • Use the search pill only on the spiral plate.

Don’t

  • Fill the spiral or turn it into a cube cluster.
  • Add gold gradients over the hall.
  • Use a live street address or a real architect’s name.
