# Pulsiva — Brand Identity System & Specification

**Type:** Brand Identity System  
**Category:** SaaS / AI Intelligence  
**Status:** Complete deliverable  
**Reference Origin:** Rebranded lime-on-black AI identity board. Modular dot system, kinetic grids, digital textures, sense/think/move mantra, halftone signal figure, grotesk + mono pairing.

---

## 1. Brand Overview & Strategic Positioning
- **Brand Name:** **Pulsiva** (`pulsiva`)
- **Category:** `SaaS` / AI Intelligence
- **Tagline:** *"Intelligence in motion."*
- **Proposition:** *Pulsiva visualizes machine intelligence as a living signal — dots that sense, think, and move.*
- **Visual Metaphor:** **The Signal Triangle** — nineteen dots rising to a point: raw signals converging into decision.
- **Brand Archetype:** **The Magician × The Explorer** — alive, precise, always activating.

---

## 2. Color System & Semantic Tokens

| Token | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| `--color-void` | `#0A0F02` | Primary field, poster ink |
| `--color-lime` | `#C6F52E` | Activation accent, lockup, mantra |
| `--color-moss` | `#4A5A1A` | Deep texture, secondary dots |
| `--color-bone` | `#F2F4E8` | Reversed type, light panels |
| `--color-white` | `#FFFFFF` | Stark contrast |

---

## 3. Typographic Voice & Hierarchy

- **Display:** `Space Grotesk` 700 (open equivalent of the reference grotesk), uppercase mantras with `///` separators.
- **Mono:** `IBM Plex Mono` for telemetry, spec codes, Aa specimens.
- **Mantra:** `SENSE /// THINK /// MOVE` — always in that order, always lime-on-void or void-on-lime.

---

## 4. The 4-Tier Logo Stack

1. **Master Lockup:** Signal-triangle dots + `Pulsiva`.
2. **Horizontal Nav Lockup:** Compact triangle + wordmark.
3. **Symbol Glyph:** Nineteen-dot triangle.
4. **Inversions:** Lime/void · Bone/void · Void/lime · White/void.

---

## 5. Stress Tests

1. **Blur:** Triangle direction survives 20px blur.
2. **Recall:** "Dots making an arrow up" — 5-second sketch.
3. **16px:** Outer triangle holds; inner dots merge (approved).
