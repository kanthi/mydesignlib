05 — Use

Do

Keep the rounded tile, the dashed ring, the emerald outer arc, the sky inner arc, and the white center. Set the name in Plus Jakarta Sans, title case, next to the tile.

Don't

Do not swap the arc colors, close the arcs into full rings, or set the name in a script face. Clarion
