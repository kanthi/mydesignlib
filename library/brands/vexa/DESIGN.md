# Vexa — Brand Identity System & Specification

**Type:** Brand Identity System  
**Category:** SaaS / Product Intelligence  
**Status:** Complete deliverable  
**Reference Origin:** Rebranded violet-X product-intelligence board. Ultraviolet field, white and ink grounds, crossing-bars mark with signal dot, X-tiling pattern, signage / apparel / badge / app-icon applications.

---

## 1. Brand Overview & Strategic Positioning
- **Brand Name:** **Vexa** (`vexa`)
- **Category:** `SaaS` / Product Intelligence
- **Tagline:** *"The living layer of product intelligence."*
- **Proposition:** *Vexa turns everything your team knows — docs, chats, tickets, calls — into one living layer of product intelligence.*
- **Visual Metaphor:** **The Crossing Dot** — two paths cross; the dot marks where insight lands.
- **Brand Archetype:** **The Sage × The Creator** — infrastructure that feels designed.

---

## 2. Color System & Semantic Tokens

| Token | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| `--color-ultra` | `#7A4DFF` | Primary field, app icon, signage letters |
| `--color-white` | `#FFFFFF` | Lockup ground, tee, badge |
| `--color-ink` | `#141414` | Exhibition ground, wordmark ink |
| `--color-lilac` | `#C9B8FF` | Pattern tint, hover states |

---

## 3. Typographic Voice & Hierarchy

- **Display & Wordmark:** `Space Grotesk` 700, `-0.02em`. Sentence-case `Vexa` lockup.
- **Support:** `IBM Plex Mono` for swatch codes, spec labels, badge roles.

---

## 4. The 4-Tier Logo Stack

1. **Master Lockup:** Cross-dot glyph in rounded squircle + `Vexa`.
2. **App Icon:** White glyph on ultraviolet squircle.
3. **Symbol Glyph:** Standalone crossing bars + dot.
4. **Inversions:** White/ultra · Ultra/white · White/ink · Ink/lilac.

---

## 5. Stress Tests

1. **Blur:** X-cross survives 20px blur; dot reads as aperture.
2. **Recall:** "An X with a dot" — 5-second sketch.
3. **16px:** Bars hold at 16px; dot merges below 24px (approved).

---

## 6. Touchpoints

1. **Office signage** — dimensional ultra letters on concrete.
2. **Swatch columns** — ultra / white / ink with RGB codes.
3. **X-tiling pattern** — lilac-on-ultra repeat.
4. **Lockup sheet** — icon, wordmark, app icon, X→Ẋ evolution.
5. **Apparel & badge** — tee chest print, lanyard ID (fictional Mira Osei).
6. **Home screen** — app icon among generic glyphs.
