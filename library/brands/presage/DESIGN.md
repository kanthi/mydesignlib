# Presage — Brand Identity System & Specification

**Type:** Brand Identity System  
**Category:** SaaS / Analytics Intelligence  
**Status:** Complete deliverable  
**Reference Origin:** Rebranded dark analytics-identity board. Characterized by ember orange on coal black, ice-blue gradient fields, a five-ray dawnburst mark over a dotted datawave, dot-asterisk pattern device, and transit-poster / night-billboard applications.

---

## 1. Brand Overview & Strategic Positioning
- **Brand Name:** **Presage** (`presage`)
- **Category:** `SaaS` / Analytics Intelligence
- **Tagline:** *"Numbers that speak first."*
- **Proposition:** *Presage is analytics that reads the dashboard for you — catching problems and opportunities before they turn into a stat in a report.*
- **Visual Metaphor:** **The Dawnburst Wave** — five rays of first light over a datastream of dots: the signal, breaking before sunrise.
- **Brand Archetype:** **The Sage × The Jester** — serious machinery, irreverent voice ("Join the waitlist / Stay guessing").

---

## 2. Color System & Semantic Tokens

| Token | Hex Value | Semantic Role | Usage & Guidelines |
|---|---|---|---|
| `--color-ember` | `#E8441B` | Signal Flare | Primary brand color, CTAs, app icon, gradient wedge |
| `--color-coal` | `#141414` | Night Ledger | Dark exhibition field, poster ink, billboard night |
| `--color-ice` | `#DCE4EC` | Morning Field | Light hero ground, gradient base, reversed mark ground |
| `--color-cream` | `#F7F3EC` | Paper Warmth | Editorial surfaces, billboard headline ground |
| `--color-steel` | `#4A5560` | Instrument Grey | Secondary text, blueprint annotations |
| `--color-white` | `#FFFFFF` | Stark Contrast | Master mark on coal and ember |

**Dawn gradient:** `120deg, #DCE4EC → #F7F3EC 45% → #E8441B 130%` — ember always breaks from lower-right.

---

## 3. Typographic Voice & Hierarchy

- **Display & Wordmark:** `Space Grotesk`, 500–700, `-0.02em`. Plain-spoken confidence for headlines and the `Presage` lockup.
- **Editorial Serif:** `Instrument Serif` italic for pull-lines and poster subheads.
- **Telemetry & Meta:** `IBM Plex Mono`, uppercase micro-labels, tabular numerals for the datawave annotations.

---

## 4. The 4-Tier Logo Stack

1. **Master Lockup:** Dawnburst mark + `Presage` wordmark.
2. **Horizontal Nav Lockup:** Compact mark + wordmark at 26px cap-height.
3. **Symbol Glyph:** Standalone dawnburst — 5 rays, bar, 5-dot wave.
4. **Adaptive Inversions:**
   - White mark on Coal `#141414`
   - Coal mark on Ice `#DCE4EC`
   - White mark on Ember `#E8441B`
   - Ember mark on Cream `#F7F3EC`

---

## 5. The 3-Step Logo Stress Test Suite

1. **Blur Test:** At 20px blur, rays-plus-bar read as a rising sun over signal dots.
2. **5-Second Recall:** One hook — a sunrise with five rays sitting on dots.
3. **16-Pixel Test:** 9-unit ray strokes and 5.5-unit dots survive favicon scale; wave merges to a dotted smile.

---

## 6. The 5 Core Identity Touchpoints

1. **Hero Field:** Ice-to-cream ground with ember wedge, coal master lockup.
2. **App Icons:** Ember / Coal / Steel-rounded tiles with white glyph.
3. **Statement Panels:** Coal fields — *"Not another dashboard. The reader of dashboards."*
4. **Transit Posters:** Backlit shelter trio — statements left and right, mark poster center.
5. **Night Billboard:** Cream sheet on coal — *"Know before it shows in the numbers."* with watcher silhouette.
