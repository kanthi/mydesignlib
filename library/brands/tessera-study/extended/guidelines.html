Clearspace

Keep one tile-block of empty field on every side. Minimum symbol size is 16px. The word does not go under 120px wide. Order stays gold, pale, olive, amber. The tiles stay square. They do not grow a gap or a radius.

05 — Use

Do

Keep the four tiles in order and set the name in Outfit, uppercase, with open tracking, beside them. Claims use Playfair Display.

Don't

Do not round the tiles, swap their order, or set the lockup in the serif. Tessera
