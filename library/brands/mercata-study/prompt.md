# Mercata study

Practice study of the Mercata page in `library/websites/mercata/`. The replica keeps that page. The extended system redraws the fold mark.

Catalog date stays the source page date, 2026-10-07.

## Palette
- Ink `#111827`
- Paper `#FFFFFF`
- Accent `#FF5024`
- Ember `#FF6B44`
- Night `#0E0D0C`

The tile runs ember to `#DF3911`. Small text on paper uses `#9A3412`.

## Type
Plus Jakarta Sans, weight 800, tracking −0.04em. The name on the page is lowercase mercata. JetBrains Mono is the interface mono.

## Deliverables
Hub, site replica, compare.png, guidelines, landing, social kit, merch, email signature, logo reveal.

Contact is demo@mercata.example. Stationery is the commerce desk.
