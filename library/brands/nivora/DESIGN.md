# Design System & Brand Identity Specification: Nivora

## 1. Brand Overview & Rebrand Blueprint
- **Origin Reference:** Modern geometric corporate identity specimen & asymmetric bento system
- **Brand Identity:** **NIVORA** (`nivora™`)
- **Positioning:** Enterprise software engineering, distributed cloud infrastructure, and computational intelligence services. Architectural precision, high-contrast chromatic energy, and dual-arc portal geometry.
- **Tagline:** *"Intelligent Software & Distributed Systems"*
- **Category:** `Services` (Type: `brand`)

---

## 2. Chromatic System & Tokens
- **Substrate Abyssal Spruce:** `#042627` (Deepest forest green/slate container background)
- **Deep Spruce Card:** `#083234` (Surface elevated card background)
- **Electric Chartreuse Lime:** `#D8F84C` (High-visibility focal accent and interactive primary)
- **Dark Spruce Typography:** `#062C2D` (Primary text on lime surfaces)
- **Crisp Bone White:** `#F5F8F5` (High-contrast text on spruce surfaces)
- **Muted Sage:** `#7E9F94` (Secondary technical metadata, borders, construction grids)
- **Border Subtle Spruce:** `rgba(216, 248, 76, 0.14)`

---

## 3. Shape Psychology & Logo Mark Anatomy
- **Geometry:** Dual-Arc Aperture Portal / Forward-Leaning Computational Lens.
- **Construction Logic:**
  1. **Left Curved Quadrant:** Solid convex quadrant rising from $y=100$ to $y=16$, representing bedrock stability and structural engineering foundation.
  2. **Right Orbital Arch:** Curved concentric aperture segment sweeping from $y=16$ to $y=108$, embodying acceleration, cloud routing, and dynamic data flow.
  3. **Channel Aperture Gap:** Calibrated $6\text{px}$ diagonal negative-space channel separating the two forms, maintaining optical legibility under extreme reduction.
  4. **Nucleus Accent Dot:** Subtle circular accent representing node synchronization and data packets.

---

## 4. Typography Hierarchy
- **Wordmark & Display Headings:** `Plus Jakarta Sans`, 800 ExtraBold, letter-spacing `-1.5px`.
- **Primary Body Copy:** `Inter`, 400 Regular / 500 Medium.
- **Technical Metrics & Latency Readouts:** `JetBrains Mono`, 500 Medium, tabular lining numbers.

---

## 5. Deliverable Structure & 7-Tile Bento Grid
- **Tile 1 (Totem Signage):** Vertical brand totem in deep spruce on chartreuse backdrop with vertical wordmark and base mark.
- **Tile 2 (OS Dock & System Icon):** Apple iOS dock with centered chartreuse squircle app icon.
- **Tile 3 (In-Situ Device Hardware):** Matte black smartphone on organic textured slate showcasing mobile interface.
- **Tile 4 (Hero Specimen Card):** Interactive chartreuse master hero with SVG code copy and high-res vector export.
- **Tile 5 (Portrait Collateral 1):** Executive portrait on geometric arc grid lines with enterprise capability messaging.
- **Tile 6 (Edge-Painted Duplex Stationery):** Multi-layered 600gsm business cards with chartreuse edge painting and foil-stamped geometry.
- **Tile 7 (Portrait Collateral 2):** Applied engineering specimen with resource efficiency editorial copy.
- **Interactive 3-Step Stress Suite:** Optical blur slider ($0-24\text{px}$), multi-scale rendering matrix ($128\text{px} \to 16\text{px}$), and substrate inversion toggle.
