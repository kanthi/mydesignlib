FAQ

Everything you need to know

Have questions? We're here to help you accelerate your client acquisition pipeline.

Is this better than writing proposals manually for clients?

Yes. Manual proposals take hours and often lack structure. Pactum AI generates professionally structured, persuasive proposals in seconds helping you save time and increase your close rate.

Does it work for different industries and business types?

Absolutely. Whether you provide software engineering, design, marketing retainers, architectural consulting, or sales advisory, our AI adapts to your niche deliverables and industry benchmarks.

Will AI make my proposals sound generic or impersonal?

No. Pactum incorporates your custom agency tone of voice, past client case studies, and exact project parameters so every proposal reads like a bespoke strategy deck.

Can I use my own pricing structure and packages?

Yes. You can configure fixed milestones, recurring retainers, tiered pricing models (Good/Better/Best), and hourly caps directly within your proposal builder.

Can I track when clients open and view my proposal?

Yes. You receive instant notifications when clients view your link, how much time they spend on each section, and when they forward it internally to decision-makers.

Is my client data safe, fully secure, confidential, and well-protected?

Yes. All client data and briefs are encrypted in transit and at rest with SOC-2 compliant infrastructure. Your proprietary project data is never used to train public foundation models.
