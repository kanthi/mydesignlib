# DESIGN — Plinth

## Overview
- **Audience**: Architectural practices, structural engineering ateliers, cultural institutions, and spatial design consultancies seeking a permanent, monolithic identity grounded in material honesty.
- **Single CTA**: Download Brand Assets & Vector Guidelines
- **Emotion**: Monolithic, tectonic, silent, eternal — morning raking light across board-formed cast concrete, deep slate stone, and a cantilevered geometric emblem.
- **Create this feeling through**:
  - Warm raw concrete (`#D6D1C7`) and deep slate (`#1E232A`) as foundational grounds.
  - A precision monolithic geometric A-frame mark with a cantilevered crossbeam.
  - Tactile physical materiality (heavy cotton paper, Welsh slate, brass datum plates, board-formed concrete).
  - Rigorous 4-tier responsive logo stack and complete 3-step stress test suite.

## Source of truth
- This file + CSS `:root` in `index.html`. Code takes precedence if divergent.

## Aesthetic commitment
- **Name**: Brutalist Architectural Monolith — Slate & Raw Concrete
- **Signature element**: Monolithic Cantilever Glyph + Spaced Monumental Wordmark (`P L I N T H`, tracking `0.28em`).

## Colors (semantic)
- `surface-obsidian`: `#14171D` — Deepest shadow field, dark mode master background.
- `surface-slate`: `#242B35` — Structural stone accent, secondary badge ground.
- `surface-concrete`: `#9EA3A8` — Mid-tone board-formed concrete.
- `surface-limestone`: `#DCD7CE` — Warm mineral surface.
- `surface-paper`: `#F4F0E8` — Tactile stationery cotton stock, light mode ground.
- `accent-brass`: `#B59560` — Architectural site marker and metal inlay.
- `hairline-grid`: `rgba(44, 51, 61, 0.14)` — Architectural drafting guidelines.
- `text-primary`: `#14171D` — High-contrast structural ink.
- `text-secondary`: `#555C66` — Specification and technical metadata.
- `text-inverse`: `#F4F0E8` — Crisp off-white on obsidian and slate.

## Typography
- **Wordmark / Display**: `Outfit` 700 / `Plus Jakarta Sans` 700, uppercase, letter-spacing `0.28em`.
- **Headings**: `Outfit` 600, letter-spacing `-0.025em`.
- **Technical Specs / Code**: `IBM Plex Mono` 400–500, tabular figures.
- **Editorial / Body**: `Outfit` 400, line-height 1.6.

## Responsive Logo Stack
1. **Primary Master Lockup**: Full vertical stacked lockup (Monolithic mark + wordmark + atelier subtitle).
2. **Secondary Horizontal Lockup**: Compact inline lockup for architectural title blocks and digital headers.
3. **Symbol / Monogram Glyph**: Standalone geometric silhouette for social avatars, site pins, and site signage.
4. **Micro-Scale Favicon (16px)**: Optically compensated heavy-weight glyph eliminating thin line collapse.

## Ban list
- Gradient rainbow slop, rounded bubble shapes, generic clip-art houses/compasses, commercial Inter typography without tracking, decorative drop-shadow clutter.
