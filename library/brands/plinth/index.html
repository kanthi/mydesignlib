01 // Architectural Geometry

Mathematical Blueprint & Vector Anatomy

Constructed along rigid 68° tectonic thrust lines with an asymmetrical 22° cantilever shear. Every vector terminal aligns with modular architectural grid divisions.

01 // Tectonic Thrust (68°)

The primary legs rise at an acute 68° angle, evoking the towering structural trusses of cathedral buttresses and monolithic cast-in-place retaining walls.

02 // The Cantilever Crossbeam (22°)

Instead of a static horizontal tie, the crossbeam shears at 22°, projecting outward like an architectural brise-soleil or cantilevered sky terrace.

03 // 1.5× Clear-Space Boundary

The exclusion zone around the mark is pegged to 1.5× the crossbeam thickness. No foreign typographic or graphical elements may invade this perimeter.
