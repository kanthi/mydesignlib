# Reyla — Brand Design System

## Brand Identity Overview
Reyla is a premier digital design studio and web architecture brand built around structural excellence, layer-by-layer craftsmanship, and fiery chromatic warmth. Grounded in a deep burgundy canvas, the brand leverages luminous vermilion fire gradients and crisp icy blue highlights to convey technological refinement and tactile craft.

- **Brand Name**: Reyla
- **Category**: Studio / Creative Agency / Digital Architecture
- **Origin Reference**: Fictional rebrand inspired by a contemporary creative director delivery deck study.

## Visual Language & Geometry

### 1. The Modular Stepped-Tier Emblem
The Reyla mark is constructed on a 3-tier modular grid representing architectural layers:
- **Element 1 (Top Inverted L)**: Top horizontal arm with right-side vertical step, establishing an outer framing shelter.
- **Element 2 (Middle Horizontal Bar)**: Clean horizontal tier tucked inside the left channel.
- **Element 3 (Bottom-Right Anchor Square)**: Offset foundation unit establishing diagonal dynamic equilibrium and grounding the mark.
- **Negative Space Channel**: A uniform 10% negative space channel runs horizontally between tiers and drops vertically along the center axis, creating an unmistakable stepped silhouette at both 16px favicon and large-format billboard scale.

### 2. Chromatic System
- **Deep Burgundy (`#24070B`)**: Rich, ultra-deep mahogany backdrop providing organic warmth compared to generic obsidian.
- **Vermilion Fire (`#E64415`)**: High-energy primary brand accent and directional focal point.
- **Flame Bright (`#FA6B38`)**: Radiating highlight, gradient stepping, and interactive hover feedback.
- **Ice Sky Tint (`#E8F0FE`)**: Crisp optical contrast countering warm combustion gradients.
- **Neutral Studio Canvas (`#EDECE8`)**: Editorial presentation card substrate.

### 3. Typographic Hierarchy
- **Display & Wordmark**: `Plus Jakarta Sans` (Weights: 600, 700, 800) — Precision geometric grotesk with tight letter-spacing (`-0.04em`) providing contemporary confidence.
- **Interface & Body**: `Inter` (Weights: 400, 500, 600) — High-legibility neutral sans for dense presentation slides and editorial descriptions.
- **Technical Telemetry**: `JetBrains Mono` (Weights: 400, 500, 600) — Palette hex readouts, project metadata, and service tags.
