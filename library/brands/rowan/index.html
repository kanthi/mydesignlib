Geometry & Drafting // Specimen 03

5×5 Modular Ribbon Construction

Mathematical anatomy of the interlocking Ri monogram, built on dual circular centers C1 (1x, 2.5x) and C2 (4x, 2.5x) with concentric radii R=1.5x and R=2.5x.

2-Fold Rotational Equilibrium

The Rowan mark resolves the inherent tension between glyph 'R' and glyph 'i' by rotating the upper shoulder arc 180° around the center node (2.5x, 2.5x) to construct the lower leg and foundation of the lowercase letterform.

Component Grid Range Mathematical Rule
Stem 'R' x: [0..1], y: [0..5] Full-height vertical pillar (1.0x width)
Upper Arch C1 = (1x, 2.5x) Concentric circular arcs (R=1.5x & R=2.5x)
Lower Ribbon C2 = (4x, 2.5x) 180° point reflection of upper arch
Floating Tittle x: [4..5], y: [0..1] Exact 1x × 1x modular square dot
