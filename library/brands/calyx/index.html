Photography & art direction

Aurora is the only color field. People are black-and-white, brim-shadowed, still. Signs live in real rooms with hard sun.

Do’s and don’ts

One mark, three lockups, the aurora as photography. Everything else is noise.

Do

  • Use the cup mark in lime on navy, white on aurora, navy on paper.
  • Keep the wordmark lowercase and optically tight.
  • Let teal/sea dominate charts; lime is one bar.
  • Photograph signs in rooms with a single hard light.

Don’t

  • Rebuild the mark as an interlocking S or a leaf.
  • Rotate, outline-shadow, or set the mark in mid-grey.
  • Fill a page with lime or a CSS rainbow mesh.
  • Set body copy in lime, or drop Inter on the wordmark.
