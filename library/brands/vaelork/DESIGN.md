# DESIGN — Vaelork

## Overview
- **Audience**: Institutional investors, sovereign tech enterprises, and systems architects seeking an uncompromising visual language for precision infrastructure.
- **Single CTA**: Download Brand Guidelines & Specimen Assets
- **Emotion**: Monolithic, structural, mathematically rigorous — an architectural deep navy field, warm ivory stationery, and an interlocking geometric monogram that commands trust.
- **Create this feeling through**:
  - Deep architectural navy (`#0A3366`) as a dominant grounded field.
  - Warm antique ivory paper (`#F8F3E6`) for physical tactile collateral.
  - One faceted interlocking V monogram constructed on an isometric matrix.
  - 8-specimen identity board matching physical and digital touchpoints (construction matrix, app icons, business cards, browser UI, 3-pillar brand story cards, hang tags, and mobile app in hand).

## Source of truth
- This file + CSS `:root` in `index.html`. Prefer code if they diverge.

## Aesthetic commitment
- **Name**: Architectural Monolith — Deep Navy & Ivory
- **Signature element**: Interlocking faceted V mark + spaced geometric sans wordmark (`V A E L O R K`, tracking `0.28em`).

## Colors (semantic)
- `surface-navy`: `#0A3366` — Primary monolithic brand field, primary card, lockup ground.
- `surface-navy-dark`: `#061F3E` — Deep contrast ground, mobile mockup backdrop.
- `surface-ivory`: `#F8F3E6` — Warm stationery paper, card back, construction matrix ground.
- `surface-page`: `#F3EFE6` — Page background ground.
- `surface-card`: `#FFFFFF` — Clean white surfaces.
- `hairline-grid`: `#5B82A6` — Construction blueprint guidelines on ivory.
- `text-primary`: `#0A1C30` — Primary text on light fields.
- `text-secondary`: `#4A5E75` — Secondary descriptive copy.
- `text-on-navy`: `#FFFFFF` — Crisp white typography on navy fields.
- `action-primary`: `#0A3366` — Primary interactive buttons.
- `action-hover`: `#062144` — Hover state.

## Typography
- **Wordmark / Display**: `Plus Jakarta Sans` / `Outfit` 700, uppercase, letter-spacing `0.28em`.
- **Headings**: `Plus Jakarta Sans` 800, tight letter-spacing `-0.035em`.
- **Spec / Code**: `IBM Plex Mono` 400–500, tabular numeric figures.
- **Body**: `Plus Jakarta Sans` 400–500, line-height 1.6.

## Space & Board Layout
- **Container**: `min(1240px, 100% - 2.5rem)`
- **Identity Board**: 2 columns × 4 rows (8 distinct specimen tiles):
  1. *Tile 1 (Top Left)*: Solid Navy Monogram + Spaced Wordmark Lockup.
  2. *Tile 2 (Top Right)*: Warm Ivory Isometric Construction Matrix & Blueprint Grid.
  3. *Tile 3 (Row 2 Left)*: Dual App Icon Specimens (Solid Navy & Ivory Line).
  4. *Tile 4 (Row 2 Right)*: Slanted Business Card Pair (Navy front + Ivory QR back).
  5. *Tile 5 (Row 3 Left)*: Browser Window UI Specimen (`🔒 www.vaelork.com`).
  6. *Tile 6 (Row 3 Right)*: 3-Pillar Brand Story Cards (`01 Scale Infinitely`, `02 Innovate Permissionlessly`, `03 Interoperate Seamlessly`).
  7. *Tile 7 (Row 4 Left)*: Die-Cut Hang Tag with Embossed Micro-Monogram Grid.
  8. *Tile 8 (Row 4 Right)*: Smartphone in Hand displaying glowing brand screen.

## Ban list
- Purple gradients, decorative drop shadows on identity tiles, comic fonts, copying original trademark names.
