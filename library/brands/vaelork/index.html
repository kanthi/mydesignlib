01 · Construction Geometry

Isometric Mark Anatomy

The Vaelork monogram is generated from interlocking isometric planes. The mark combines a diagonal primary stroke with a faceted diamond facet, creating dimensional authority without relying on faux-3D gradients.

Matrix Specifications

Base Aspect Ratio 1 : 1 (Square Enclosure)
Primary Stroke Angle 60.0° (Isometric Normal)
Facet Return Angle 120.0° Counter-Plane
Minimum Clear Space 1X Cap-Height Unit
Digital Minimum Size 16px × 16px (Favicon Scale)
Print Minimum Size 6.0mm Width

Clear Space & Protection Zone

No graphical elements, typographic locks, or boundary rules may enter the perimeter defined by the X-height dimension surrounding the monogram.

03 · Typography

Type Hierarchy & Wordmark

Display titles use Plus Jakarta Sans with refined kerning and structural boldness, while technical documentation utilizes IBM Plex Mono.

Display Wordmark
V A E L O R K

Uppercase Plus Jakarta Sans 700 with wide letter-spacing (+0.28em). Used strictly for master lockups, stationery headers, and signages.
