**Type:** Brand Identity System  
**Category:** Developer / Robotics & Deep Computing

---

## 1. Brand Overview & Proposition
- **Brand Name:** **Vektor Systems** (`vektor`)
- **Category:** `Developer` / Next-Generation Modular Computing & Cybernetics
- **Proposition:** *Constructivist kinetic hardware systems engineered for high-throughput autonomous intelligence.*
- **Visual Metaphor:** The Dynamic Chevron Monogram — sharp 45-degree parallel geometry fusing the letterforms 'V' and 'K' into an arrow of forward velocity and structural integrity.
- **Tone & Persona:** Precise, unapologetic, hyper-functional, high-contrast, kinetic.

## 3. The 3-Step Logo Stress Test Suite

1. **Favicon & Micro-Scale (16px / 24px):**
   - Solid silhouette retains bold angular recognition without blurred lines.
2. **Monochrome Inversion:**
   - 100% vector stroke fills ensure seamless reproduction in pure black, stark white, or laser-etched metal without grayscale artifacts.
3. **Complex Tactile Substrates:**
   - Tested across 3D acrylic wall relief, micro-textured business card paper, silk/polyester fabric drapes, and heavy cotton canvas bags.
