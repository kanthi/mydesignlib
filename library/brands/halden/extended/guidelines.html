Halden · Desk objects

Guidelines

A shallow tray: a thick rounded bar with a small lip at each end. The mark is not a letter. Use it in currentColor, with HALDEN set in Inter Tight.

Clearspace and minimum size

Clearspace on every side equals the height of one lip. Do not set type or another mark inside that margin.

Minimum

18px tall

The symbol does not go under 18px in height. Below that, use the wordmark alone.

Imagery

Photographs use pale seamless grounds, walnut, paper, sage, and amber glass. One object per frame when the picture is a product. No type, marks, or watermarks inside the photograph. Lifestyle crops may be round or softly squared. Banners use an 18px radius and sit inset from the page edge.

Do and don’t

Do
  • Keep the tray mark in one color.
  • Use hairline rules instead of shadows on the product grid.
  • Put sale prices in the orange chip with a struck previous price.
  • Leave the page #F6F6F6 and the cards white.
Don’t
  • Redraw the mark as a letter or as two circles.
  • Stretch the tray or fill it with a pattern.
  • Write a paragraph in white on the sale chip.
  • Drop the mark on a busy photograph without a solid panel.
