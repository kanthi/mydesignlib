A FEW MOTION NOTES

Keep the feeling.
Lose the noise.

Good motion is part of the interface’s language. It gives a hint, shows a change, and gets out of the way when the job is done.

01 / INTENT

Give every move a reason.

Use movement to show hierarchy, state, continuity, or feedback. If it does not help the person using the page, it can stay still.

02 / RHYTHM

Keep the timing human.

Short responses feel direct. Longer transitions need room to unfold. Let the distance and importance guide the duration.

03 / ACCESS

Respect the reduced setting.

Keep the meaning when motion is reduced. Offer a clear still state and avoid making animation the only way to understand a change.
