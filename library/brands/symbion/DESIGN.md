# Symbion — Brand Identity System & Specification

Comprehensive brand identity specification for **Symbion**, a bio-synthetic AI research laboratory and neural intelligence platform. Symbion bridges computational deep learning with organic biological intelligence, represented by a four-pointed concave bio-spark nexus.

---

## 1. Brand Core & Aesthetic Philosophy

- **Name**: Symbion (Symbol: Bio-Nexus Spark `◆`)
- **Category**: Bio-Synthetic Intelligence & Neural Computing
- **Core Tagline**: *Where Biological Intelligence Meets Artificial Cognition*
- **Visual Personality**: Pristine, authoritative, bio-luminescent, minimalist, ultra-high-end.
- **Palette Essence**: High-contrast juxtaposition of deep nocturnal carbon `#090c0a` with radiant bio-emerald `#00d26a` / `#10b981`, tactile sage `#e6f4ea`, and stark optical white.

## 3. Logo Mark Construction & Geometry

- **The Bio-Nexus Spark**: Constructed on a precise 1:1 square grid. Four concave arcs intersecting at 90-degree orthogonal axes, evoking both a biological leaf structure and an optical starburst.
- **Clearspace**: Minimum isolation zone equal to 100% of the emblem's central radius ($1\times$).
- **Minimum Digital Reproduction**: 16px (favicon / watchface status), 8mm physical print.
- **Variants**:
  1. `Primary Full`: Bio-Nexus Spark + Titlecase Wordmark `Symbion`
  2. `Monochrome Dark`: White emblem on Carbon `#0A0E0C`
  3. `Monochrome Light`: Carbon emblem on Pure White
  4. `Vibrant Emerald`: White knockout emblem inside rounded square `#00D26A`

## 4. Physical & Digital Touchpoint Touchpoints

1. **Stationery & Hardcover Monograph**: Deep charcoal linen with foil-stamped bio-spark and technical telemetry text (`symbion.ai`).
2. **Wayfinding Cubic Signage**: Matte powder-coated emerald cube with precision-milled white acrylic emblem inset.
3. **Apparel & Headwear**: Structured heavyweight cotton cap with clean 3D embroidered emblem.
4. **Polycarbonate Access Badge**: Translucent polycarbonate frame with duotone emerald security portrait and magnetic lanyard clasp.
5. **Architectural Corridor Poster**: Deep black backlit poster with radiant volumetric green spark gradient.
6. **Smartwatch Complication**: High-contrast OLED dial with animated bio-frequency complication.

## 5. Anti-Slop Directives

- ❌ No generic stock logos or unrefined bezier curves.
- ❌ No harsh fluorescent neon without deep balancing pine shadows.
- ❌ All typography must adhere to clean geometric sans-serif tracking rules.
