# Sonarium — Living Brand Identity System Specification

## 1. Brand Essence & Cultural Positioning
- **Institution Name**: Sonarium Centre de Musique (`SONARIUM`)
- **Location**: Historic 13th-Century Infirmary & Music Hall, Ghent, Belgium
- **Category**: Cultural Institution / Philharmonic & Experimental Music Centre
- **Brand Positioning**: *Alive in every moment. Past, present and future.*
- **Design Philosophy**: Shifting away from historic architectural clichés and rigid musical iconography (treble clefs, 5-line staffs) toward a generative representation of music as an organic, living acoustic wave.

---

## 2. The Living Mark: John Cage Notation & Oscilloscope
- **Origin & Conceptual Precedent**:
  - Unconventional graphic score notation pioneered by American composer **John Cage** (e.g. *Fontana Mix*, *Aria*).
  - Physical audio waveform tracing produced by an analogue **oscilloscope**.
- **Generative System Behavior**:
  - The swooping ribbon adapts its amplitude, harmonic density, and line velocity across musical repertoires:
    - **Baroque & Chamber**: Balanced, rhythmic, ornamental curves.
    - **Contemporary Avant-Garde**: Indeterminate, angular, sudden dynamic glissandos.
    - **Modern Jazz**: Syncopated polyrhythmic loops.
    - **Electronic & Spatial**: Dense high-frequency oscillations.

---

## 3. Typographic System
- **Display & Identity Headline**: Neo-grotesque `Forma DJR` / `Plus Jakarta Sans`, 700 / 800 weight. Plain, unadorned, modern, and democratic to balance the expressive fluid gesture of the mark.
- **Body & Programme Notes**: `Inter`, 400 / 500 weight, high legibility across printed concert programmes.
- **Season Codes & Performance Telemetry**: `JetBrains Mono`, 500 weight, uppercase tracking (`+0.12em`).

---

## 5. Applications & Environmental Wayfinding
- **Concert Posters**: Bold color fields contrasting monolithic typography against expressive generative waves.
- **Acoustic Hall Signage**: Cast patinated bronze and laser-cut timber wall plaques honoring the Gothic infirmary architecture.
- **Embossed Foil Tickets**: Heavy cotton paper stock with hot-stamped holographic soundwave ribbons.
