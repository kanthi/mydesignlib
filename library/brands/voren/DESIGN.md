# Voren — Brand Identity System & Specification

**Type:** Brand Identity System  
**Category:** Fintech / Open Financial Infrastructure  
**Status:** Complete deliverable  
**Reference Origin:** Rebranded brutalist noise-and-ink finance board. Burnt orange field, near-black ink, giant grotesk wordmark with hatched fills, pixel-staircase peak mark, corner-arrow furniture, mono microcopy, line-mountain illustrations, and altitude statistics.

---

## 1. Brand Overview & Strategic Positioning
- **Brand Name:** **Voren** (`voren`) — Voren Team, est. 1990
- **Category:** `Fintech` / Open Financial Infrastructure
- **Tagline:** *"Rise above."*
- **Proposition:** *Voren stitches together an open financial system where trust is compiled, not promised — transparent rails that belong to everyone.*
- **Visual Metaphor:** **The Pixel Ascent** — a staircase of pixels climbing to a signal dot: finance built block by block, toward daylight.
- **Brand Archetype:** **The Rebel × The Sage** — dismantles old banking behind a wall of rigorous type.

---

## 2. Color System & Semantic Tokens

| Token | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| `--color-ember` | `#D6481F` | Primary field, altitude numerals, arrow device |
| `--color-ink` | `#1E2120` | Wordmark ink, dark panels, mountain lines |
| `--color-bone` | `#E8E4DA` | Microcopy ground, poster paper |
| `--color-rust` | `#8E2F12` | Hatch fills, deep shade |

Grain: SVG turbulence overlay at 8–12% on all fields — noise is mandatory, never decoration-free.

---

## 3. Typographic Voice & Hierarchy

- **Display:** `Archivo` Black 900, uppercase, tight. Giant wordmarks, altitude numerals, RISE ABOVE band.
- **Microcopy:** `IBM Plex Mono`, lowercase or small caps, 10–12px. Manifestos, coordinates, specs.
- **Hatch device:** 45° 2px line fills inside display letterforms on dark grounds.

---

## 4. The 4-Tier Logo Stack

1. **Master Lockup:** Pixel-peak mark + `VOREN` grotesk caps.
2. **Giant Wordmark:** Standalone `VOREN` with hatched lower third.
3. **Symbol Glyph:** Pixel staircase + signal dot.
4. **Inversions:** Ink on ember · Bone on ink · Ember on bone · Rust hatch on ink.

---

## 5. Stress Tests

1. **Blur:** Staircase silhouette survives 20px blur; dot merges into peak.
2. **Recall:** "Stairs going up to a dot" — sketchable in 5 seconds.
3. **16px:** 16-unit modules hold; dot drops below 24px (approved simplification).

---

## 6. Touchpoints

1. **Hero field** — ember, giant hatched VOREN, corner arrows, TEAM / EST. 1990.
2. **Arrow panel** — ink ground, ember wayfinder curve, code-manifesto.
3. **Mountain plates** — line illustrations, moon/sun discs, RISE ABOVE band.
4. **Altitude stat** — 4120M numeral plate.
5. **Stationery** — bone card, mono manifesto, pixel seal.
