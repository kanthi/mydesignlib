# Brand Identity Specification: NutraPulse

## 1. Brand Overview & Positioning
- **Origin Reference:** Computational AI metabolic nutrition identity study
- **Brand Identity:** **NutraPulse** (`nutrapulse`)
- **Category:** `Wellness` / `Product` (Type: `brand`)
- **Tagline:** *"Metabolic Intelligence. Precision Vitality."*
- **Positioning:** Investor-grade AI nutrition, metabolic tracking, and cellular health platform combining bio-computational precision with organic vitality.
- **Brand Personality:** Organic yet hyper-modern, vital, clinical-grade trustworthy, kinetic, and minimal.

---

## 2. Chromatic Identity & Tokens
- **Deep Spruce Substrate (`--color-spruce`):** `#0C1811` (Primary dark canvas)
- **Raised Forest (`--color-forest-surface`):** `#14241B` (Raised cards and UI panels)
- **Electric Chlorophyll (`--color-lime`):** `#8EE620` (Signature primary mark and energetic accent)
- **Metabolic Azure (`--color-azure`):** `#2F80ED` (Secondary clinical intelligence accent)
- **Soft Celery Cream (`--color-celery`):** `#E4F7C8` (Warm organic highlight and light mode canvas)
- **Matcha Canvas Light (`--color-linen`):** `#F4F8EE` (Light mode substrate)
- **Pure White (`--color-white`):** `#FFFFFF` (High-contrast typography)
- **Muted Sage (`--color-muted`):** `#7D9183` (Secondary metadata and guidelines)
- **Subtle Border (`--border-subtle`):** `rgba(142, 230, 32, 0.14)`

---

## 3. Typography & Hierarchy
- **Primary Wordmark & Display:** `'Plus Jakarta Sans'`, 800 ExtraBold, circular terminals with `-0.03em` tracking.
- **Body & Editorial Copy:** `'Inter'`, 400 Regular / 500 Medium.
- **Data & Nutritional Telemetry:** `'JetBrains Mono'`, 500 Medium for macro-nutrient readouts and code tokens.

---

## 4. Logo Mark Architecture
- **Concept:** Rotational double leaf-helix "N" loop.
- **Symbolism:**
  - Continuous metabolic energy loop (180° rotational symmetry).
  - Dual botanical leaves representing whole-food organic nutrition.
  - Dynamic "N" initial letterform for brand recognition.
- **Geometry & Clearspace:**
  - Bounding ratio: $1:1$ square with optical center at $(50, 50)$.
  - Clearspace unit: $X = \text{height} / 4$. Minimum clearspace $= 1.5X$ on all sides.
  - Scale tolerance: Verified legible down to 16px favicon scale.

---

## 5. Physical & Digital Touchpoints
- **Mobile Hardware Application:** iOS Dynamic Island and lockscreen widget with real-time nutrient scanning HUD.
- **Architectural Signage:** Double-sided illuminated exterior blade sign with brickwork texture and night-glow pass.
- **Digital Ecosystem:** Browser tab with favicon, desktop application icon, and component button system (Primary, Secondary, Ghost, Glow).
- **Interactive Studio:** Live stage switcher, scale stress tester, color token clipboard, and SVG vector export.
