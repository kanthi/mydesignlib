Construction Rules

  • 1. Proportion: Aspect ratio must remain fixed at 1:1. Never stretch or horizontally scale.
  • 2. Clearspace: Maintain minimum padding equal to 1.5X where X is the stroke terminal radius.
  • 3. Alignment: When paired with the wordmark, the vertical center of the mark aligns with the cap-height of NutraPulse.
  • 4. Minimum Scale: 16px digital canvas (verified legible as favicon).
