Mark

Two chevrons. One direction.

A wake, not a letter E. Clear space is the height of one chevron. Never spin it as a loader.

Color

Coral on black. Salt on asphalt.

Type

Wordmark

Wakehaul

Sora 600. Always with the wake. Never condensed to fit a door.

Spec

LOT 48 · TEU 2,400 · #E4452F

IBM Plex Mono for manifests and hex.

Voice

Name the lot, then the berth.

Forward

Write as if the ship already left. Dates and TEU, not “end-to-end solutions.”

Plain

Ocean or road. If it is both, say both. Do not say “multimodal magic.”

Held

One coral field. The rest of the sentence is black.

Rules

Do

  • Set the wake in white on coral, or coral on black.
  • Keep aerial plates un-tinted.
  • Use paper only for bills and cards.

Don’t

  • Redraw the wake as a letter E.
  • Put coral type on the sea field.
  • Add a globe, a plane, or a smile.
