# DESIGN — Calanthe

## 1. Brand Essence & Positioning
- **Entity**: Calanthe (`calanthe`)
- **Category**: Studio / Mindful Care & Botanical Technology
- **Origin Reference**: Brand Guidelines structure derived from minimal client brand identity boards (Primary Logo, Logo Scaling, Typography, Color Palette, Logo Usage)
- **Positioning**: *Human care, continuous and present in every direction.*
- **Aesthetic Philosophy**: Warm terracotta minimalism, classical renaissance serif dignity (EB Garamond) paired with ultra-precise geometric floral symmetry ($C_6$ radial balance). Rejection of cold AI abstraction in favor of tactile organic poise.

---

## 2. Geometric Anatomy: The Six-Fold Radial Blossom
- **Rotational Symmetry**: Strict $C_6$ ($60^\circ$ interval) radial symmetry centered on a Cartesian plane $(100, 100)$.
- **Petal Geometry**:
  - Six identical continuous teardrop loops radiating outward with rounded outer apex and concave waist curvature.
  - Symmetrical negative-space channel separating petal flanks.
  - Calibrated outer stroke weight: $9.5\text{px}$ on a $200\text{px}$ canvas, balancing the main stem thickness of the EB Garamond logotype.
- **Center Astroid Star**:
  - A 6-pointed hexagram starburst aperture in the negative-space nucleus, creating a crystalline focal point.
- **Exclusion Clear Space**:
  - Exclusion zone defined as $0.5 \times R$ (half the radius of the mark). No text or external borders may infringe within this zone.

---

## 3. Typographic Voice
- **Primary Logotype**: `EB Garamond`, 500 Medium, lowercase (`calanthe`), optical tracking `-0.02em`. Symmetrical letterforms with warm humanist terminals.
- **Display Headlines**: `Plus Jakarta Sans`, 700 Bold, tracking `-0.02em`.
- **Editorial & Micro-Interface**: `Inter`, 400 Regular / 500 Medium, line-height $1.6$.
- **Telemetry & Tokens**: `JetBrains Mono`, 500 Medium, tracking `+0.05em`.

## 5. The 6 Canonical Presentation Boards
1. **00 // Cover Board**: Terracotta field with oversized clean typography ("Brand Guidelines") and micro-mark lockup in top-right.
2. **01 // Primary Logo**: Master lockup with bracket annotations ("Logomark" / "Logotype") and core brand philosophy statement.
3. **02 // Logo Scaling**: Stepped optical hierarchy showcase ($36\text{px}, 72\text{px}, 114\text{px}, 216\text{px}$) with minimum size rules.
4. **03 // Typography**: Oversized display glyph pair "Aa", metric specs (Size: 150, Line Height: 0, Spacing: -2), and full Latin glyph alphabet.
5. **04 // Primary Color**: Rationale essay, continuous gradient tint ramp, and stacked swatch cards with one-click hex/RGB tokens.
6. **05 // Logo Usage**: Usage guidelines with 4-quadrant application tiles (Peach gradient, Ambient glow aura, Pure paper white, Muted tint).

---

## 6. The 3-Step Logo Stress Test Suite
- **Gaussian Blur Test**: Pass at $15\text{px} - 25\text{px}$ blur radius; 6-lobe perimeter retains distinct silhouette.
- **Grayscale Inversion Test**: 100% flat black on white and white on black legibility without gradient loss.
- **16px Micro-Scale Test**: Verified crisp optical resolution at $16\text{px} \times 16\text{px}$ browser favicon scale.
