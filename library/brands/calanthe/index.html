Design System Engineering

Geometric Anatomy & CAD Blueprints

Mathematical construction of the six-fold radial blossom. Symmetrical 60° rotational coordinates, calibrated negative-space channel, and optical stroke balancing.

Rotational Symmetry C₆ · 60° Intervals

6 identical petal loops revolving around the central coordinate (100, 100), ensuring exact equilibrium and omnidirectional balance.

Radiant Core Star Hexagram Astroid

A delicate 6-pointed starburst aperture centered in the negative space acts as a crystalline anchor, drawing the viewer's eye into the calm center.

Stroke Weight & Optical Mass 9.5px @ 200px Grid

Calibrated stroke weight matches the primary stem width of EB Garamond letterforms, preventing typographic dominance or glyph fragility.

Exclusion Clear Space X-Height = 1/2 Mark Radius

No external text, UI elements, or boundaries may infringe within 0.5R of the logomark perimeter.
