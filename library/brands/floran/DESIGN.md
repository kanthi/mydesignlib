# Floran — Brand Identity Specification

## Identity Core & Vision
**Floran** (`floran`) is a mindful living and biophilic interior architecture brand. Rooted in the Japanese concept of *Ma* (negative space) and organic harmony, Floran crafts living environments, botanical fixtures, and tactile artifacts that dissolve the boundaries between natural ecosystems and domestic life.

> *“Where nature meets home. Stillness, shaped. Made for mindful living.”*

---

## 1. Brand Pillars & Values

- **Calm**: Low-stimulation spaces that soothe the nervous system.
- **Grounded**: Earth-born minerals, raw woods, and tactile ceramics.
- **Intentional**: Every curve tells a story of balance; every texture breathes with purpose.
- **Human**: Spaces shaped around genuine human cadence, not transient trends.
- **Organic**: Biophilic integration with light, flora, and natural ventilation.

---

## 3. Shape Psychology & Geometric Construction

The Floran mark represents a duality of nature and architecture:
- A square perimeter grounding the form in human architectural proportion.
- Two parabolic curved arcs intersecting at the base to form an unfolding botanical sprout and leaf vessel.
- Built on a 4-quadrant radial geometry where every arc aligns with golden ratio proportions.

---

## 4. Typography

- **Brand Header & Display**: `Newsreader`, `serif` (weights 400, 500, italic)
  - Editorial warmth, literary proportion, timeless serif restraint.
- **Interface & Architectural Metadata**: `Plus Jakarta Sans`, `sans-serif` (uppercase, letter-spacing `0.12em`)
  - Crisp modern clarity, rhythmic spacing.
