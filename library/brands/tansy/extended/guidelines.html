Tansy · Flower house

Guidelines

Five rounded petals around a small disc. The mark is not a letter. Use it in one color. The display word is Tansy, set in Nunito. The nav wordmark is Yellowtail, and it is the only script.

Construction

Each petal is the same rounded shape, turned by 72 degrees. A disc covers the meeting point. Do not add a sixth petal, a stem, or a letter inside the bloom.

Clearspace and minimum size

Clearspace on every side equals the height of one petal. Do not set type or another mark inside that margin.

Minimum

18px tall

The symbol does not go under 18px in height. Below that, use the word TANSY alone.

Imagery

The valley photograph sits on the bottom of the hero and is masked into the field so the hill and the flat color read as one surface. It is never a flat gold fill, and it is never recolored. Still lifes are photographs of baskets and stems. No type, marks, or watermarks inside a photograph.

Do and don’t

Do
  • Keep the bloom in one color.
  • Sit the word on the field so the baseline crosses the flowers.
  • Mask the valley photo into the field.
  • Leave one petal of clearspace, and stop at 18px.
  • Use Yellowtail only for the nav name.
Don’t
  • Recolor the flower photo to a flat gold.
  • Set a paragraph in cream on the flat field.
  • Turn the bloom into a letter or add a stem.
  • Stretch the petals or draw a second logo.
  • Put the wordmark on a busy photograph without the field.
