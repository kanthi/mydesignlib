# Zelox — Brand Design System

## Brand Identity Overview
Zelox pairs deep full-stack architecture with kinetic precision. Designed around a centrosymmetric Z-ribbon emblem, electric iris purple, and high-contrast tactile substrates, Zelox represents high-performance custom software engineering and enterprise infrastructure.

- **Brand Name**: Zelox
- **Category**: Services / Custom Software Engineering / Technology
- **Origin Reference**: Fictional rebrand inspired by a software engineering brand case study.

## Visual Language & Geometry

### 1. The Kinetic Z-Ribbon Emblem
The Zelox emblem is a 3-facet continuous geometric ribbon forming a stylized letter "Z" with opposing triangular arrowheads.
- **Topology**: Centrosymmetric ($C_2$ rotational symmetry). The top horizontal facet terminates in a leftward-pointing acute arrowhead, while the bottom facet points rightward, communicating dual directional velocity and continuous data interchange.
- **Facet Construction**: Built on a 45-degree angle shear with consistent stroke rhythm and distinct optical lighting facets (highlighted diagonal bridge vs. saturated terminal arms).
- **Extreme Scale Robustness**: Tested down to 16px favicon scale where the outer silhouette remains unmistakable.

### 2. Chromatic System
- **Iris Purple Primary (`#7367FF`)**: Signature hue radiating technical energy and creative engineering.
- **Iris Light (`#988FFF`)**: Highlight facet color and active badge glow.
- **Deep Obsidian (`#08070F`)**: Foundation backdrop ensuring infinite contrast depth.
- **Card Elevation Base (`#141324`)**: Elevated tactile container tone.
- **Pure White (`#FFFFFF`)**: Crisp foreground typography and high-visibility signals.

### 3. Typographic Hierarchy
- **Display Typography**: `Space Grotesk` (Weights: 600, 700) — Tech-forward, high-contrast monospace-inspired geometric sans.
- **Interface & Body**: `Plus Jakarta Sans` (Weights: 400, 500, 600, 700) — Precision readability for descriptive editorial layouts.
- **Technical Telemetry**: `JetBrains Mono` (Weights: 400, 500, 600) — Token specs, asset dimensions, and engineering IDs.

   - *Tile 03: Debossed Moleskine*: Tactile notebook with blind deboss mark and purple elastic ribbon.
   - *Tile 04: Transit Billboard*: Architectural scale ("We Provide Custom Software And Engineering Services").
   - *Tile 05: Apparel Specimen*: Minimalist embroidered crewneck hoodie patch.
   - *Tile 06: VIP Lanyard Pass*: High-contrast event badge for core engineers.
   - *Tile 07: Fluted Business Card*: Matte purple card on vertical fluted architectural texture.
   - *Tile 08: 3D Monolithic Mark*: Tactile extrusion floating over atmospheric lighting.
   - *Tile 09: Full Design Token Suite*: Explicit hex, role, and usage specs.
3. **3-Step Logo Stress Test**: Optical scaling down to 16px, polarity reversal on black and white substrates, and single-ink monochrome validation.
4. **Vector Asset Distribution Hub**: Downloadable production SVGs for mark, lockup, app icon, and repeating pattern.
