Type in use

Outfit at four weights. Never mix a second display face. Captions and hex stay in IBM Plex Mono.

Wordmark

truss

Lowercase only. Tracking −0.04em. Do not outline, do not add a tagline under the mark.

Clear space

Keep a gap equal to the width of one truss leg around the lockup. Minimum digital size: 16px for the mark, 14px for the word.

Color tokens

Four fields. Buttons are black. Grey is a surface, never a fill for the mark.

Ink#000000
Paper#FFFFFF
Grey#E5E5E5
Charcoal#3A3A3A

Print rules

One color per face. If the field is black, the mark is white. Never grey on charcoal.
