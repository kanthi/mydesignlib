FAQ

Questions, answered

Can’t find what you need? Book a 20-minute call and we’ll walk you through it.

How long does it take to get started?

Most teams are up and running the same day. Import your contacts, connect your inbox and calendar, and switch on a playbook. Guided onboarding is included on every plan.

Can I migrate from my current CRM?

Yes. We offer one-click imports from the most popular CRMs and spreadsheets, and our team will migrate complex setups for free on Growth and Enterprise plans.

What exactly do the AI agents do?

Agents handle repeatable work such as qualifying leads, scheduling meetings, drafting follow-ups and updating records. You decide what they can do on their own and what needs approval.

Is my data secure?

Vendora is SOC 2 Type II compliant, encrypts data in transit and at rest, and never uses your customer data to train shared models. Enterprise plans add SSO, SCIM and data residency.

Can I switch plans later?

Anytime. Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated. Downgrades apply at the end of your current billing period.

Do you offer discounts for startups or nonprofits?

Yes. Eligible startups and registered nonprofits get 50% off Growth for the first year. Reach out to our team to apply.
