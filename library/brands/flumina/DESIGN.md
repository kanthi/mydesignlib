## 3. Mathematical Geometry & 4-Way Cross Mark

The Flumina mark is engineered on a **4-fold rotational symmetry axis ($C_4 / D_4$)**:
1. **Central Hub**: Origin $(50, 50)$ with a punch hole of radius $R = 6.2\text{px}$.
2. **4 Outer Arm Heads**:
   - Located at distance $D = 33\text{px}$ along the cardinal axes ($0^\circ, 90^\circ, 180^\circ, 270^\circ$).
   - Outer circular head radius: $R_{\text{head}} = 12.5\text{px}$.
   - Arm punch hole radius: $R_{\text{hole}} = 6.2\text{px}$.
3. **4 Halo-Nested Corner Satellite Dots**:
   - Located along diagonal axes ($45^\circ, 135^\circ, 225^\circ, 315^\circ$) at distance $D = 23.3\text{px}$ from center.
   - Dot radius: $R_{\text{dot}} = 5.2\text{px}$.
4. **Concentric Fillet Arcs**:
   - The waist between adjacent arm heads curves inward around the corner dots with an exact circular radius $R_{\text{fillet}} = 10.84\text{px}$, maintaining a uniform $5.6\text{px}$ optical halo around each dot.
