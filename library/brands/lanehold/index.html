Brand guidelinesVision

Brand guidelinesMission

We run the truck and the paper from one desk.

A transporter’s yard and a forwarder’s booking share a number. We open a lane, hold it, and close it — the load is not handed between three vendors at the gate.

Your cargo meets a quiet lane.

02 · Mark

Two arcs that couple

The clasp is a hold, not a wheel. Two arcs meet the way a truck meets a booking — they lock, they do not spin.

Clearance

  • Keep a clasp-width of empty field on every side.
  • Lime on ink, ink on lime, ink on paper. Never lime on forest for small sizes.
  • Do not add a fourth petal, a wheel hub, or a road stripe through the gap.
  • Do not outline the mark. Do not drop a shadow.

03 · Color

Lime is type. Forest is a field.

Lime never fills a page. It writes the thesis, paints the ribbon, and sits as the mark. Forest holds the mission. Ink holds the cover.

04 · Type

Condensed theses. Quiet body.

Barlow Condensed carries every all-caps line. IBM Plex Sans explains once, then stops. Tracking on theses is 0.02em — not a shout.

Display · Barlow Condensed 700

The lane holds. The load moves.

Body · IBM Plex Sans 400

A lane is opened, held, and closed from one desk. Measure stays under fifty-two characters. We do not write “seamless logistics.”

05 · Photo

Lanes, yards, canopy

Shoot the hold: wet yards at dusk, empty highways through trees, overhead canopy. No stock smiles. People, if present, are working.

06 · Applications

The clasp rides the lane

Ribbon is decoration, not an icon. Put the lockup on ink, lime, or a veiled photograph. Leave the trailer quiet.
