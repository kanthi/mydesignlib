Usage

Do and don’t

Do

  • Keep each palette inside its own board
  • Build phones, social cards, and Aa specimens in HTML
  • Use the pinwheel, horizon, capsule pair, eye, car, and twin loft as drawn
  • One primary studio CTA: Request the kit

Don’t

  • Cite OVO, Dose, ScreenCheck, Vision Agency, Spaces, or SkyNode
  • Mix mint into Alto or bloom into Sightline
  • Use Inter as a display face or real OS app icons
  • Stretch a mark or outline it in a gradient
