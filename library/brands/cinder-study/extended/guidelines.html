Clearspace

Keep one dot-width of empty field on every side. Minimum symbol size is 16px. The word does not go under 110px wide. The mark stays a single filled circle. It does not grow a ring or a flame.

05 — Use

Do

Keep the single heat dot and set the name in Outfit, uppercase, with open tracking, beside it. Claims use Instrument Serif italic.

Don't

Do not outline the dot, add a flame, or set the lockup in the italic face. Cinder
