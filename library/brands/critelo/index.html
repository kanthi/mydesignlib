Positioning

Critique, not compliments

Critelo is a fictional AI for designers who want structured feedback — not generic praise. Upload a logo, website, brand system, or poster; get principles-backed notes that make the work better.

Honest

Name the problem. Point to the principle. Suggest a next move.

Specific

Hierarchy, type, color, spacing, brand fit — never “make it pop.”

Warm signal

Blue for clarity, coral for energy — serious without being cold.

Usage

Do and don’t

Do

  • Use white mark on blue/coral contour fields
  • Use blue mark on paper backgrounds
  • Keep clear space ≥ half the mark height
  • Run process as Upload → Analyze → Improve → Repeat

Don’t

  • Recolor the mark in gradients or multicolor
  • Use Inter or purple mesh as the brand default
  • Ship vague feedback language (“looks premium”)
  • Copy real product names or client logos
