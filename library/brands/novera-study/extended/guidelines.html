Clearspace

Keep one hexagon of empty field on every side. Minimum symbol size is 16px. The word does not go under 72px wide. The name stays title case in Outfit, weight 600. The hexagon is a stroke, not a filled plate. Stroke width is 1.7 on the 24 view box.
