# Avocet — Brand Identity System & Specification

## 1. Overview & Positioning
- **Brand**: **Avocet** (`avocet`)
- **Category**: `SaaS` / Autonomous Customer Support & Workforce Orchestration
- **Proposition**: *Autonomous resolution pipelines built for high-volume enterprise teams.*
- **Core Visual Metaphor**: The Ascending Wing Peak — precision geometric chevron with counter-balancing diagonal facets and internal negative-space archway. Signals forward velocity, operational clarity, and elevation.
- **Audience**: VP of Support, Customer Experience Directors, Technical Operations leaders, and scaling enterprise founders.
- **Core Archetype**: Deep Tech & Enterprise Infrastructure with human-centered operational calm.

## 3. The Avocet Mark & Geometric Construction

### Geometric Rules
- **Bounding Ratio**: 1:1 square bounding box (100u × 100u).
- **Ascending Apex**: Sharp 60°/45° diagonal angle representing upward trajectory.
- **Counter-Facet**: Sliced planar diagonal separating the left primary pillar from the right dynamic strut.
- **Portal Archway**: An inverted keyhole arch in the negative space providing instant silhouette memorability and balance.

### 4-Tier Responsive Mark Stack
1. **Primary Master Lockup**: Upward Mark + lowercase geometric wordmark `avocet` (horizontal spacing: 0.65em).
2. **Stacked Vertical Lockup**: Centered Mark above `avocet` wordmark (vertical spacing: 0.4em).
3. **Standalone Monogram / App Icon**: Mark centered on Cerulean tile with 22% border radius.
4. **16px Favicon Glyphs**: Optically thickened strokes for uncompromised micro-scale legibility.

---

## 4. Typography Scale & Hierarchy
- **Wordmark**: Custom geometric lowercase `avocet` based on geometric sans with optical kerning.
- **Display & Headings**: `Plus Jakarta Sans` (weights: 600, 700, 800) — clean geometric curves, balanced counters, open apertures.
- **Body & Captions**: `Plus Jakarta Sans` (weights: 400, 500).
- **Technical Blueprint & Specs**: `IBM Plex Mono` (weights: 400, 600) — tabular figures, angle indicators, coordinates.

---

## 5. 3-Step Logo Stress Test Suite
1. **The 16px Favicon Test**: Tested at 16px, 24px, 32px, 48px, 64px — sharp silhouette with zero detail blur.
2. **Monochromatic Inversion Test**: Validated on `#000000` pitch black and `#FFFFFF` stark white.
3. **Substrate Versatility Test**: Validated across paper cardstock, glass acrylic, illuminated 3D signage, and digital screen pixels.
