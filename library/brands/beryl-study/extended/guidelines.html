01 — Logo

Symbol, wordmark, lockup

The symbol is a point-up hex, cut like a beryl crystal: a green face, a dark core, and two highlight facets. The word is Plus Jakarta Sans Bold, tracked tight. Use the horizontal lockup by default. The symbol alone is for small and square spaces. The site replica still uses the green disc; this gem is the identity mark.

02 — Colour

Night, card, one green

The field is near-black green. Cards sit one step lighter. Action green is the lamp: buttons, the live plan, checks, and a glow. It is not a full-bleed paint. Ratios below are computed from the tokens (WCAG 2.2).

03 — Typography

Plus Jakarta, one mono

Plus Jakarta Sans for headlines and UI, weight 700 at display and 400–600 in the body. Geist Mono for captions, plan labels, and the study chrome. Hierarchy comes from size and the green lamp, not from a second display face.

04 — Light

Green is a lamp

One glow per surface, usually from the lower corner, fading into the night field. Do not fill the gem with a gradient. Do not set small type on the hottest part of the lamp.

05 — Imagery

One photograph, then diagrams

The glass orb is the only photograph. It sits on the night field, centered, with no type across the highlight. Charts, the radar, the chat, and the wire globe stay HTML and SVG. The gem is never a photo filter.

06 — Do & don't

Keep the gem intact

The facets stay flat colour. The word stays Plus Jakarta Sans. The green stays the lamp.

✓ Do
Beryl
Gem and word on one line, clear space held, ink on paper.
✕ Don't Do not stretch, rotate, or drop a hard shadow under the gem.
✓ Do Hold the book One green, used as a lamp behind the type, not as a second wordmark.
✕ Don't Beryl Do not reset the word in another face, italic, or outline.
