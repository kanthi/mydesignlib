# Beryl — Visual Identity & Brand System Reference

## Prompt Concept
A practice study of the Beryl night-green coin desk, packaged the way an identity system is packaged: a full-page site replica, a faceted gem mark, brand guidelines, a GSAP landing page, a social kit, merch and stationery, and a logo reveal. Beryl is a dark desk for a small coin book: prices, notes, and a week export.

## Visual Characteristics
- **Color Palette**: Night page (#070807), Card (#101410), Action green (#2BD132), Deep green (#14941A), Type (#F4F6F4), Muted (#9AA39A), Ink (#071107), Line (rgba(43,209,50,.35)).
- **Identity Elements**: Faceted hex gem (the crystal), horizontal and stacked lockups, mono and reverse, app icon. The site replica keeps the green disc wordmark so the page compare stays with the library site. The glass orb is a new photograph in the same role.
- **Typography**: Plus Jakarta Sans (400–700) for display and UI. Geist Mono for labels, captions, and the study chrome.
- **Deliverables Suite**: Site replica, reference compare, brand guidelines, GSAP landing, three square posts and one story, letterhead, envelope, tote, tees, ID badge, email signature, and a logo reveal with a webm.
