Holt · Privacy desk

Guidelines

The mark is a split ring: a circle stroke with a gap at the top. It is not a shield, not a triangle, and not a letter. Set HOLT in Inter, medium weight.

Construction, clearspace, minimum size

The ring is a circle with a gap centered at the top, about 40 degrees. Clearspace on every side equals one stroke width. Do not set type or another mark inside that margin.

Minimum

16px

The symbol does not go under 16px. Below that, use the wordmark alone.

Imagery

The globe and the lock are drawn in SVG. They are not photographs. The three discs beside “150 places” are plain green, blue, and red circles, not flags. Portraits are small, original, and carry no type.

Globe: dark disc, green limb, arcs, dots.

Lock: a padlock inside a soft hexagon with a green glow.

Portraits stay small, in a stack, next to the rating line.

Do and don’t

Do
  • Keep the gap at the top of the ring.
  • Use one color for the ring. Mono is #F4F7F5. Reverse is #080808 on the pale button.
  • Draw the globe and the lock. Do not paste a photograph of either.
  • Use plain color discs, not flags.
  • Leave plans as figures on the page. No payment.
Don’t
  • Redraw the ring as a shield, a triangle, or a letter.
  • Close the gap, or move it to the side.
  • Stretch the ring or fill it with a pattern.
  • Put type inside the clearspace.
  • Set a sentence in ink on the amber line.
