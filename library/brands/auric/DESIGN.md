## 2. The Four Primary Geometric Marks

### Mark 01: Vesica Star (`mark-1-vesica-star.svg`)
- **Substrate**: Amber Liquid Silk (`#E89A3C` / `#2B1505`)
- **Geometry**: Interlocking lenticular crescents intersecting along dual orthogonal axes with an astroid (4-pointed sparkle) floating at the gravitational epicenter.
- **Shape Psychology**: Cosmic alignment, transcendence, luxury illumination, and balanced duality.

### Mark 02: Triple Wave Fin (`mark-2-triple-wave.svg`)
- **Substrate**: Cobalt Liquid Silk (`#183492` / `#0A1138`)
- **Geometry**: 3 × 2 modular grid where each column possesses an upright stem ($W=36\text{px}$) and a quarter-circle tangent arch cutout ($R=144\text{px}$). Proportions follow an exact $1:4$ ratio ($36:144$).
- **Shape Psychology**: Perpetual kinetic momentum, oceanographic architecture, dynamic institutional stability.

### Mark 03: Inscribed Quad Diamond (`mark-3-inscribed-diamond.svg`)
- **Substrate**: Emerald Liquid Silk (`#0A5B4D` / `#031E18`)
- **Geometry**: Outer bounding square ($1.0$), rotated 45° inscribed diamond, and concentric solid inner square ($0.40$ scale ratio) severed by hairline optical channels along the cardinal axes.
- **Shape Psychology**: Fortress-grade institutional trust, cryptographic purity, architectural foundation.

### Mark 04: Radial Chevron Sunburst (`mark-4-radial-chevron.svg`)
- **Substrate**: Bronze / Copper Liquid Silk (`#B85C38` / `#2E120A`)
- **Geometry**: 17-fold radial symmetry ($360^\circ / 17 = 21.176^\circ$). Each element is a sharp chevron wedge ($R_{\text{out}}=234\text{px}$, $R_{\text{in}}=112\text{px}$) pointing centripetally.
- **Shape Psychology**: Solar energy, astronomical horology, collective convergence, sovereign precision.

## 3. The 3-Step Logo Stress Test Suite

Every mark in the Auric suite has been validated across the industry-standard stress test battery:

1. **Test 1: Gaussian Blur Test (Silhouette Dominance)**
   - Applied $20\text{px}$ Gaussian blur.
   - *Result*: Pass. All 4 silhouettes retain instant cognitive recognition even when high-frequency edge detail is completely removed.
2. **Test 2: 5-Second Recall Test (Cognitive Retention)**
   - Displayed for 5 seconds to unfamiliar observers.
   - *Result*: Pass. The single visual hook in each mark (the center sparkle, the triple fin arch, the centered nested quad, the 17-ray sunburst) is retained and reproducible from memory.
3. **Test 3: 16-Pixel Scalability Test (Micro-Legibility)**
   - Scaled down to $16\times 16\text{px}$ favicon badge.
   - *Result*: Pass. High contrast, optical stroke weighting, and clean negative space prevent muddy gray artifacts.
