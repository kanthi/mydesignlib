# Nexvest Brand System — Generative & Reference Prompt

## Prompt Concept
A comprehensive, ultra-modern fintech brand identity system for an algorithmic wealth acceleration and venture portfolio platform named "Nexvest". 

## Visual Characteristics
- **Color Palette**: Radiant Emerald (#00E599), Lime Mint Highlight (#70F284), Bright Mint Glow (#36FFA7), Obsidian Base (#080C0A), Matte Charcoal (#131A16), Crisp White (#FFFFFF).
- **Emblem**: Kinetic "Q/Velocity" arch glyph — a continuous open curved flow ring enclosing three forward-angled parallel momentum vectors at 15°, where the lower vector gracefully acts as the dynamic letterform tail.
- **Aesthetic**: High-performance venture technology meets tactile physical luxury; clean geometric vector rhythm paired with dark obsidian materiality, radiant mint glow accents, and editorial typography (Plus Jakarta Sans + Inter + JetBrains Mono).
- **Mockup Touchpoints**: 6-panel tactile brand application flatlay suite:
  1. Executive digital workflow & tablet checklist telemetry overlay.
  2. Duplex foil business cards resting on an electric lime-mint radiant surface.
  3. Matte black loop-cap insulated commuter flask with radial vector brand wrap.
  4. Fluted architectural walnut/charcoal slat wall with floating matte black dimensional sign.
  5. Executive stationery suite (embossed black portfolio folder, gold-tipped pen, glazed espresso cup).
  6. Heavyweight natural canvas tote bag suspended from an architectural wooden ladder.
