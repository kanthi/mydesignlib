Clearspace

Keep one square of empty field on every side. Minimum symbol size is 16px. The word does not go under 64px wide. The name stays lowercase. The squares stay offset. They do not separate into a row.

02 — Colour

Salmon is for the front square and for large figures. Kickers and links on mint and litchi use juniper. On a deep field the back square switches to mint so it stays visible.

05 — Use

Do

Keep the juniper square behind the salmon square. Set the name in Outfit, medium, lowercase, tight, beside it.

Don't

Do not split the squares, give them a stroke, or set the name in a serif or in title case. Sorrel
