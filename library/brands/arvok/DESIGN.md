# Arvok — Brand Identity System & Specification

**Type:** Brand Identity System  
**Category:** Fintech / Growth Infrastructure  
**Status:** Complete deliverable  
**Reference Origin:** Rebranded gradient-"A" fintech identity board. Characterized by deep-navy-to-electric-blue ascent gradient, geometric apex mark with transit bar and spark, glassmorphic browser mockups, blade signage, billboard and business-card applications.

---

## 1. Brand Overview & Strategic Positioning
- **Brand Name:** **Arvok** (`arvok`)
- **Category:** `Fintech` / Growth Capital Infrastructure
- **Tagline:** *"Built for your next phase of ascent."*
- **Proposition:** *Arvok is growth infrastructure for companies entering their next phase — capital, tooling, and guidance that move at the speed of ambition.*
- **Visual Metaphor:** **The Apex Transit** — an asymmetric peak crossed by a horizontal transit bar: ambition (the climb) intersected by momentum (the line that carries you through).
- **Brand Archetype:** **The Magician × The Ruler** — transformation with institutional composure.

---

## 2. Color System & Semantic Tokens

| Token | Hex Value | Semantic Role | Usage & Guidelines |
|---|---|---|---|
| `--color-navy` | `#060B2E` | Ascent Depth | Primary exhibition field, poster ink, browser-stage backdrop |
| `--color-cobalt` | `#1C4DF1` | Core Momentum | Gradient anchor, primary CTA, business-card face |
| `--color-azure` | `#3E8BFC` | Lift | Gradient mid-stop, hover states, chart fills |
| `--color-cyan` | `#3AE0D8` | Signal Spark | Gradient highlight, success ticks, the apex spark |
| `--color-ice` | `#F3F6FF` | Clean Slate | Card stock, documentation surfaces, reversed type ground |
| `--color-white` | `#FFFFFF` | Stark Contrast | Master mark on gradient and photography |

**Ascent gradient:** `135deg, #1C4DF1 → #060B2E 55% → #3AE0D8 130%` — light always travels lower-left to upper-right.

---

## 3. Typographic Voice & Hierarchy

- **Display & Wordmark:** `Space Grotesk`, 500–700, tight `-0.02em`. Geometric confidence for headlines and the lowercase `arvok` lockup.
- **Editorial Support:** `Instrument Serif` italic for pull-quotes and the tagline when set large.
- **Telemetry & Meta:** `IBM Plex Mono`, uppercase micro-labels (`EST. 2026`, `GROWTH SYSTEM`), tabular numerals.

---

## 4. The 4-Tier Logo Stack

1. **Master Lockup:** Apex mark + lowercase `arvok` wordmark.
2. **Horizontal Nav Lockup:** Compact mark + wordmark at 28px cap-height.
3. **Symbol Glyph:** Standalone apex mark with transit bar and spark.
4. **Adaptive Inversions:**
   - White mark on Ascent Gradient
   - White mark on Deep Navy `#060B2E`
   - Navy mark on Ice `#F3F6FF`
   - Cobalt mark on White

---

## 5. The 3-Step Logo Stress Test Suite

1. **Blur Test:** At 20px Gaussian blur the peak-plus-bar silhouette stays unmistakable.
2. **5-Second Recall:** One hook — a mountain peak with a line speeding through it, plus a spark.
3. **16-Pixel Test:** Thick blades and the rounded bar survive favicon scale; spark merges into the peak.

---

## 6. The 5 Core Identity Touchpoints

1. **Hero Field:** Ascent gradient, white master lockup, corner ticks, `ASCENT / SYSTEM` side labels.
2. **Blade Sign:** Projecting sign on concrete — white face, lit edge.
3. **Billboard:** Night wall, split poster — product render left, headline right.
4. **Browser:** Glassmorphic chrome, tab `arvok`, URL `www.arvok.example`.
5. **Stationery & Poster:** Cobalt business card over ice backer; navy poster — *"Built for your next phase of ascent."*
