FAQ

Questions, answered.

How long does setup take?

Most teams connect their bank and accounting tools and send their first automated invoice the same day. Larger ERP migrations usually take one to two weeks with our onboarding team.

Is MintQX a bank?

No. MintQX is a financial technology company. Funds are held with our regulated banking partners and are protected accordingly.

Which currencies can I pay out in?

Over 60 currencies across 140+ countries, with live FX rates and the option to lock rates for scheduled payouts.

Can I keep my accounting software?

Yes. MintQX syncs both ways with QuickBooks, Xero, NetSuite and more, so your ledger stays the source of truth.

What happens if I cancel?

You can export all your data at any time. Plans are month-to-month unless you choose yearly billing.
