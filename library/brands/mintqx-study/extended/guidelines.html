Clearspace

Keep one mark of empty field on every side. Minimum symbol size is 16px tall. The word does not go under 72px wide. The name stays MintQX in Instrument Sans, weight 500. The left plate is lime. The right plate is cyan. Do not merge them into one shape.

White on night carries small text. Lime and cyan are the mark and large type. Lime on paper does not separate, so the name on a paper field stays night, not lime.
