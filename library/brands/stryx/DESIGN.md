# DESIGN — Stryx Brand Guidelines

## Brand Overview
- **Name**: Stryx
- **Essence**: Sovereign Legal AI — The Sentinel of European Enterprise Law
- **Mission**: Delivering sovereign, uncompromised artificial intelligence for complex legal discovery, compliance orchestration, and contract intelligence.
- **Brand Personality**: Authoritative, watchful, mathematically precise, calm, and impervious.

## 01. Logo Design & Geometry
- **The Sentinel Mark**: An interlocking geometric fusion of an owl's vigilant gaze (Athena's owl of wisdom and nocturnal insight) and an enterprise heraldic defense shield.
- **Geometry & Construction Grid**:
  - Outer boundary: 60-degree heraldic shield taper with curved corner chamfers.
  - Ocular apertures: Dual concentric circular arcs angled at 15 degrees inward.
  - Central septum: Precision vertical line descending from the crest to form the beak/spine.
- **Clear Space**: Minimum clearance of `1.0X` where `X` equals the height of the central ocular ring.
- **Minimum Reproduction**: `18px` digital / `6mm` physical.

## 02. Color Palette & Proportions
- **Primary Sovereign Green (`#132B20`)** — 60% Dominance: Represents institutional permanence, trust, and deep legal roots.
- **Pale Iris Tint (`#D6DCF2`)** — 25% Structural Field: High-clarity soft contrast surface for digital interfaces, document backgrounds, and highlight cards.
- **Sage Leaf Accent (`#4D7555`)** — 10% Focus: Active interface toggles, key metadata, and physical ribbon accents.
- **Obsidian Ink (`#0C1410`)** — 5% Punctuation: Primary headline typography and high-contrast boundaries.
- **Pure White (`#FFFFFF`)** — Base paper and negative space ground.

## 03. Typography Architecture
- **Display & Headlines**: `Outfit` (Weights: 600, 700, 800) — European modernist grotesque with geometric precision and crisp optical cuts.
- **Body & Editorial**: `DM Sans` (Weights: 400, 500, 700) — Neutral, humanistic reading clarity designed for extensive legal documents.
- **Technical & Clauses**: `JetBrains Mono` (Weights: 500) — Precision monospace for compliance telemetry, clause hashes, and sovereign audit logs.

## 04. Applications & Collateral
1. **Architectural & Spatial Signage**: Brushed titanium dimensional logos on deep green acoustic felt; frosted privacy manifestations for legal war rooms.
2. **Stationery & Dossiers**: Heavyweight 360gsm cotton legal presentation folders, blind debossed covers with gold-foil seal, stitched binding.
3. **Apparel & Gear**: Forest green organic heavyweight hoodie with white silicone-embossed chest mark; laser-etched stainless steel badges.
4. **Out-of-Home & Digital**: Airport transit totems and billboard campaigns: *"Europe's Sovereign Legal AI"*.
5. **Digital Product UI**: Sovereign desktop console with real-time clause extraction, encrypted enclave verification, and dark mode interface.
