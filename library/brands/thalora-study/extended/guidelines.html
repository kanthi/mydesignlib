Clearspace

Keep one mark of empty field on every side. Minimum symbol size is 24px. The word does not go under 96px wide. The mint vein stays on the leaf.

Do / don't

Do

Keep the circle open, the leaf translucent, and the vein mint. Use the night tile when the mark sits on a photograph.

Don't

Do not fill the circle solid, drop the vein, or set the name in the display serif on the lockup.
