FAQ

Questions, answered

Can't find what you need? Talk to our team.

Can I import from my current tool?

Yes. One-click importers cover the most common task and project tools, plus CSV for everything else. Comments, assignees and due dates come across.

Is there a free plan?

Starter is free for up to five members with unlimited tasks. Upgrade when you need timelines, reports or automations.

How does per-user pricing work?

You pay for active members only. Guests and viewers are always free, and seats are prorated when people join or leave.

Where is my data stored?

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, with regional hosting options on the Business plan.

Do you offer discounts for non-profits?

Yes, registered non-profits and education teams get 50% off Team and Business.

Can I cancel anytime?

Monthly plans can be cancelled at any time. Yearly plans run to the end of the term and can be exported whenever you like.
