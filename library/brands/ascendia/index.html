Brand Geometry Rationale

The Ascendia mark synthesizes an 8-spoke kinetic sunburst with an upward-pointing arrow formed strictly within negative space. The 5u by 5u proportion integrates seamlessly with the 12u wordmark.

  • Always maintain at least 1u clear-space padding around all sides.
  • Negative arrow must always point exactly upwards (0° zenith).
  • Primary color lockup uses Electric Lime (#9FE814) with Dark Olive (#212E18).
  • Monochrome executions permitted in black on white or white on black.
