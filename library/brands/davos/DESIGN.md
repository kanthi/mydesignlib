## 2. The 3-Slide Master Case Study Architecture

The deliverable provides full-screen and side-by-side presentation views of all 3 slides matching the reference case study:

### Slide 01: The Designer's Exploration Desk (Ideation & Divergence)
- Expansive isometric drafting table layout with 20+ graphite ideation marks investigating spatial topology, interlocking ribbon loops, ascending growth data charts, and dynamic monogram letterforms.
- **4 Shortlisted Directions** staged on raised tactile white squircles with calibrated soft drop shadows:
  1. **Concept A (Rhombus S-Link)**: Interlocking 2.5D loop representing continuous data pipelines.
  2. **Concept B (Speed D Monogram)**: Kinetic forward-leaning letterform with faceted speed cuts.
  3. **Concept C (Spatial Cube)**: Isometric data slab with a hovering isometric compute block.
  4. **Concept D (Ascending Wave Arch — Selected)**: Monumental outer arch wave embracing 3 ascending growth steps.

### Slide 02: Systematic Stylistic & Variant Matrix (4×5)
A 4×5 iterative matrix analyzing each candidate direction across 5 distinct stylistic execution models:
1. **Monoline Wireframe**: High-contrast precision line stroke testing structural clarity at small sizes.
2. **Dimensional Extrusion**: Isometric 3D faceted volume and perspective shading.
3. **Translucent Glassmorphism**: Soft frosted acrylic transparency with optical refraction.
4. **Tonal Slate Gradient**: Controlled monochromatic value steps defining functional elevation.
5. **App Squircle Execution**: Production-ready tactile icon with calibrated drop shadow.

### Slide 03: Geometric Construction & Ratio Blueprint
The final selected mark (Concept D) engineered on a $6 \times 6$ Cartesian coordinate grid:
1. **Concentric Primary Arcs**: Outer circle $R_{\text{outer}} = 2.0x$ and inner circle $R_{\text{inner}} = 1.0x$ centered at $(3, 3)$.
2. **Tangent Wave Crest Bulb**: Tangent end cap circle of radius $R = 0.5x$ centered at $(1.5, 2.5)$.
3. **3-Tier Ascending Growth Steps**: 3 smooth ascending wave petals ($y \in [4.2, 5.0]$, $y \in [3.6, 5.0]$, $y \in [3.0, 5.0]$) in calibrated slate tones.
4. **Lockup Clearspace & Dimension Rules**:
   - **Horizontal Lockup**: Mark height $2x$, Wordmark height $x$, Gap $0.4x$, Margin padding $x$.
   - **Vertical Lockup**: Mark height $2.5x$, Wordmark height $x$, Gap $0.6x$, Margin padding $x$.
