Clearspace

Keep one disc of empty field on every side. Minimum symbol size is 16px. The word does not go under 72px wide. The name stays uppercase. The chevron is a stroke, not a filled triangle.

Blue on white passes for small type. Blue on ink is for large figures only. On night, the disc flips to white so the chevron still reads.

05 — Use

Do

Keep the disc and the open chevron. Set the name in Geist, bold, uppercase, tight, beside it.

Don't

Do not fill the chevron, turn it into a letter, or set the name in a serif. Halyard
