# Omnia — Visual Identity & Brand System Reference

## Prompt Concept
A comprehensive, multi-deliverable visual identity system study and extended brand universe for Omnia, an autonomous AI execution layer. Encompassing a 6-panel vector identity board, gold-amber starburst ribbon symbol, full brand guidelines manual, interactive GSAP landing page, multi-format social media kit, tactile merchandise flatlays, and cinematic logo reveal.

## Visual Characteristics
- **Color Palette**: Obsidian Graphite (#0D0D0D), Radiant Amber (#F0B400), Goldenrod (#D38F12), Warm Parchment Cream (#ECE6DA), Bone Paper (#F7F4EE).
- **Identity Elements**: Intricate 8-point harmonic starburst ribbon geometry, typographic lockups, luxury woven beanie flatlay specimen, app icon token suite.
- **Typography**: Inter (Display & Sans), Geist Mono (Telemetry & Grid).
- **Deliverables Suite**: Identity board replica, comprehensive brand standards manual, high-performance GSAP scroll-driven product landing page, social media asset templates, apparel and stationery mockups, and programmatic logo animation reveal.
