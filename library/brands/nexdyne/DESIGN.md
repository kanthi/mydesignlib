## 2. Geometric Blueprint & The Kinetic Arc Mark
- **Concept**: The Nexdyne mark is an engineered synthesis of a forward propulsion vector and a cybernetic joint axis. It balances monolithic stability with rotational velocity.
- **Mathematical Specification**:
  - **Outer Trajectory Arc**: Semi-circular circular trajectory sweeping from coordinate `(38, 62)` through radius $R_1 = 98\text{px}$ to terminal coordinate `(198, 195)`.
  - **Inner Fillet Elbow**: Precision structural stem starting horizontally at `(38, 128)`, curving across a $r_2 = 24\text{px}$ fillet to `(120, 152)`, and grounding vertically at `(120, 195)`.
  - **Stroke Weight**: Calibrated $26\text{px}$ constant vector caliper with optically balanced semicircular round terminals (`stroke-linecap: round`).
  - **Clearspace Unit ($X$)**: The internal clearance between the inner elbow and outer arc is designated as $1X$. Minimum clearspace perimeter around the mark is $1.5X$.
  - **Scale Thresholds**: Verified down to $16\text{px} \times 16\text{px}$ micro-favicon resolution with zero stroke collision.

## 4. Typographic System
- **Display & Logotype**: `Plus Jakarta Sans` / `Space Grotesk` (700 Bold / 800 ExtraBold). Modern neo-grotesque with geometric ink-traps, wide apertures, and optical tracking (`-0.03em`).
- **Technical Telemetry & CAD Calipers**: `JetBrains Mono` (500 Medium / 600 SemiBold). Precision monospaced type for coordinates, dimension units, and hardware specifications.
- **Body & Documentation**: `Inter` / `Plus Jakarta Sans` (400 Regular / 500 Medium). High legibility, neutral voice, optimized for dense technical documentation.

## 5. 4-Tier Responsive Mark Stack
1. **Primary Master Lockup**: Concentric kinetic arc mark positioned left of the 700-weight geometric wordmark `Nexdyne`.
2. **Horizontal Header Lockup**: Reduced height ratio ($32\text{px}$) with proportional letter tracking for persistent system navigation bars.
3. **Standalone Monogram Glyph**: The isolated kinetic arc housed in an adaptive squircle ($48\text{px}$ to $512\text{px}$) for application icons and social avatars.
4. **Adaptive Monochrome & Inversion**: High-contrast white glyph for `#060510` obsidian dark mode; solid pitch black glyph for `#FBFBFD` technical paper printouts.
