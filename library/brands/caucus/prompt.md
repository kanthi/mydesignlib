# Caucus — visual identity

## Prompt concept
A visual identity for Caucus, a private mediation room for claims under a million. One sitting, one table, signed heads of terms before anybody leaves. The mark is two facing bowls with an open gap. The package is a six-card board plus the guidelines, landing page, social kit, merch, email signature, and logo reveal in that same system.

## Visual characteristics
- **Palette**: Tyrian `#66023C`, Citron `#CAD1B3`, Mulberry `#8E2A54`, Pith `#EFEEDA`, Ink `#2B0119`, white `#FFFFFF`, board ground `#000000`.
- **Hero wash**: citron glow upper right (`#E7E6B4`), mulberry lower right (`#9A3A62`), tyrian linear field (`#4C0628` → `#72143F` → `#5A1034` → `#320418`).
- **Mark**: two facing bowls, open gap, `currentColor`, `fill-rule: evenodd`. Not a cut square.
- **Wordmark**: CAUCUS. Libre Franklin 700, uppercase, tracking −3%.
- **Domain**: caucus.law. Mail: desk@caucus.example (mailto only).
- **Type**: Libre Franklin only. Headlines at 500. No serif.

## Deliverables
- Hub (`index.html`) — replica group and extended group
- Replica six-card board, theme switcher included
- Brand guidelines — clearspace, 18 px minimum, ramps, contrast, type, imagery, do and don’t
- Website landing — GSAP and ScrollTrigger, reduced motion, 320 to 1600
- Social kit — three posts at 1080 × 1080 and one story at 1080 × 1920
- Merch and stationery — letterhead, envelope, tote, two tees, ID badge
- Email signature
- Logo reveal — bowls from opposite sides, then the wordmark, replay
