The mark

Two bowls, taken from the same circle and set face to face. Neither stands in for the other. The gap is constant and it never closes. Nothing is outlined.

CAUCUS

Horizontal lockup. Bowls at 1.15 em, gap 0.42 em. Wordmark Bold, uppercase, −3%.

CAUCUS

Stacked, reversed out of Tyrian.

Clearspace and minimum size

Clearspace is three tenths of the mark on every side. Do not set the bowls under 18 px. Below that the gap collapses. At 18 px the bowls stay heavy and the gap holds.

Colour

Two colours, because a dispute has two sides. Tyrian is the ground. Citron is the signal. It marks the one open question — the date — and is not a layout fill. Mulberry carries secondary type on light. Pith is the paper. Ink is the only dark. Mixes belong on this chart, not in a layout.

Imagery

The field is a wash, not a photograph. Tyrian holds the ground. A citron glow sits upper right. Mulberry sits lower right. Do not place a picture under the wordmark, and do not add a texture the wash does not already have. People are not shown. The room is the type and the two bowls.

Do and don’t

Do

  • Keep the bowls facing and the gap open.
  • Hold citron for the date control. Repeat that control, not a second use.
  • Set CAUCUS in Libre Franklin Bold, uppercase, tracked −3%.
  • Keep clearspace at 0.3 × the mark, and stay at or above 18 px.

Don’t

  • Outline the bowls, close the gap, or rotate them.
  • Add a third colour in a layout, or tint a card.
  • Set the word in a serif, or in sentence case.
  • Put a photograph, a texture, or a drop shadow under the mark.
