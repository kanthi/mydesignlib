# Removed templates — below the quality bar

Culled **2026-08-16**. Owner judgment: these read as **AI-made with poor choices**. They are not a taste reference and must not be used as quality-bar examples.

This is a working log. More items will be appended as they are culled.

## Why they failed

They look generated, not designed. Typical tells (most items hit several):

- Safe SaaS / agency template: centered hero, dual CTAs, three equal cards, pill nav, generic dashboard mock
- Default AI type kit: **Inter** as the display or body face (almost every item below)
- Soft purple / teal / lime accent on cream or near-black with no subject vernacular
- Invented `-ora` / `-well` / `-lab` names stacked on interchangeable layouts
- Copy that could sit on any product (`streamline`, `seamless`, `smarter decisions`, `feel heavenly`)
- Finish that stops at “looks like a landing page” instead of a committed aesthetic

Earlier ship notes treated **Respondra**, **Ovara**, **Nordwerk**, then **Quietly** / **Fieldnote**, as good examples. That bar was too low. Keep **Noirline**, **Claybank**, and **Claret** as the working standard — until those get reviewed too.

## How to cull more

1. Append a dated section (or rows in the table) with **id**, **name**, and **one sentence** on the poor choice.
2. Delete `library/websites/<id>/` (or brands/logos/systems).
3. Drop the object from `data/catalog.json`.
4. Strip the id from any `related[]` and from quality-bar examples in `AGENTS.md` / ship-template.
5. Confirm JSON still parses and the showcase loads.

Do not keep a culled folder “for reference.” The note is the reference.
